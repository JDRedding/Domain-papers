# **Saturnine Dreadnought**

## **Origins & development**

- Created by the **tech‑enclaves of Saturn**, specifically Phoebe, alongside Saturnine Terminator armour.  
- The original template was **too complex** for mass production until **Vulkan** refined it.  
- Vulkan then **shared the pattern with all Legions**, meaning both Loyalists and Traitors fielded them during the Heresy. 

## **Core identity**
: What the Saturnine Dreadnought actually is

The Saturnine Dreadnought is a **Great Crusade–era walker chassis** derived directly from **Saturnine Terminator Armour technology**, not from the Contemptor/Leviathan lineage. It is **larger, tougher, and more heavily armed** than a Leviathan, designed for **mid‑range dominance** rather than melee.  

It requires pilots with **exceptional mental discipline**, and many sources repeat the rumour that **latent psychic ability** helps interface with its generators and shielding.  

## **Historical & narrative significance**
- Used by multiple Legions due to Vulkan’s dissemination.  
- Appeared in major battles such as **Isstvan V / Drop Site Massacre**.  
- Notable pilots include:  
  - **Seperoth, Mountain of Fire** (Salamanders)  
  - **Ahkenoptek, Ruinmaker** (Thousand Sons)  
  - **Gulgorath, Avatar of Scorn** (Iron Warriors)  

## **Physical characteristics**

The Saturnine Dreadnought is described as:

- A **rare, elite, Saturn‑derived walker**  
- **Larger and tougher** than Leviathans  
- **Mid‑range firepower specialist**  
- **Psychically‑attuned pilot requirement (rumoured)**  
- **Historically significant** across multiple Legions  
- **High‑cost centrepiece unit** in Horus Heresy gameplay

### **Size & durability**

- **Taller and bulkier** than a Leviathan
- **Overlapping ceramite + adamantium plating**
- **Thermal Diffraction Field**: reduces Strength of **Las, Plasma, Melta, Flame** weapons
- **4+ invulnerable save**  

### **Generators & shielding**

The enlarged Saturnine pauldrons house **shield generators** and sometimes **teleportation synchronisers**.  

## **Armament (verified)**
### **Chiron configuration**
- **Heavy Plasma Bombard** (indirect fire, 5" blast)
- **Twin Disintegration Cannons** (S9, D3 shots, Overload)
- **Two Photonic Incinerators** (template, short‑range point defense)

### **Ophion configuration**
- **Inversion Beamer** (conversion‑type, stronger at close range).  
- **Graviton Pulveriser** (suppression + immobilization).  
- **Heavy Particle Shredders** or **Concussive Resonators** for point defense. 

## **Tabletop profile**
(Horus Heresy 3rd Edition)

From Warhammer Community’s rules:

- **M6, WS4, BS4, S8, T8, W8, I3, A2**  
- **Save 2+ / Invulnerable 4+**  
- **Bulky (8), Walker (Heavy)**  
- **Thermal Diffraction Field** (damage reduction vs key anti‑tank traits)  
- **Base cost ~340 points**

## **Role in gameplay**
The Saturnine is **not a melee brawler** (Leviathan still wins there). It is a **mid‑range fire support anchor**:

- Controls zones with plasma barrages.  
- Deletes vehicles with disintegrators.  
- Suppresses/immobilizes with graviton weapons.  
- Survives incoming anti‑tank fire better than any other Dreadnought due to T8, W8, and the diffraction field.
