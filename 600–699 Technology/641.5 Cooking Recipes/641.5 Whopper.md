# The Whopper
600–699 Technology/641.5 Cooking Recipes/

The Whopper is Burger King’s iconic flame-grilled beef burger, known for its distinctive taste, size, and classic toppings. The Whopper is Burger King’s flagship flame-grilled burger, celebrated for its distinctive taste, sizable portion, and flexibility in customization, making it a staple option for both traditional and plant-based burger lovers.

The Whopper is considered an iconic fast-food burger and has been featured in numerous marketing campaigns, promotions, and limited-time releases around the world. Its flame-grilled flavor and customizable toppings contribute to its enduring popularity among fast-food consumers.

## Overview and History

The Whopper was first introduced in 1957 by Burger King as its signature menu item. It was created to compete with larger burgers offered at other fast-food chains and has since become a staple for the brand. The Whopper is recognized for its flame-grilled patty, which gives it a characteristic smoky flavor compared to pan-cooked or fried burgers.

## Size and Nutrition

A standard Whopper is larger than the typical fast-food burger and is often considered a full meal option when paired with fries or a drink. Nutritional information for a classic Whopper includes approximately:

- **Calories:** 657 kcal  
- **Protein:** 28 g  
- **Fat:** 40 g (12 g saturated fat)  
- **Carbohydrates:** 49 g  
- **Sodium:** 980 mg  

These values may vary slightly by location, bun type, or additional toppings. The Impossible Whopper provides a plant-based alternative with similar taste and slightly fewer calories and fat while supporting vegetarian and vegan diets.

## Ingredients

The classic Whopper typically includes:

- Beef patty (flame-grilled, usually 1/4 lb)  
- Sesame seed bun  
- Mayonnaise  
- Lettuce  
- Tomato slices  
- Pickles  
- Ketchup  
- Sliced onions  

Optional additions include bacon, cheese, or custom sauces depending on promotions or regional variations. The Whopper is also available in variants, such as the Double Whopper (two beef patties), Impossible Whopper (plant-based patty), and limited-time specialty versions with extra toppings or sauces.

### Composition
**Makes:** 1 hamburger

- 1 sesame-seed hamburger bun  
- 1/4 pound ground beef  
- Dash salt  
- 3 dill pickle slices  
- 1 teaspoon catsup  
- 3 to 4 onion rings  
- 2 tomato slices  
- 1/4 cup chopped lettuce  
- 1 tablespoon mayonnaise  

### Instructions

1. Preheat a barbecue grill on high.  
2. Toast both halves of the bun, face down, in a hot skillet. Set aside.  
3. Form the beef into a thin patty slightly larger than the bun.  
4. Lightly salt the hamburger patty and cook on the barbecue grill for 2 to 3 minutes per side.  
5. Build the burger in the following stacking order from the bottom up:  
   - bottom bun  
   - hamburger patty  
   - pickles  
   - catsup  
   - onion rings  
   - tomatoes  
   - lettuce  
   - mayonnaise  
   - top bun
