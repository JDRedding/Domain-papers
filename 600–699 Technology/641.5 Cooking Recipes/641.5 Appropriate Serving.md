# Appropriate Serving
600–699 Technology/641.5 Cooking Recipes

***Vegetables & Sauces to Serve with Fish & Meats****

| Dish | Suggested vegetables, sauces, and accompaniments |
|------|--------------------------------------------------|
| **Fish, boiled** | Boiled potatoes, tomatoes, horseradish, melted butter, sliced lemon, tomato or tartar sauce |
| **Fish, fried** | Potatoes, cucumbers, tomatoes, lemon, sauce tartare |
| **Fish, baked** | Mashed potatoes, peas, corn, lettuce salad, drawn butter or Holland sauce |
| **Beef, roasted** | Sweet potatoes, tomatoes, beets, string beans, squash, macaroni, mustard, horseradish |
| **Beef, boiled or corned** | Fried or Lyonnaise potatoes, turnips, parsnips, cabbage, horseradish sauce |
| **Beefsteak** | Fried or Saratoga potatoes, baked tomatoes, peas, corn, parsnips, squash |
| **Veal, boiled** | Mashed potatoes, spinach, macaroni and cheese, horseradish |
| **Veal, roasted** | Mashed potatoes, asparagus, sweet potatoes, parsnips, tomatoes with rice |
| **Veal steak** | Potatoes, sweet potatoes, carrots and peas, spinach, tomatoes |
| **Pork** | Potatoes, sweet potatoes, onions, tomatoes, apple sauce, baked apples |
| **Mutton or lamb, boiled or roasted** | Mashed potatoes, asparagus, cauliflower, peas, turnips, currant jelly, Jerusalem artichokes, mint or caper sauce |
| **Mutton or lamb chops** | Boiled potatoes, fried sweet potatoes, salsify, pickled beets, tomato sauce |
| **Turkey** | Potatoes, sweet potatoes, squash, onions, parsnips, string beans, oyster plant, acid jelly, oyster or cranberry sauce |
| **Chicken, boiled** | Potatoes, boiled rice, tomatoes, turnips, acid jelly, celery or oyster sauce |
| **Chicken, roasted** | Baked or browned potatoes, succotash, onions, beets, squash, acid jelly |
| **Duck or goose, roasted** | Potatoes, onions, corn, macaroni, rice croquettes, apple sauce |
| **Game** | Potatoes, salad, spinach, tomatoes, acid jelly |

In addition to the above, relishes may be served, as celery, radishes, olives, and pickles, also cold slaw or a salad.
