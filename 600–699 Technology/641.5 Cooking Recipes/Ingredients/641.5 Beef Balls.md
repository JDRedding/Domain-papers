# Beef Balls
600–699 Technology/641.5 Cooking Recipes

Beef balls are a savory dish made from chopped beef, eggs, and bread, mixed with seasoning, then shaped and cooked for a tender and flavorful result. Beef balls can be served on their own with a dipping sauce, added to soups, stews, or pasta dishes, or used as an appetizer with toothpicks. Garnish with fresh herbs if desired.

## Ingredients

1/2 pound raw chopped beef  
2 egg yolks  
1 cup bread, soaked and squeezed dry  
1 heaping tablespoonful creamed butter  
A little salt  
A trace of nutmeg  
White of 1 egg, beaten stiff  

## Directions

Mix well the chopped beef, egg yolks, soaked bread, creamed butter, salt, and nutmeg.  
Work in the beaten egg white.

1. **Prepare the mixture:** In a mixing bowl, combine the chopped beef, egg yolks, soaked and squeezed bread, creamed butter, salt, and nutmeg. Mix thoroughly until well combined.  
2. **Incorporate the egg white:** Gently fold in the beaten egg white. This lightens the mixture and helps create a tender texture.  
3. **Shape the balls:** Using your hands or a small spoon, form the mixture into uniform balls, about 1 to 1.5 inches in diameter. Try to make them smooth and compact without overhandling.

### Cooking Options

- **Boiling:** Bring a pot of lightly salted water or broth to a boil. Reduce the heat to a gentle simmer and cook the beef balls for 8–10 minutes until firm and cooked through.  
- **Baking:** Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Place the beef balls on a greased baking sheet and bake for 15–20 minutes, turning once halfway through, until browned and cooked through.  
- **Pan-frying:** Heat a little oil or butter in a skillet over medium heat. Fry the beef balls, turning occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through.

## Tips for Best Results

- Ensure the bread is well soaked but thoroughly squeezed to remove excess moisture; this prevents the mixture from being too wet.  
- Do not overmix the egg white into the beef; fold gently to retain the airy texture.  
- Adjust seasoning according to taste; adding garlic, onion, or parsley can enhance flavor.

This method yields tender, flavorful beef balls with a delicate texture suitable for a variety of dishes.
