# Apple Sauce
600–699 Technology/641.5 Cooking Recipes

Apple sauce is a versatile, sweetened or unsweetened semi-cooked fruit puree made primarily from apples, often used as a snack, dessert, or ingredient in cooking and baking. Apple sauce is a simple, nutritious, and versatile purée that can be enjoyed on its own, used in recipes to enhance flavor and texture, or incorporated as a healthy ingredient in both sweet and savory dishes. Apple sauce can be stored in the refrigerator for up to a week or frozen for several months for longer preservation. Good with roast pork or goose.

## What Is Apple Sauce?

Apple sauce is made by cooking peeled, cored, and chopped apples until they break down into a soft, smooth, or chunky puree. It can be sweetened with sugar or left naturally sweet, and additional flavorings such as cinnamon, nutmeg, or lemon juice are often added to enhance taste. The texture can range from completely smooth to slightly chunky depending on personal preference or recipe requirements.

## Nutritional Benefits

Apple sauce retains many of the vitamins and antioxidants found in fresh apples, such as vitamin C and dietary fiber (particularly if the peel is included). Depending on added sugar, it can be low in calories and fat, making it a healthier alternative to many packaged desserts. It also aids digestion due to its fiber content and can contribute to hydration because of its high water content.

## Culinary Uses

- **Eating straight:** Served as a snack or dessert.  
- **Baking substitute:** Often replaces oil or butter in recipes like muffins, cakes, and brownies, reducing fat content while adding natural sweetness.  
- **Savory dishes:** Pairs well with pork, chicken, or roasted vegetables as a condiment.  
- **Baby food:** Unsweetened apple sauce is commonly used as a nutritious and easily digestible food for infants.  

## How to Make Apple Sauce

1. **Choose apples:** Sweet varieties like Fuji, Gala, or Honeycrisp produce a naturally sweet sauce, while tart apples like Granny Smith add acidity.  
2. **Prepare apples:** Peel, core, and chop apples into even pieces.  
3. **Cook:** Place apples in a saucepan with a small amount of water, and cook over medium heat until tender (about 15–20 minutes). Optional sugar or spices can be added during cooking.  
4. **Mash or blend:** For smooth sauce, use a blender, food processor, or immersion blender. For chunky sauce, lightly mash with a fork.

### Ingredients

- 6 medium-sized tart apples  
- Water, enough to cover  
- 1 teaspoonful butter  
- Sugar, to taste  
- Powdered cinnamon, for dusting  

### Directions

1. Peel, quarter, and core the apples.  
2. Put at once in a graniteware saucepan with water enough to cover.  
3. Boil until tender.  
4. Rub through a sieve.  
5. Stir in the butter and sugar to taste.  
6. When dished, dust a little powdered cinnamon on top.
