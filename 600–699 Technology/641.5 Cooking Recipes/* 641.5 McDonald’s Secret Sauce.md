# McDonald’s Secret Sauce
600–699 Technology/641.5 Cooking Recipes/

McDonald’s Secret Sauce is the distinctive dressing used on Big Macs, known for its creamy, tangy, and slightly sweet flavor profile. McDonald’s Secret Sauce is a blend that combines mayonnaise, sweet pickle relish, mustard, vinegar, garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika. Its tangy and slightly sweet taste is designed to complement the beef, cheese, lettuce, and sandwich buns of the Big Mac. The sauce is sometimes compared to a classic Thousand Island dressing but has a unique flavor that has become iconic to McDonald’s branding. McDonald’s Secret Sauce remains one of the most recognizable fast food sauces, celebrated for its unique and nostalgic taste.

## Ingredients and Flavor Profile

While the official McDonald’s formulation is proprietary, popular copycat recipes approximate it with the following components:

- **Mayonnaise:** Provides the creamy base.  
- **Sweet pickle relish:** Adds sweetness and texture.  
- **Yellow mustard:** Adds tang and slight spiciness.  
- **White vinegar:** Contributes acidity to balance flavor.  
- **Garlic and onion powders:** Provide savory undertones.  
- **Paprika:** Adds mild color and depth.  

The combination creates a creamy, tangy, slightly sweet, and savory sauce that elevates the Big Mac’s layered flavors.

## Usage

The Secret Sauce is specifically used on the Big Mac, spread over both the top and bottom buns to ensure every bite includes the signature taste. Its popularity has led McDonald’s to occasionally market it for sale in bottles at select locations or through limited-time promotions.

## DIY Tip

For those interested in making it at home, mixing the above ingredients in proportion (roughly 1/2 cup mayonnaise, 2 tablespoons sweet pickle relish, 1 tablespoon mustard, 1 teaspoon vinegar, and a pinch of garlic and onion powder) closely mimics the classic flavor. Adjust sweet or tang elements to personal preference.

**Makes:** about 3/4 cup

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise  
- 2 tablespoons French dressing  
- 4 teaspoons sweet pickle relish  
- 1 tablespoon finely minced white onion  
- 1 teaspoon white vinegar  
- 1 teaspoon sugar  
- 1/8 teaspoon salt  

## Instructions

1. Combine all of the ingredients in a small bowl.  
2. Stir well.  
3. Place the sauce in a covered container.  
4. Refrigerate for several hours, or overnight, so that the flavors blend.  
5. Stir the sauce a couple of times as it chills.
