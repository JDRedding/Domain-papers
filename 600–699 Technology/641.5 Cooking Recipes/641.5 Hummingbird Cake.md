# Hummingbird Cake
600–699 Technology/641.5 Cooking Recipes/

Hummingbird cake is a moist, sweet, spiced cake with bananas, pineapple, and often pecans, topped traditionally with cream cheese frosting.

## Description and Ingredients

Hummingbird cake is a Southern dessert known for its rich flavor and moist texture. The main ingredients typically include:

- **Ripe bananas** — mashed to provide natural sweetness and moisture  
- **Pineapple** — crushed pineapple adds tropical flavor and additional moisture  
- **Vegetable oil** — keeps the cake tender and moist  
- **Sugar** — sweetens the batter, often a combination of granulated and brown sugar  
- **Eggs** — for structure and richness  
- **Flour** — usually all-purpose  
- **Spices** — particularly cinnamon; some recipes add nutmeg or allspice for depth  
- **Optional nuts** — chopped pecans or walnuts for texture  
- **Vanilla extract** — enhances overall flavor  

The cake is almost always topped with a cream cheese frosting, which complements the sweetness and adds a slight tang. Some variations also include shredded coconut or a layer of pineapple in the frosting.

## History

Hummingbird cake originated in the Southern United States, particularly noted in Kentucky. Its name is thought to come either from the cake’s sweetness, which might “attract hummingbirds,” or its popularity in the southern states. The recipe gained wider attention after it appeared in early Southern cookbooks and major magazines in the 1970s.

## Tips for Baking

- Use fully ripe bananas to maximize sweetness and flavor.  
- Drain pineapple well if the liquid is too much, to prevent a soggy cake.  
- Layering is optional; it can be baked as a sheet cake or in multiple layers for a tiered cake.  
- Add nuts or coconut according to preference for texture and flavor.  
- Chill before slicing if using cream cheese frosting, as it helps the frosting set.  

Hummingbird cake is frequently served for special occasions like birthdays, holidays, or gatherings because of its rich flavor and visually appealing layers. Its combination of bananas, pineapple, spices, and cream cheese frosting makes it a unique and beloved dessert across the Southern U.S. This easy hummingbird cake is a moist, flavorful dessert made with bananas, pineapple, and pecans, topped with classic cream cheese frosting.

### Ingredients

#### For the Cake

- 2 cups all-purpose flour  
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda  
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon  
- 1/4 teaspoon salt  
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar  
- 3 large eggs  
- 1 cup vegetable oil  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract  
- 1 1/2 cups mashed ripe bananas (about 3 medium bananas)  
- 8 ounces crushed pineapple, drained  
- 1 cup chopped pecans (optional)  

#### For the Cream Cheese Frosting

- 8 ounces cream cheese, softened  
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened  
- 3–4 cups powdered sugar, sifted  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract  
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans for garnish (optional)  

### Instructions

1. **Preheat the oven** to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.  
2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.  
3. **Prepare the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, beat the eggs and sugar until fluffy. Add the oil and vanilla and mix well.  
4. **Combine bananas and pineapple:** Stir the mashed bananas and drained pineapple into the wet mixture until evenly distributed.  
5. **Combine wet and dry ingredients:** Gradually fold in the dry ingredients until just combined. Be careful not to overmix.  
6. **Add nuts:** Fold in the chopped pecans, if using.  
7. **Bake:** Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans. Bake for 25–30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.  
8. **Cool:** Allow the cakes to cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn them out onto a wire rack to cool completely.  
9. **Prepare the frosting:** Beat the cream cheese and butter together until smooth. Gradually add the powdered sugar and vanilla extract until the frosting reaches your desired sweetness and consistency.  
10. **Assemble the cake:** Place one cake layer on a serving plate. Spread a layer of frosting on top, then place the second cake layer on top. Frost the top and sides of the cake. Sprinkle with chopped pecans if desired.  
11. **Serve:** Refrigerate for about 30 minutes before slicing for easier serving.

Enjoy a moist, tropical-flavored cake that’s perfect for any celebration. This recipe keeps the classic flavors while simplifying preparation, making it accessible even for beginner bakers.
