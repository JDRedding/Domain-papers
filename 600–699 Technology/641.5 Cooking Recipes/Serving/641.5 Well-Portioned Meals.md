# Cooking Well-Portioned Meals

Cooking delicious meals starts with balanced recipes, clear steps, and sensible serving sizes for each dish. Following these principles helps keep meals nutritious, well-portioned, and visually appealing, so cooking stays enjoyable and serving stays simple for any occasion.

- **Balance portions:** Fill half the plate with vegetables, one-quarter with protein, and one-quarter with grains or starches.
- **Consider appetite:** Adjust portion sizes based on age, activity level, and meal timing.
- **Presentation matters:** Use colorful vegetables, fresh herbs, and garnishes to make the plate more appealing.
- **Accompaniments:** Bread, sauces, or dips can complement a meal—just watch portion sizes so the plate stays balanced.

## Popular Recipe Categories

### 1. Breakfast

Easy, nutritious options include:

**Scrambled eggs with vegetables**  
Whisk eggs with milk or cream, sauté spinach, tomatoes, or peppers, then cook until soft. Serve with toast or avocado slices.  
Typical serving: 2 eggs per person.

**Overnight oats**  
Combine rolled oats, milk or yogurt, chia seeds, and fruit in a jar. Refrigerate overnight.  
Serving: about ½–¾ cup per person.

**Smoothie bowls**  
Blend frozen fruits with yogurt or milk, pour into a bowl, and top with granola, nuts, or seeds.  
One bowl serves one person.

### 2. Lunch

Filling, balanced meals include:

**Chicken or tofu salad**  
Combine protein, leafy greens, avocado, tomatoes, cucumber, and a light vinaigrette.  
Serving: 3–4 oz of protein per person with about 1–2 cups of vegetables.

**Grain bowls**  
Layer quinoa or brown rice with roasted vegetables, beans, and a dressing.  
Portion per person: 1 cup grain, 1–1.5 cups vegetables.

**Sandwich wraps**  
Use whole-grain tortillas with lean meats, cheese, and vegetables.  
Serve one wrap per person with a side salad.

### 3. Dinner

Hearty, flavorful dishes include:

**Pasta with vegetables or protein**  
Cook pasta and mix with sautéed vegetables, marinara or pesto, and optional protein like chicken, shrimp, or beans.  
Recommended serving: 1–1.5 cups cooked pasta per person.

**Stir-fry**  
Sauté vegetables and protein in a savory sauce; serve over rice or noodles.  
Serving: ½–1 cup protein and 1–2 cups vegetables per person.

**One-pan roasted meals**  
Roast chicken or fish with vegetables and potatoes on a single tray.  
Portion: 4–6 oz protein per person with 1 cup of roasted vegetables.
