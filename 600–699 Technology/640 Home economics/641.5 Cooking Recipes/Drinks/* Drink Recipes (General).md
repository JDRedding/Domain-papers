# Drink Recipes

Can make a wide range of drinks at home—from refreshing smoothies and homemade lemonades to classic cocktails and cozy hot beverages—perfect for any mood or occasion. These collections gives plenty of recipes with clear steps, whether making something casual, entertaining for a holiday, or just mixing a daily favorite.

## Smoothies and Non-Alcoholic Beverages

Smoothies are a quick way to enjoy fruit and nutrients. Popular recipes include:

- **Blueberry Banana Smoothie** — fresh blueberries, banana, yogurt, and a touch of honey
- **Green Monster Smoothie** — spinach, banana, and almond milk blended together
- **Homemade lemonade or strawberry lime soda** — fresh citrus juice mixed with water and sweetener
- **Herbal mocktails** — cucumber lime spritzer or watermelon agua fresca as light, flavorful alternatives to alcohol

## Classic Cocktails

For gatherings or a drink at home, cocktails can be simple or a little more polished:

- **Margarita** — lime, triple sec, and tequila; try frozen or mango variations
- **Whiskey Sour** — whiskey, lemon juice, and simple syrup, with optional egg white for a frothy texture
- **Mojito** — fresh mint, lime, sugar, rum, and soda water
- **Piña Colada** — coconut, pineapple, and rum blended into a tropical drink
- **Espresso Martini** — coffee liqueur and vodka for a dessert-like cocktail

## Hot Beverages

Warm drinks work well for cozy evenings or seasonal sipping:

- **Hot chocolate** — rich and creamy; try spiced or gourmet versions
- **Spiced chai latte** — tea infused with cinnamon, cloves, and cardamom, served with steamed milk
- **Mulled wine or hot buttered rum** — aromatic and warming in colder months

## Specialty and Creative Drinks

Twists on classics and modern favorites include:

- **Sgroppino** — a sparkling Italian cocktail with lemon and vodka
- **Pomegranate Cosmopolitan or Blueberry Blast Smoothie** — colorful, flavorful options that also look great
- **Ranch Water** — tequila, lime juice, and sparkling water for a light, effervescent drink
