# Bouillon  
- 600–699 Technology/641.5 Cooking Recipes
*Clear Beef Soup*

Bouillon is a flavorful liquid made by simmering meat, often used as a base for soups, sauces, and stews, and commonly sold in cubes, powders, or ready-to-use liquids. Bouillon is a versatile and essential ingredient in many kitchens, valued for its ability to quickly add rich flavor and complexity to a wide variety of dishes. Whether using cubes, powders, liquid, or homemade versions, bouillon can significantly enhance both everyday cooking and gourmet recipes.

Bouillon, derived from the French word meaning “to boil,” is essentially a broth. It is made by simmering meat in water to extract flavors and nutrients. Bouillon serves as a foundation for soups, stews, risottos, gravies, and sauces, imparting depth to dishes without adding solid ingredients. It can be clear or concentrated, depending on preparation.

## Types of Bouillon

- **Liquid Bouillon (Ready-to-use):** Sold in cartons or jars; often used for immediate cooking needs.  
- **Bouillon Cubes:** Concentrated, dehydrated cubes that dissolve in hot water; convenient for storage and quick flavoring.  
- **Bouillon Powder:** Similar to cubes but in powdered form; allows flexible measurement for recipe requirements.  
- **Homemade Bouillon:** Made by simmering meat, bones, or vegetables for several hours; often strained and stored for later use. Homemade versions are free from artificial additives and high sodium, providing a more natural flavor.  

## Common Uses in Cooking

- Making soups and broths as a base.  
- Enhancing the flavor of rice, grains, or pasta dishes.  
- Adding depth to sauces, gravies, or braised dishes.  
- Using in marinades to tenderize and flavor proteins.  

## Nutritional

- Store-bought bouillon cubes or powders often contain high sodium content, flavor enhancers, and sometimes MSG.  
- Homemade bouillon allows control over salt, seasonings, and additives, making it healthier.  

## Homemade Recipe

### Ingredients

- 2 pounds minced fresh beef  
- 2 quarts cold water  
- 1 carrot, chopped fine  
- 1 onion, chopped fine  
- 1 stalk of celery, chopped fine  
- 1 sprig of parsley, chopped fine  
- 1 pound minced lean beef  
- Salt  
- Pepper  
- Celery salt  

### Directions

1. Put the 2 pounds of minced fresh beef in the cold water.  
2. Bring to a boil and let simmer for one-half hour.  
3. Add the chopped carrot, onion, celery, and parsley.  
4. Let simmer one hour longer.  
5. Strain through a fine sieve into an earthen bowl.  
6. Next day, remove the fat.  
7. Add the 1 pound of minced lean beef.  
8. Bring the stock to a boil and let simmer again twenty minutes.  
9. Season with salt, pepper, and celery salt.  
10. Serve hot in thin cups.
