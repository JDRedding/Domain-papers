# 🚀 **United States Asteroid Mining Program (USAMP)** 
— Relative, Phase‑Driven Plan

Asteroid mining becomes profitable **only after** the U.S. reaches:
- In‑space fuel production  
- In‑space manufacturing  
- Orbital construction  
- Industrial‑scale refining

The metals themselves are not the payoff —  
**the space‑based industrial economy is.**

---

## 🛰️ **Phase 1 — Discovery & Target Qualification**
Lead term: **Asteroid Catalog**  
This phase ends when the U.S. has:

- A complete catalog of near‑Earth asteroids with:
  - **Δv < 5 km/s**  
  - **Composition classification** (M‑type, C‑type, S‑type)  
  - **Rotation period**  
  - **Surface cohesion**  
  - **Thermal profile**

- A ranked list of “mineable” candidates  
- A validated orbital mechanics model for each target  
- A national telescope + radar network dedicated to mining feasibility

**Phase completion condition:**  
At least **three** asteroids meet extraction criteria.

---

## 🚀 **Phase 2 — Launch & Transport Infrastructure Build‑Out**
Lead term: **Heavy Launch Cadence**  
This phase ends when the U.S. has:

- Reusable heavy‑lift boosters operating at stable cadence  
- Orbital fuel depots stocked with propellant  
- Solar‑electric propulsion tugs capable of:
  - **Long‑duration station‑keeping**  
  - **Asteroid rendezvous**  
  - **Slow‑push relocation**

- A national orbital construction yard for assembling mining hardware

**Phase completion condition:**  
At least **two** mining‑capable tugs are operational in orbit.

---

## 🪨 **Phase 3 — Rendezvous & In‑Situ Survey**
Lead term: **Capture Strategy**  
This phase ends when:

- A U.S. mining probe has:
  - Landed or anchored to a target asteroid  
  - Conducted regolith sampling  
  - Mapped subsurface composition  
  - Measured cohesion, porosity, and thermal response  
  - Verified extraction feasibility

- A full engineering model of the asteroid’s mechanical properties exists

**Phase completion condition:**  
At least **one** asteroid is confirmed as economically extractable.

---

## ⛏️ **Phase 4 — Extraction System Deployment**
Lead term: **Mining Architecture**  
This phase ends when:

- Mining modules are deployed with:
  - **Thermal fracturing arrays**  
  - **Microwave volatiles extractors**  
  - **Electrolytic metal separators**  
  - **Dust‑control magnetic curtains**  
  - **Anchoring harpoons or regolith screws**

- Continuous extraction is demonstrated for:
  - Water  
  - Metals  
  - Silicates

**Phase completion condition:**  
Sustained extraction for **one full asteroid rotation cycle**.

---

## 🔥 **Phase 5 — Orbital Refining & Processing**
Lead term: **Orbital Refinery**  
This phase ends when:

- A refinery in orbit can:
  - Vacuum‑smelt metals  
  - Cast ingots  
  - Crack hydrocarbons  
  - Produce oxygen + hydrogen + methane  
  - Store propellant  
  - Package return cargo

- Refinery throughput reaches:
  - **Tens of kilograms per cycle** (early)  
  - **Scaling to hundreds** (mature)

**Phase completion condition:**  
Refined materials are ready for return or in‑space use.

---

## 🌎 **Phase 6 — Return Logistics & Earth Integration**
Lead term: **Return Capsule**  
This phase ends when:

- Return capsules reliably deliver refined materials to Earth  
- U.S. industry has:
  - Rare‑metal processing pipelines  
  - Space‑derived fuel distribution  
  - Aerospace manufacturing using asteroid metals  
  - Strategic reserves of platinum‑group metals

**Phase completion condition:**  
Earth‑side industry consumes asteroid‑derived materials at scale.

---

## 🏭 **Phase 7 — Industrial Scaling & Economic Integration**
Lead term: **Industrial Integration**  
This phase ends when:

- Space‑derived fuel reduces launch costs  
- Orbital construction uses asteroid metals  
- In‑space manufacturing becomes cheaper than Earth launch  
- Exportable materials generate revenue  
- GDP expands due to:
  - Aerospace manufacturing  
  - Rare‑metal supply stability  
  - Space‑based industrial sectors

**Phase completion condition:**  
Asteroid mining becomes a net‑positive economic engine.

---

## 🛡️ **Phase 8 — Regulatory, Security & Strategic Framework**
Lead term: **Space Mining Law**  
This phase ends when:

- Property rights for extracted resources are codified  
- Safety protocols exist for:
  - Debris  
  - Mining operations  
  - Asteroid relocation  
- Defense systems protect against misuse  
- International agreements stabilize mining rights

**Phase completion condition:**  
Mining operations are legally protected and strategically secure.

---

## 📈 **Phase 9 — National Payoff Phase**
Lead term: **Asteroid Economics**  
This phase ends when:

- Space‑derived fuel reduces national launch costs  
- Space manufacturing reduces dependence on Earth‑launched mass  
- Rare‑metal imports drop  
- Exportable materials generate revenue  
- Tax revenue increases from new industrial sectors  
- National debt is reduced through:
  - GDP expansion  
  - Industrial growth  
  - Strategic resource independence

**Phase completion condition:**  
Asteroid mining contributes measurable national economic uplift.
