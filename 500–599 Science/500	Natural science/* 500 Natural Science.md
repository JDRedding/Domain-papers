# Natural Science

Natural science is the branch of science that studies the physical world: its matter, energy, living organisms, and the laws that govern natural phenomena. It seeks to explain how the universe works through observation, experimentation, measurement, and evidence-based reasoning. The aim is to find patterns, develop theories, and account for processes ranging from atomic interactions to planetary motion and biological evolution. Natural science underpins much of modern life:

- **Technology:** electronics, materials science, and energy systems
- **Medicine:** understanding disease and developing treatments
- **Environmental management:** climate science, conservation, and resource use
- **Space exploration:** planetary science and astrophysics

It supplies the foundational knowledge that societies use to innovate, solve problems, and understand the world.

### Physical sciences

These study non-living systems:

- **Physics:** matter, energy, forces, and motion
- **Chemistry:** substances, reactions, and molecular structure
- **Earth science:** geology, meteorology, oceanography, and atmospheric science
- **Astronomy:** celestial objects, space, and cosmology

### Life sciences (biological sciences)

These study living organisms:

- **Biology:** cells, organisms, and ecosystems
- **Botany:** plants
- **Zoology:** animals
- **Genetics:** heredity and DNA
- **Ecology:** interactions among organisms and environments

## What Makes Natural Science Distinct

Natural science differs from other scientific domains in both subject matter and method.

- **Versus the social sciences:** Social sciences study human behavior and societies (for example, psychology and sociology). Natural sciences study physical and biological systems.
- **Versus the formal sciences:** Formal sciences such as mathematics and logic use abstract systems rather than empirical observation of the natural world.
- **Versus the applied sciences:** Applied fields such as engineering and medicine use scientific knowledge to solve practical problems. Natural science focuses on discovering fundamental principles.

## Core Methods

Natural science relies on systematic methods, including:

- **Empirical observation:** gathering data from the natural world
- **Experimentation:** testing hypotheses under controlled conditions
- **Measurement and quantification:** using instruments to obtain precise data
- **Modeling and simulation:** representing complex systems
- **Peer review and replication:** checking reliability and accuracy

These methods help scientists build explanations that are testable, falsifiable, and predictive.
