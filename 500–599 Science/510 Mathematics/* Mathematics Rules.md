# Mathematics Rules
## What Understanding the Rules Means
These are tools, not magic words. The Common Core State Standards say a hallmark of mathematical understanding is being able to justify, at an age-appropriate level, why a statement is true or where a rule comes from—not only recalling a mnemonic. Procedural skill and and understanding both matter. 

## Number Types
- **Natural numbers:** $1, 2, 3, \ldots$ (some texts include $0$)
- **Whole numbers:** $0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots$
- **Integers:** $\ldots, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots$
- **Rational numbers:** numbers that can be written as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ with integers $a, b$ and $b \neq 0$
- **Irrational numbers:** cannot be written as a fraction ($\pi$, $\sqrt{2}$)
- **Real numbers:** all rational and irrational numbers

## Arithmetic Laws
**Commutative**

$$
a + b = b + a, \qquad ab = ba
$$

**Associative**

$$
(a + b) + c = a + (b + c), \qquad (ab)c = a(bc)
$$

**Distributive**

$$
a(b + c) = ab + ac
$$

**Identity and inverse**

$$
a + 0 = a, \qquad a \cdot 1 = a, \qquad a + (-a) = 0
$$

If $a \neq 0$, then $a \cdot \dfrac{1}{a} = 1$. Division by zero is undefined.

## Order of Operations (PEMDAS)

1. Parentheses (and other grouping symbols)
2. Exponents and roots
3. Multiply and divide, left to right
4. Add and subtract, left to right

Multiply/divide are the same rank. Add/subtract are the same rank.

## Fraction Rules

From the [Online Fractions Calculator cheat sheet](https://onlinefractionscalculator.com/fraction-rules-cheat-sheet/): add/subtract need matching denominators; multiply goes straight across; divide by inverting the second fraction.

**Same denominator**

$$
\frac{a}{c} + \frac{b}{c} = \frac{a + b}{c}, \qquad \frac{a}{c} - \frac{b}{c} = \frac{a - b}{c}
$$

**Different denominators**

$$
\frac{a}{b} + \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad + bc}{bd}
$$

**Multiply**

$$
\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ac}{bd}
$$

**Divide** (“invert and multiply”)

$$
\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c} = \frac{ad}{bc}
$$

**Mixed numbers:** convert first:

$$
n\ \frac{a}{b} = \frac{nb + a}{b}
$$

**Simplify:** divide numerator and denominator by their GCF.

**Conversions**

- Fraction $\to$ decimal: divide top by bottom
- Decimal $\to$ fraction: write digits over $10$, $100$, etc., then simplify
- Fraction $\to$ percent: divide, then multiply by $100$
- Percent $\to$ fraction: write over $100$, then simplify

Never add or subtract “straight across.” That is the most common fraction error.

## Exponents and Roots

An exponent is repeated multiplication:

$$
a^n = \underbrace{a \cdot a \cdot \ldots \cdot a}_{n\ \text{times}}
$$

**Laws** (when the expressions are defined)

$$
a^m a^n = a^{m+n}, \qquad \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}, \qquad (a^m)^n = a^{mn}
$$

$$
(ab)^n = a^n b^n, \qquad a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}, \qquad a^0 = 1\ (a \neq 0)
$$

A root undoes an exponent:

$$
\sqrt[n]{a} = a^{1/n}, \qquad \sqrt{a} = a^{1/2}
$$

## Basic Algebra
- Do the same operation to both sides of an equation.
- Combine like terms: same variable and same exponent.
- Isolate the variable by undoing operations in reverse order (undo $+$, then $\times$, etc.).
- Substitute a value for a variable to evaluate an expression.

## Proportions and Ratios

A ratio compares two quantities. A proportion is two equal ratios:

$$
\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}
$$

Cross-multiply to solve:

$$
ad = bc
$$

## Core Geometry

- **Perimeter:** sum of the side lengths
- **Rectangle area:** $A = \ell w$
- **Triangle area:** $A = \dfrac{1}{2}bh$
- **Circle:** $A = \pi r^2$, circumference $C = 2\pi r$
- **Pythagorean theorem** (right triangles only): $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, where $c$ is the hypotenuse
