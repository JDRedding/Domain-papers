# 519.5 STATISTICS 
[COMPLETE REFERENCE](https://github.com/JDRedding/Domain-papers/blob/main/300%E2%80%93399%20Social%20Sciences/310%20Statistics/310%20STATISTICS%20-%20COMPLETE%20REFERENCE.md)

Overall :
- Reference note meant to help distinguish:
  - statistics as a mathematical field
  - statistics as collected data
  - 310-class library shelving
  - and the broader conceptual foundations of statistical reasoning.

Main points:

- Statistics in two senses:
  - the field: the science of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data
  - the data: the numerical facts themselves

- Standard terminology:
  - “statistics” as a field is singular; “the statistics” as data is plural
  - “statistical” is the correct adjective, not “statistic”

- Core concepts:
  - population vs sample
  - parameter vs statistic
  - descriptive vs inferential statistics
  - parametric vs nonparametric methods
  - frequentist vs Bayesian thinking
- Foundational methods:
  - mean, median, mode, variance, standard deviation, percentiles, regression, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals

- Classification:
  - In Dewey Decimal Classification, 310 is not the home of mathematical statistics
  - 310 mainly covers general statistical yearbooks and geographic collections (314–319)
  - mathematical statistics/probability belongs more around 519/519.5
  - demography, economic statistics, and vital statistics are shelved elsewhere
