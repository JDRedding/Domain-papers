# News Media
- Journalism and Publishing
- 070 News Media

News media, journalism, and publishing form the backbone of information dissemination. Together they shape public discourse by producing and distributing news, analysis, and literature. News media, journalism, and publishing form a single, changing ecosystem:

- **Trust and credibility** matter more as audiences scrutinize sources.
- **Monetization** now commonly includes advertising, subscriptions, and memberships.
- **Global, instant distribution** can send a story worldwide at once, creating both opportunity and ethical risk.
- **Regulation** covers copyright, privacy, and efforts to counter fake news.

These industries remain central to democratic life and cultural expression. They continue to adapt to new formats, economic models, and ethical demands in order to inform and engage audiences.

News media are the platforms that deliver news and information to the public. They include:

- **Print media**, such as newspapers and magazines, historically the main form of news delivery.
- **Broadcast media**, including television and radio, which offer real-time coverage and reach large audiences.
- **Digital media**, including online news sites, social media, and news apps, now especially influential because of speed and interactivity.

The role of news media is to inform the public, hold power to account, and give context to current events. Recent trends include the rise of digital-first outlets, declining print circulation, and wider use of multimedia such as video and interactive graphics.

## Journalism

Journalism is the practice of gathering, assessing, creating, and presenting news and information. Key forms include:

- **Investigative journalism**, which uncovers hidden facts and corruption and plays an important role in democracy.
- **Reportage and feature writing**, which present events, stories, and analysis to the public.
- **Data journalism**, which uses statistical and computational methods to interpret information and tell stories.

Ethical journalism stresses accuracy, fairness, transparency, and accountability. Journalists also face misinformation, political pressure, and the need to remain financially viable in a digital economy.

## Publishing

Publishing is the production and distribution of content for public use, including books, newspapers, journals, and digital media. Major traditions include:

- **Commercial publishing**, driven by market demand and profitability.
- **Academic and scientific publishing**, which circulates research and peer-reviewed knowledge.
- **Self-publishing**, made possible by the internet, which lets individuals distribute work directly to readers.

The industry is changing quickly:

- A shift from print to digital formats
- Subscriptions and paywalls replacing much traditional advertising revenue
- Growth of e-books and audiobooks
- Use of AI tools in editing, layout, and content analytics
