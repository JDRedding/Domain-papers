# Shelf‑mounting 
Hook‑on rail fasteners 

Hook-on bed rails are designed to connect a helf to pre-existing backboard using slotted metal plates or hooks instead of traditional bolt-on screws. The hooks typically engage into corresponding slots or receiver brackets on the backer wallboard, creating a firm and secure connection.

## Types and Materials
Hook-ons come in a variety of materials and constructions:

- Steel 
: Heavy-duty options provide durability and rigidity. Many models include center supports for enhanced stability.
- Wooden 
: Typically made from solid or engineered wood, often finished in cherry, oak, or other colors. Many wooden shelves are compatible with traditional slot-style boards 

Conversion Kits: Some bolt-on frames can be retrofitted into hook-on systems using adapter brackets 

## Installation Guidelines
Measure Carefully: Confirm hook slot spacing, slot depth, and width. Match hook plates to the board design 

Remove Foundation: Clear the surface before installation.

Attach Hook Plates: For metal, align hooks with slots; for wood, pilot holes may help secure screws 

Add Center Supports (Optional): For heavier shelves, attaching center glides or crossbars improves stability 

Test Fit: Ensure all hooks engage fully, shelf is level, and is stable

```
SIDE VIEW

------------------------------------
| SLOT PLATE                       |
------------------------------------
        ↓ drops onto hooks

Backer board
====================================
| HOOK PLATE                       |

```

When the shelf drops down, the hooks fully seat into the slots.

## 🔒 **ANTI‑LIFT LOCK OPTIONS**

### **1. Screw + Washer (your preferred method)**
```
TOP VIEW OF LOCK

Shelf underside
------------------------------------
| SLOT PLATE                       |
------------------------------------
   ^ top of slot

Washer overlaps slot edge:
      ________
     |        |
     | washer |
     |________|
        ↑ screw through washer
        into backer board
```

Effect:
- Washer covers the slot opening  
- Screw anchors into the board  
- Shelf cannot lift upward  

This is the simplest and strongest.

---
