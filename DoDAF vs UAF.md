# 🧭 DoDAF vs UAF 
DoDAF is the **older, document‑centric U.S. defense architecture framework** that defines required viewpoints.  
UAF is the **newer, model‑based, SysML‑aligned framework** that *absorbs* DoDAF (and MODAF/NAF) into a unified, executable architecture model. DoDAF still exists because it’s required; UAF exists because it’s better

- **DoDAF = viewpoint taxonomy + required deliverables**  
- **UAF = unified, executable architecture modeling environment**  

DoDAF is **legacy but still required**.  
UAF is **current and actively used**.

### **1. What DoDAF is**  
**DoDAF** (Department of Defense Architecture Framework) is a **policy‑level standard** that specifies *what architectural views must be produced* for defense acquisition and systems engineering.  
It defines:

- Operational views (OV series)  
- System views (SV series)  
- Capability views (CV series)  
- Data/Information views (DIV series)  
- Standards, Services, Projects, and All Views  

DoDAF is **viewpoint‑driven**: it organizes architecture information into stakeholder‑specific documents.  
It is **tool‑agnostic**, **non‑executable**, and **static** by design.

### **2. What UAF is**  
**UAF** (Unified Architecture Framework) is an **OMG standard** that unifies DoDAF, MODAF, and NAF into a **single model‑based metamodel** built on SysML/UML.  
It provides:

- A formal, integrated meta‑model  
- A domain grid (Strategy, Operational, Resources, Services, Personnel, Security, Projects, Capabilities, Information, Infrastructure)  
- Consistency rules enforced by modeling tools  
- Support for digital engineering and MBSE workflows  

UAF is **model‑driven**: architectures are built as structured, traceable models rather than static documents.

### **3. Relationship between DoDAF and UAF**  
DoDAF defines **required outputs**.  
UAF defines **how to model the architecture** that produces those outputs.

In practice:

> Architects build models in UAF, and the modeling tool generates DoDAF‑conformant views.

This preserves DoDAF’s role in policy while modernizing the engineering process.

### **4. Why DoDAF persists**  
DoDAF remains in use because:

- It is embedded in DoD acquisition policy  
- It provides a common vocabulary across defense organizations  
- It defines deliverables required for program documentation  

Its persistence is institutional rather than technical.

### **5. Why UAF is the modern approach**  
UAF is preferred because:

- It supports **traceability** across strategy → operations → systems → projects  
- It enforces **consistency** through a meta‑model  
- It integrates with **SysML**, simulation, and digital engineering  
- It works across **international defense frameworks**  
- It enables **automation**, **validation**, and **model execution**

UAF is the practical evolution of DoDAF into a modern MBSE ecosystem.
