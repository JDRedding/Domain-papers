### 0. Placement
SID says which paths exist. MFE carries the bipolar state on those paths. PED says the strength, the well, and the local flow. Q accepts or projects the updated state. That is the engine slice of an equation, with no classical step renamed as a layer.

From the master specification:

$$
\mathrm{PED}(E)=(P(E),\mathrm{Eval}(E),D(E)),\qquad E=(M,F_e,\bar M,\bar F).
$$

PED is the internal field of the Momentum–Flux Engine, not a second geometry. Power is radial engine strength $P(E)=\tfrac12(M^2+F_e^2)$. Evaluation is the potential $V(E)$. The dynamic-slice is the local engine flow $\Phi(E)$ generated by a rewrite. PED does not know what is admissible.

SID does not emit flux. It emits geometric signals that modulate engine thresholds:

$$
\sigma(G)=\text{structural pressure},\qquad \rho(G)=\text{relational tension},
$$

$$
\theta_M=\theta_M^0+\alpha_\sigma\sigma(G),\qquad \theta_F=\theta_F^0+\beta_\rho\rho(G).
$$

(and, when required, $\lambda_P$, $\lambda_E$). These modulate the engine; they are not PED coordinates.

Q is external. After evolution $E'=\Gamma(E)$, Q tests the updated state against manifold bounds and returns 1 or 0; inadmissible states are projected by $\sigma_Q$. Branch choice is that filter, not a third PED slot.

The balance constraint $F_{\mathrm{eq}}=0$ of an equation is the neutral manifold. It is not the engine coordinate $F_e$. These objects remain distinct:

- Balance: $F_{\mathrm{eq}}=0$
- PED Evaluation: $V(E)$
- PED Criticality: $\nabla V(E)=0$
- PED Dynamics: $\Phi(E)$
- Q: $Q[E']\in\{0,1\}$

### 1. Top-level equation
G carries the balance node. A valuation sends nodes to an engine state E.

**PED.Power**  
$P(E)=\tfrac12(M^2+F_e^2)$: radial strength of the two sides under that valuation.

**PED.Evaluation**  
$V(E)$: well depth at the balance node. Criticality of V is $\nabla V(E)=0$, not a restatement of the balance condition $F_{\mathrm{eq}}=0$.

**PED.Dynamic-slice**  
$\Phi(E)$: the local engine flow generated when a rewrite moves E. Isolation and substitution are flows on E, not new edges. $\Phi$ is unconstrained.

**Q**  
After $E'=\Gamma(E)$,  
$Q[E']=1$ iff the updated state lies inside the manifold bounds (domain, branch cut, sign, reality). Otherwise $\sigma_Q$ projects. Coherence length is how far $\Phi$ can run before Q drops to 0.

```
G ──SID signals σ,ρ──► thresholds θ_M, θ_F
E = (M, F_e, M̄, F̄)
P = ½(M²+F_e²)     Eval = V(E)     D = Φ(E)
E' = Γ(E)
Q[E'] = 1 iff inside bounds, else σ_Q(E')
```

### 2. Identity, conditional, functional
**Identity** (`a+b = b+a`).  
P is invariant under the swap: both valuations give the same radial strength. V is flat on the whole domain. $\Phi$ of the swap is the identity on E. Q accepts the entire domain; no filtering occurs.

**Conditional** (`x-5 = 4`).  
PED: V is critical at $x=9$; $\Phi$ is the isolation flow of adding 5. This statement alone does not imply acceptance.  
Q subsequently determines $Q(9)=1$ for the chosen admissible manifold; elsewhere the pipeline projects. Coherence length is a point.

**Functional** (`f(x)=g(x)`).  
PED computes graph strength, intersection wells, and composition/inversion flow (preserving criticality of V).  
Q admits only the intersection of domains where both valuations are defined, and rejects jumps across singularities.

### 3. Algebraic, transcendental, differential
**Algebraic.**  
P is radial strength of the carrier under the valuation of $x$. Eval is critical exactly at roots. $\Phi$ is the flow of normalization, factoring, and root extraction; these change representation of E and leave the critical set fixed.  
Q restricts the critical set (real, positive, physical range). $\sigma(G)$ from degree raises $\theta_M$: higher structural pressure, tighter engine threshold.

Example $x^2+1=0$: PED identifies the critical states $x=\pm i$. Q decides $Q_{\mathbb{C}}(\pm i)=1$, $Q_{\mathbb{R}}(\pm i)=0$. Admissibility is not encoded in PED.

**Transcendental** (`sin x = cos x - 1/2`).  
P is oscillatory because $F_e$ is oscillatory. Eval is critical on the discrete intersection. $\Phi$ is identity-rewrite flow, then inverse or iterative flow.  
Q selects the branch interval and drops at singularities. Coherence is one period unless Q explicitly extends it.

**Differential** (`dy/dx = f(x)`).  
P is strength of the rate pair. Eval is critical along trajectories whose engine state matches $f$. $\Phi$ is the integral flow.  
Q admits trajectories that meet initial or boundary bounds and drops when the flow hits a singularity. Coherence length is time-to-singularity, not a geometric edge.

For $dy/dx=1/x$, the logarithmic flow belongs to PED; the exclusion $x=0$ belongs to Q.

### 4. The Q Manifold
Q determines whether an evolved state is admissible. Q is not part of SID. Q is not part of PED. Q does not generate dynamics, potential, or strength. Q evaluates the result of evolution.

The processing chain is:

```
RDG → SID → MFE → PED → Γ → Q
```

SID: Where may motion occur?  
PED: How does the engine move?  
Q: Which resulting states survive?

Let $E=(M,F_e,\bar M,\bar F)$ denote the engine state. PED evolves the state via $E'=\Gamma(E)$. Q acts only on the evolved state.

The master definition is

$$
Q(E')=(B,T,\Pi)
$$

where

- $B=$ admissible manifold,
- $T=$ admissibility test,
- $\Pi=$ projection operator.

More explicitly:

$$
B\subset\mathrm{StateSpace},\qquad
T:E'\to\{0,1\},\qquad
\Pi:E'\to B
$$

with

$$
T(E')=1\iff E'\in B,\qquad T(E')=0\text{ otherwise},
$$

and

$$
Q(E')=
\begin{cases}
E' & \text{if }T(E')=1,\\
\Pi(E') & \text{if }T(E')=0.
\end{cases}
$$

The admissible manifold $B=\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{admissible}}$ is declared externally; it is not produced by PED. Different admissible manifolds produce different Q behavior while leaving PED unchanged. Examples include the real numbers, the complex numbers, positive values, physical states, boundary-compatible states, and branch-selected states.

The test operator $T(E')$ evaluates membership only; it does not alter the state. Interpretation: $1=$ accept, $0=$ reject.

If $T(E')=0$, the projection operator $\Pi(E')$ (equivalently $\sigma_Q(E')$) restores admissibility:

$$
E'\mapsto\Pi(E')\in B.
$$

Projection is a Q operation. It is not a PED flow and is not generated by $\Phi$.

PED may generate evolution indefinitely. Q determines how long that evolution remains admissible. Coherence length $L_Q$ is the maximal interval over which $T(E(t))=1$:

$$
L_Q=\sup\{\Delta t:T(E(t))=1\text{ for all }t\text{ in the interval}\}.
$$

PED answers how the system evolves; Q answers how long evolution remains valid.

Canonical modes include:

- Domain Q: domain membership (e.g., $x\neq 0$ for $f(x)=1/x$).
- Branch Q: branch consistency (e.g., $\log z$, $\sqrt{z}$, $z^\alpha$).
- Reality Q: field membership (e.g., $Q_{\mathrm{real}}(\pm i)=0$, $Q_{\mathrm{complex}}(\pm i)=1$ for $x^2+1=0$).
- Sign Q: positivity, nonnegativity, orientation.
- Boundary Q: initial conditions, boundary conditions, constraint surfaces.
- Physical Q: energy ranges, material constraints, causal restrictions, allowed observables.

### 5. Degree
**Linear.**  
P has constant slope set by the leading coefficient. Eval has one critical point. $\Phi$ is the two-step flow: subtract, divide.  
Q is 1 at that point if it lies in the declared bounds. No branch.

**Quadratic.**  
P carries curvature; the discriminant is the engine invariant that decides whether V has two real wells, one, or a complex pair. $\Phi$ is complete-the-square or discriminant flow.  
Q selects which well is admissible. Complex wells are Q = 0 on the real slice and Q = 1 only if the complex band is open.

**Cubic and quartic.**  
P is higher-order radial strength. Eval has up to three or four critical points. $\Phi$ is the depress / Cardano flow, or the remove-cubic / resolvent flow; both are changes of E that preserve the critical set.  
Q selects admissible wells and records bifurcations as changes in the admissible band, not as new SID edges.  

For degree $\ge 5$, $\Phi$ by radicals does not reach the full critical set; that is an engine limitation.

### 6. Systems
Each equation has its own E. Joint evaluation is criticality of V on every balance node at once. $\Phi$ is cross-equation flow (substitution, elimination, row reduction) driven by the shared engine update.  
Q = 1 only on the intersection of the individual admissible sets. A stable manifold versus isolated points is a property of the joint band, not of SID.D.

### 7. Special forms
**Binomial** (`x^n - a = 0`).  
P is radial strength $a^{1/n}$, with angular distribution on the root circle. Eval is critical at n points in $\mathbb{C}$. $\Phi$ extracts those n engine states from the power node.  
Q selects the principal or real root and drops across the branch cut.

**Reciprocal.**  
P is unchanged under $x\leftrightarrow 1/x$. Eval is critical on the symmetric root set. $\Phi$ is the flow $z=x+1/x$.  
Q excludes $x=0$ and any declared modulus band. Coherence across the reciprocal map is Q remaining 1 on both sides of the substitution.
