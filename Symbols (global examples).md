# Famous symbols

ASCII representation of over 30 global symbols, including the 
Armenian Arevakhach, Basque Lauburu, Egyptian Ankh, Yin-Yang 
Taijitu, Ashoka Chakra, Hamsa, Swastika, Dharmachakra, Star 
of David, Om, and Taeguk.

Many symbols depicted share visual motifs like rotating arms 
for eternity or the sun, endless knots for continuity, and 
stars or hands for protection, emerging independently across 
cultures and religions.
