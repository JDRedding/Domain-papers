# Chicken Broth

Chicken broth is a flavorful liquid made by simmering chicken, vegetables, and seasonings in water, commonly used for cooking, sipping as a healthful beverage, or forming the base of soups and sauces.

## What Is Chicken Broth?

Chicken broth is a clear, savory liquid obtained by gently simmering chicken meat and/or bones, often with aromatic vegetables such as onion, carrot, and celery, along with herbs like parsley, thyme, and bay leaves. The simmering extracts flavors, nutrients, and gelatin from the chicken into the water, resulting in a nutritious and flavorful liquid. Some recipes also include garlic, peppercorns, or other spices for added depth.

## Culinary Uses

- **Soup base:** Chicken broth is frequently used as the foundation for soups, stews, gravies, and risottos.  
- **Cooking grains:** It can replace water when cooking rice, quinoa, or pasta to enhance flavor.  
- **Sauce preparation:** Chicken broth provides a liquid for making sauces, reductions, and pan gravies.  
- **Direct consumption:** Warm chicken broth can be enjoyed as a comforting beverage, often with added vegetables or noodles.  

## Nutritional and Health Benefits

- **Protein and minerals:** Broth made with bones contains collagen, amino acids, calcium, magnesium, and phosphorus, which support joint, skin, and bone health.  
- **Hydration and electrolytes:** Chicken broth helps replenish fluids and electrolytes, especially during illness or after exercise.  
- **Gut health:** Homemade simmered broths may contain gelatin, which can support digestion and gut lining integrity.  
- **Low in calories:** A typical cup of homemade chicken broth is low in calories, making it suitable for light meals or fasting periods.  

## Preparation Tips

- Use both meat and bones for richer flavor and nutrients.  
- Simmer slowly rather than boil to ensure clarity and prevent cloudiness.  
- Skim off foam and impurities during cooking for a clean broth.  
- Cooking time can vary: 1–2 hours for meat-only broth, 4–6 hours or more for bone broth to extract maximal nutrients.  
- Store in the refrigerator for up to 5 days or freeze for several months.  

Chicken broth is versatile, nutritious, and easy to make at home, serving as a core ingredient in many recipes or as a soothing food during illness or recovery.

## Ingredients

- 1 large fat fowl  
- Salt  
- Flour and butter, rolled into the shape and size of an egg  
- Optional: rice, sago, noodles, or dumplings  

## Directions

1. Pick the fowl clean, singe, and draw.  
2. Wash carefully.  
3. Put in boiling water with a little salt.  
4. Cover tight and cook slowly three to four hours.  
5. Rice, sago, noodles, or dumplings may be cooked in the broth.  
6. Chicken broth should not be served clear, so add the flour-and-butter ball and let it dissolve in the boiling soup.
