# Relational Homological Algebra 
(RDG / RDGA Framework)

**RDG Formulation**

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Geometry} &\to \text{Dynamics} \to \text{Closure} \to \text{Quotient} \to \text{Invariant} \\
G &\to \partial \to \Gamma \to H_n \to \mathrm{Inv}_G
\end{align*}
$$

and only $\mathrm{Inv}_G$ survives as the persistent relational content.

## Core RDG Objects

**RDG Geometry**

$$
G = (\mathrm{Obj}, \mathrm{Rel})
$$

where

- $\mathrm{Obj}$ = set of admissible relational objects
- $\mathrm{Rel}$ = set of directed admissible relations

**Evolution Operator**

$$
\Gamma
$$

**Algebraic Carrier Functor**

$$
\Phi(X)
$$

or

$$
\Phi(R) = I_R
$$

where $I_R$ is the ideal (or carrier structure) associated with the relational object $R$.

## Chain Complexes

**Classical form**

```text
… → C_{n+1}
         |
         v
     ∂_{n+1}
         |
         v
        C_n
         |
         v
      ∂_n
         |
         v
     C_{n-1}
         |
         v
        …
```

**Constraint**

$$
\partial_n \circ \partial_{n+1} = 0
$$

for all $n$.

**RDG interpretation**

Composition of admissible relations must collapse to the zero relation. No second-order boundary survives.

**Fundamental constraint**

$$
\Phi(\partial_n \circ \partial_{n+1}) = (0)
$$

**Compact form**

$$
\partial^2 = 0
$$

**Meaning**

Boundary of a boundary vanishes. Relational reduction terminates.

## Cochain Complexes

**Classical form**

```text
… → C^{n-1}
         |
         v
      d^{n-1}
         |
         v
        C^n
         |
         v
        d^n
         |
         v
     C^{n+1}
         |
         v
        …
```

**Constraint**

$$
d^n \circ d^{n-1} = 0
$$

**Compact form**

$$
d^2 = 0
$$

**RDG interpretation**

Successive dual relational propagation annihilates itself. No higher-order residual propagation survives repeated application.

## Exact Sequences

**Sequence**

$$
A \xrightarrow{f} B \xrightarrow{g} C
$$

**Exactness condition**

$$
\mathrm{im}(f) = \ker(g)
$$

**RDG reading**

Perfect relational incidence. Everything entering $B$ is exactly what becomes annihilated. Nothing extra remains; nothing is missing.

**Carrier interpretation**

$\Phi(f) + \Phi(g)$ has the expected dimension and no residual generators.

**In RDG language**

No excess relation. No deficit relation. Perfect transfer of admissible structure.

---

## Short Exact Sequence

$$
0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0
$$

**Meaning**

- $A$ embeds into $B$
- $B$ projects onto $C$
- $C \cong B/A$

**RDG interpretation**

$B$ is a relational extension of $C$ by $A$.

**Carrier geometry**

$\Phi(B)$ contains $\Phi(A)$ and $\Phi(C)$ as extension structure.

---

## Cycles

**Definition**

$$
Z_n = \ker(\partial_n)
$$

**Element condition**

$z \in C_n$ such that $\partial_n(z) = 0$.

**RDG meaning**

Closed relational configurations. No remaining admissible boundary.

**Closure relation**

$$
\Gamma(z) = z
$$

under boundary reduction.

---

## Boundaries

**Definition**

$$
B_n = \mathrm{im}(\partial_{n+1})
$$

**Element condition**

$$
b = \partial_{n+1}(x)
$$

**RDG meaning**

Configurations generated by a higher-order relational reduction.

**Generated relation**

$b$ belongs to the relational image of an admissible predecessor.

---

## Fundamental Inclusion

From $\partial^2 = 0$ it follows that

$$
B_n \subseteq Z_n
$$

**RDG interpretation**

Every generated reduction is automatically closed. Every admissible image is already annihilated by the next reduction.

---

## Homology

**Classical definition**

$$
H_n(C) = \frac{\ker(\partial_n)}{\mathrm{im}(\partial_{n+1})}
$$

or

$$
H_n(C) = Z_n / B_n
$$

**RDG interpretation**

Residual relational geometry. Persistent relational structure. Invariant relational form. Geometry remaining after all admissible boundary reductions have been quotiented out.

**Fundamental RDG reading**

Homology is the part of geometry that survives $\Gamma$-mediated boundary dynamics.

**Symbolically**

$$
\mathrm{Inv}_G = H_n(C)
$$

(up to the invariant extraction procedure).

**Conceptual statement**

Residual relational holes. Persistent admissible structure. Coordinate-free geometry.

## Cohomology

**Definition**

$$
H^n(C) = \frac{\ker(d^n)}{\mathrm{im}(d^{n-1})}
$$

or

$$
H^n(C) = Z^n / B^n

**RDG interpretation**

Dual residual relational geometry. Invariant measurement geometry. Persistent observational structure.

**Conceptual meaning**

- Homology: “What survives structurally?”
- Cohomology: “What survives observationally?”

## Quasi-Isomorphisms

**Morphisms**

$$
f : A^\bullet \to B^\bullet
$$

**Condition**

$$
H^n(A^\bullet) \cong H^n(B^\bullet)
$$

for all $n$.

**RDG interpretation**

Invariant-preserving relational map. Geometries may differ locally. Residual relational geometry is identical.

**Fundamental meaning**

Different realizations. Same emergent invariant structure.

**Construction principle**

Invert every quasi-isomorphism.
