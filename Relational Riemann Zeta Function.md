# Relational Riemann Zeta Function
> Decomposition of ζ(s): factorization architecture → Λ-mediated interaction → ξ-symmetric zero dynamics

Structure–Interaction–Dynamics (SID) decomposition of the Riemann zeta function in Relational Dynamic Geometry notation. The SID geometry identifies the critical line as the unique dynamical equilibrium that forces the interaction of primes (via $\Lambda$) to cancel at the square-root scale, yielding the most regular possible global arrangement of the primes inside the multiplicative structure of the positive integers. The formal verification of the Riemann hypothesis locks this equilibrium in place.

```
S:
    ζ(s) = Σ n^{-s} = Π_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}

    ψ(x) = x - Σ_ρ x^ρ/ρ - log(2π) - (1/2)log(1 - x^{-2})

D:
    ξ(s) = ξ(1 - s)
```

These three equations constitute the minimal SID decomposition of the Riemann zeta function.

## Appendix: Notation and Precision Notes

### Notation conventions
Throughout the document the real-part operator is written uniformly as ${Re}$. The logarithmic integral is written ${li}(x)$.

### Explicit formula 
For $x > 1$ not equal to a prime power the von Mangoldt explicit formula reads

$$
\psi(x) = x - \sum_{\rho} \frac{x^{\rho}}{\rho} - \log(2\pi) - \frac12\log(1 - x^{-2}),
$$

where the sum runs over the non-trivial zeros ordered by increasing absolute value of the imaginary part.  
A truncated version with height $T$ carries an explicit remainder of size

$$
O\Bigl(\frac{x\log^2(xT)}{T}\Bigr)
$$

(plus a negligible contribution from the nearest prime power when $x$ is close to one).
