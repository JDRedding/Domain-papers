# Relational Riemann Zeta Function

Structure–Interaction–Dynamics (SID) decomposition of the Riemann zeta function in Relational Dynamic Geometry notation. The SID geometry identifies the critical line as the unique dynamical equilibrium that forces the interaction of primes (via $\Lambda$) to cancel at the square-root scale, yielding the most regular possible global arrangement of the primes inside the multiplicative structure of the positive integers. The formal verification of the Riemann hypothesis locks this equilibrium in place.

## Compact Relational Dynamic Geometry Summary

**Structure**

Multiplicative arithmetic geometry generated by unique factorization.

$\mathbb{N}^+$, $\mathbb{P}$, $\mathbb{C}$  

$\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s} = \prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$

---

The triple $(N^+, P, \mathbb{C})$ with unique factorization $n = \prod_p p^{v_p(n)}$. This supplies the dual presentations

$$
\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty n^{-s}=\prod_p(1-p^{-s})^{-1}\qquad({Re}s>1).
$$

---

**Interaction**  

Prime-power mediation through Λ(n), transforming local factor structure into global analytic accumulation.

$n = \prod p^{v_p(n)}$  

$\log \zeta(s) = \sum_{k,p} p^{-ks}/k$

---

The local Dirichlet term $n^{-s}$ and Euler factor $(1-p^{-s})^{-1}$ are coupled multiplicatively. Their global accumulation is encoded by the von Mangoldt function through the logarithmic interaction

$$
\log\zeta(s)=\sum_{n\geq2}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n^s\log n},\qquad-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(s)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\Lambda(n)n^{-s}.
$$
  
The summatory function $\psi(x)=\sum_{n\leq x}\Lambda(n)$ is the geometric measure of all prime-power contributions up to $x$.

---

**Dynamics**  

Analytic evolution of ζ(s), constrained by ξ(s)=ξ(1-s), with zeros acting as dynamical modes.

$s \mapsto s + \mathrm{d}s$  

Pole at $1$, trivial zeros at $-2k$, nontrivial zeros in $0 < {Re}(s) < 1$, critical line at ${Re}(s) = 1/2$

---

Analytic continuation of the parameter $s\mapsto s+ds$, the functional equation $\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)$, the simple pole at $s=1$, trivial zeros at the negative even integers, and the non-trivial zeros $\rho=\sigma_\rho+it_\rho$ inside the critical strip. The critical line ${Re}s=1/2$ is the distinguished dynamical locus.

## Interaction ($I$)
I = Λ-mediated prime accumulation

Local Dirichlet term:

$$
{term}_n(s) = n^{-s}
$$

Local Euler factor:

$$
{factor}_p(s) = (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}
$$

Multiplicative coupling:

$$
n^{-s} = \prod_{p \mid n} (p^{-s})^{v_p(n)}
$$

Logarithmic interaction (von Mangoldt form):

$$
\log \zeta(s) = \sum_{k \ge 1} \sum_{p \in \mathbb{P}} \frac{p^{-ks}}{k} = \sum_{n \ge 2} \frac{\Lambda(n)}{n^s \log n}
$$

The von Mangoldt weights $\Lambda(n)$ are the precise relational mediators between local prime structure and global analytic dynamics. Because

$$
\psi(x)=x-\sum_\rho\frac{x^\rho}{\rho}-\log(2\pi)-\tfrac12\log(1-x^{-2})
$$

(the von Mangoldt explicit formula), every geometric feature of the prime distribution is a direct image of the zero configuration under the interaction map. The main term $x$ arises from the pole (structural singularity at $s=1$); each zero $\rho$ contributes an oscillatory wave whose amplitude is governed by $x^{{Re}\rho}$. Thus the interaction layer converts spectral data into arithmetic geometry.

The global field emerges from the accumulated prime contributions.

## Dynamics ($D$)
D = Zero-spectrum evolution under analytic continuation and ξ-symmetry

Parameter evolution:

$$
s \mapsto s + \mathrm{d}s
$$

Analytic continuation yields a meromorphic function on all of $\mathbb{C}$.

Functional equation

$$
\xi(s) = \xi(1 - s)
$$

$$
\xi(s) = \frac{1}{2} s(s-1) \pi^{-s/2} \Gamma(s/2) \zeta(s)
$$

Singularities and zeros:

- Pole: $s = 1$ (simple)
- Trivial zeros: $s = -2, -4, -6, \dots$
- Nontrivial zeros: $\rho = \sigma_\rho + it_\rho$ with $0 < \sigma_\rho < 1$

Critical-line dynamics:

$$
{Re}(s) = \frac{1}{2}
$$

Under the relational dynamics, the critical-line constraint ${Re}\rho=1/2$ is equivalent to square-root cancellation in the interaction sum:

$$
\psi(x)=x+O\bigl(\sqrt{x}\log^2 x\bigr).
$$

In geometric terms this means that the prime-power points, when weighted by $\Lambda$, realize the most uniform possible distribution compatible with the multiplicative structure of $N^+$. Equivalently,

$$
\pi(x)={li}(x)+O(\sqrt{x}\log x).
$$

The oscillations visible in the explicit formula are therefore the geometric traces of the critical-line dynamics propagating through the von Mangoldt interaction kernel. Any zero off the critical line would inject a larger radial component $x^{\sigma}$ ($\sigma>1/2$) into the interaction layer, producing systematically larger irregularities in the prime distribution—precisely the obstruction that the certified dynamics now excludes.
