# Christian devotional theology
- 240 Christian practices

**Christian devotional theology** is the practice of engaging Scripture, prayer, and reflection in order to understand God’s Word more deeply and to live toward God in both belief and action. Devotional theology aims at a life that is intellectually informed and spiritually alive, so that Christians both study and live God’s Word in every part of life.

## Core Concept

Devotional theology joins systematic theological knowledge to spiritual discipline so that believers grow in faith and apply God’s truth day by day. Devotion is not only daily reading or prayer; it is an inward disposition of the heart that orients the whole of life toward an undivided focus on God.

Passages such as Matthew 6:24 and 1 Corinthians 7:32–35 present true devotion as the priority of God’s purposes over worldly concerns—an orientation that shapes decisions, relationships, and vocation.

## Practices

Typical practices include:

- **Daily Scripture reading.** Structured readings—sometimes using tools such as the Small Catechism or selected psalms—guide meditation, reflection, and memorization.
- **Prayer and reflection.** Devotion includes dialogue with God, contemplation of God’s character, and thanksgiving, aligning the heart with God’s will.
- **Meditation on doctrine.** Some devotionals include theological reflection on Christ’s nature, salvation, or the work of the Holy Spirit in ordinary life.
- **Integration with life.** Devotion is lived out in marriage, work, and community, where faith guides action and choice.

## Resources for Daily Devotion

Tools that combine Scripture, reflection, and theology include:

- **Daily Scripture devotionals.** Collections that offer pastoral encouragement and material for individuals or families.
- **Systematic theology devotionals.** Works such as Kevin DeYoung’s *Daily Doctrine: A One-Year Guide to Systematic Theology*, which pair doctrinal study with application to God, salvation, the Church, and Christian living.
- **Thematic devotionals.** Resources such as *New Morning Mercies* and *Everyday Gospel*, which connect particular biblical truths to daily challenges.

## Practical Application

Devotional theology stresses undivided dedication: knowledge and practice held together. The believer does not merely learn facts about God but lets theological truth inform decisions, moral choices, spiritual habits, and relationships. It encourages ongoing reflection on Scripture and the habits of prayer, worship, and obedience in both personal and communal life.
