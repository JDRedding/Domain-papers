# Proper Basicality
- Epistemic Justification
- (Alvin) Plantinga’s claim 

The formal claim is usually expressed with the notions of **warrant** and **proper function**:

$$
W(b,S) \iff \text{belief } b \text{ is produced by a properly functioning cognitive faculty aimed at truth in an environment for which it was designed}
$$

A belief $b$ is **properly basic** when it is warranted without being based on any propositional evidence.
