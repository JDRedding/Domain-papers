# Problem of Evil (Logical)
The classic logical inconsistency claim is:

$$
\begin{align*}
&(1)\quad \exists x\, (Ox \land \forall y\, Gxy) \\
&(2)\quad \exists e\, Ee \\
&(3)\quad \Box \forall x \forall e\, (Ox \land \forall y\, Gxy \to \neg Ee)
\end{align*}
$$

where $O$ = omnipotent & perfectly good, $E$ = “is an instance of evil.”  

A **defense** is any proposition $d$ such that:

$$
\Diamond (d \land (1) \land (2))
$$

(i.e., the conjunction is logically possible). 

A **theodicy** is a defense that is also claimed to be true in the actual world.
