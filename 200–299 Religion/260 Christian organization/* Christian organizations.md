# Christian organizations
- 260 Christian organization

**Christian organizations** often join worship to social outreach, offering community services, charitable work, and spiritual support together. Local churches and Christian nonprofits may offer programs for people who want both faith and service. Church websites, diocesan offices, and faith-based volunteer centers in the surrounding area are useful places to find current opportunities. By choosing organizations that hold worship and outreach together, people can live out their faith in service while deepening their spiritual practice.

## Key Features

- **Faith and service together.** Worship and spiritual practice undergird social work, on the view that service expresses Christian love and commitment.
- **Volunteerism.** Programs often depend on volunteers of all ages and may offer both local and international opportunities.
- **A holistic approach.** Practical help (food, shelter, counseling) is joined to spiritual support (prayer, worship, discipleship).
- **Community impact.** Worship and social work together foster spiritual growth, meet social needs, and build supportive networks.

## Types of Christian Organizations

**Church-based ministries.** Many local churches combine regular worship with social work: food pantries, homeless shelters, youth mentoring, and visitation ministries. Baptist, Methodist, Catholic, and non-denominational congregations commonly run such programs. Volunteers from the congregation often take part in both spiritual life and service projects.

**Christian charities and NGOs.** Organizations such as Catholic Charities, The Salvation Army, and World Vision provide extensive social services—from emergency relief to community development—grounded in Christian faith and values. They often include worship, prayer, and faith formation for staff, volunteers, and those they serve.

**Mission and faith-based service programs.** Groups such as Youth With A Mission (YWAM) and Christian chapters of Habitat for Humanity combine mission, humanitarian work, and worship. Participants are encouraged to treat community service as an expression of faith, often with Bible study, prayer, and worship alongside the work itself.

**Campus and student ministries.** University-related organizations such as Cru (formerly Campus Crusade for Christ) and InterVarsity Christian Fellowship focus on students’ spiritual growth while organizing service and outreach. Worship gatherings are frequently paired with volunteer projects, tutoring, or social advocacy.
