16 Tons
Tennessee Ernie Ford's 1955  

Tennessee Ernie Ford's 1955 hit "16 Tons," a song depicting coal miners' grueling labor, debt accumulation, and entrapment by company stores in mid-20th century America.

The track's lyrics about owing one's soul to the company store continue to resonate in modern discussions of worker conditions, low wages, and debt in various industries.
