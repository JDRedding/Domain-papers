^ Document review 

Review everything for a specification, in detail, identify and close any inconsistencies and gaps, and standardized its organization, structure, content, format, wording, terminology, numbering, technical language, and requirements. This included aligning terms, tightening requirements, reducing ambiguity, and applying a consistent format so the document is clear, complete, easier to interpret and maintain, aligned with established requirements, ready for implementation, and usable across teams.
