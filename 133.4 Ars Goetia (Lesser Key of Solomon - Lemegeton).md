**The Ars Goetia (from the Lesser Key of Solomon / Lemegeton) catalogs 72 spirits that King Solomon is said to have bound.** They are ranked as Kings, Dukes, Princes, Marquises, Presidents, Earls/Counts, and one Knight. Each has a traditional appearance, number of legions, and specific “offices” (powers or knowledge). The diagram organizes them into a directional wheel under the four great kings (Amaymon/East, Corson/West, Ziminar/North, Gaap/South) while listing them by rank.

These summaries follow the traditional descriptions in the *Lesser Key of Solomon* (especially the Sloane manuscripts and Mathers/Crowley edition). Details such as exact legion counts, dual ranks, and minor appearance variants differ slightly across manuscripts. The diagram’s directional “gating” and “shutter polarity” overlays appear to be interpretive modern additions layered onto the classic list.

For primary source text and the individual seals, see the full *Ars Goetia*. The spirits are presented in the grimoire as entities to be carefully constrained, not as literal historical beings. Below is a concise summary of each spirit in standard order (names and details drawn from the Mathers/Crowley edition and related sources). Appearances and powers are traditional descriptions from the grimoire, not modern claims.

### Kings (9)
1. **Bael** — Three heads (cat, toad, man). Grants invisibility; hoarse voice. 66 legions.
2. **Paimon** — Man on a camel with a crown; preceded by musicians. Teaches all arts/sciences, secrets of the earth and mind; gives dignities and familiars. Very obedient to Lucifer. 200 legions.
3. **Beleth** — Rides a pale horse with music; initially furious. Causes love. Requires careful handling with a hazel wand and silver ring. 85 legions.
4. **Purson** — Man with lion’s face, riding a bear, carrying a viper; trumpets before him. Reveals hidden things, treasure, past/present/future; good familiars. 22 legions.
5. **Asmoday (Asmodeus)** — Three heads (bull, man, ram), rides a dragon, holds a lance and banner. Teaches arithmetic, astronomy, geometry, handicrafts; gives the Ring of Virtues; invisibility. 72 legions.
6. **Vine** — Lion (or man) with a sword on a black horse. Discovers witches, hidden things, and treasure; builds towers; causes storms. Also an Earl. 36 legions.
7. **Balam** — Three heads (bull, man, ram), rides a bear, carries a goshawk. Answers past/present/future; invisibility; wit. 40 legions.
8. **Zagan** — Bull with griffin wings (then human form). Turns water to wine, blood to oil, metals to coin; makes fools wise. Also a President. 33 legions.
9. **Belial** — Two beautiful angels in a fiery chariot. Distributes dignities and senatorships; reconciles friends and foes; gives excellent familiars. 80 legions (or more in some versions).

### Dukes (approx. 23)
2. **Agares** — Old man on a crocodile with a goshawk. Teaches languages instantly; causes earthquakes; returns runaways; destroys dignities. 31 legions.
6. **Valefor** — Lion with an ass’s head. Good familiar but tempts to theft. 10 legions.
8. **Barbatos** — Appears with kings and troops (especially when the Sun is in Sagittarius). Understands animal speech; finds hidden treasures; reconciles friends and powers. 30 legions.
11. **Gusion** — Xenopilus (baboon-like). Answers all questions past/present/future; reconciles friendships; gives honor. 40 legions.
15. **Eligos** — Knight with lance, ensign, and serpent. Discovers hidden things and wars; causes love of lords. 60 legions.
16. **Zepar** — Soldier in red. Causes women to love men; makes them barren. 26 legions.
18. **Bathin** — Strong man with serpent tail on a pale horse. Knows herbs and stones; transports people instantly. 30 legions.
19. **Sallos** — Soldier on a crocodile with a ducal crown. Causes mutual love. 30 legions.
23. **Aim** — Man with three heads (serpent, man with stars, calf) on a viper, carrying a firebrand. Sets places on fire; gives wit and true answers. 26 legions.
26. **Bune (Bime)** — Dragon (then human). Moves corpses; gathers wealth; makes one eloquent. 30 legions.
28. **Berith** — Soldier in red on a red horse. Gives true answers (but lies if not constrained); turns metals to gold; dignities. 26 legions.
29. **Astaroth** — Hurtful angel on an infernal beast, carrying a viper. Answers past/present/future; teaches liberal sciences; gives true account of the fall. 40 legions.
41. **Focalor** — Man with griffin wings. Kills and drowns; power over winds and seas; sinks ships. 30 legions.
42. **Vepar** — Mermaid. Governs waters; causes storms and festering wounds. 29 legions.
47. **Vual (Uvall)** — Huge dromedary (then human). Procures love of women; reveals past/present/future; friendship with foes. 37 legions.
49. **Crocell** — Angel. Teaches geometry and liberal sciences; warms waters; discovers baths. 48 legions.
52. **Alloces** — Soldier with lion’s face on a horse. Teaches astronomy and liberal sciences; gives good familiars. 36 legions.
54. **Murmur** — Warrior on a griffin with a ducal crown; two ministers with trumpets. Teaches philosophy; summons souls of the dead. 30 legions.
56. **Gremory** — Beautiful woman with a ducal crown on a camel. Reveals past/present/future; treasures; procures love of women. 26 legions.
60. **Vapula** — Lion with griffin wings. Teaches handicrafts, philosophy, and sciences. 36 legions.
64. **Haures (Flauros)** — Terrible leopard (then human with fiery eyes). Gives true answers; destroys enemies by fire; protects against other spirits. 36 legions.
67. **Amdusias** — Unicorn (then human). Causes trees to bend; gives excellent familiars; causes musical instruments to be heard. 29 legions.
71. **Dantalion** — Man with many faces (or a book). Teaches all arts and sciences; knows thoughts; changes the minds of others; causes love. 36 legions.

### Princes (7)
3. **Vassago** — Good nature. Declares past and future; finds hidden or lost things. 26 legions.
12. **Sitri** — Leopard with griffin wings (then beautiful human). Inflames love and lust; causes people to show themselves naked. 60 legions.
22. **Ipos** — Angel with lion’s head, goose’s foot, hare’s tail. Makes witty and bold; knows past/present/future. Also an Earl. 36 legions.
33. **Gaap** — Man leading four kings. Makes men insensible or knowledgeable; teaches philosophy and liberal sciences; transports people. 66 legions.
36. **Stolas** — Raven (then man). Teaches astronomy, herbs, and precious stones. 26 legions.
55. **Orobas** — Horse (then man). Gives true answers past/present/future; dignities and favor; does not deceive. 20 legions.
70. **Seere** — Beautiful man on a winged horse. Goes and returns quickly; brings abundance; discovers thefts. 26 legions.

### Marquises (15)
4. **Samigina (Gamigin)** — Small horse or ass (then human, hoarse). Teaches liberal sciences; accounts of souls that died in sin. 30 legions.
7. **Amon** — Wolf with serpent’s tail vomiting fire (then man with dog’s teeth or raven’s head). Tells past/future; reconciles friends and foes. 40 legions.
14. **Leraje** — Archer in green. Causes battles and contests; makes arrow wounds putrefy. 30 legions.
24. **Naberius** — Black crane (hoarse). Makes cunning in arts (especially rhetoric); restores lost dignities. 19 legions.
27. **Ronove** — Monster. Teaches rhetoric and languages; gives good servants; favor of friends and foes. 19 legions.
30. **Forneus** — Great sea-monster. Teaches rhetoric and languages; makes one loved by foes and friends. 29 legions.
35. **Marchosias** — Wolf with griffin wings and serpent’s tail (then man). Gives true answers; faithful fighter; hopes to return to the seventh throne. 30 legions.
37. **Phenex** — Phoenix (hoarse). Teaches sciences; is an excellent poet; hopes to return to heaven. 20 legions.
43. **Sabnock** — Armed soldier with a lion’s head on a pale horse. Builds high towers and camps; afflicts with wounds and sores. 50 legions.
44. **Shax** — Stork (hoarse). Steals money; removes senses; discovers hidden things; can give good familiars (with care). 30 legions.
59. **Orias** — Lion on a horse with a serpent’s tail, holding two hissing serpents. Teaches the virtues of stars and mansions; dignities and favor. 30 legions.
63. **Andras** — Angel with a raven’s head on a black wolf, carrying a sharp sword. Sows discord. 30 legions.
65. **Andrealphus** — Peacock (then man). Teaches geometry, measurement, astronomy; turns men into birds. 30 legions.
66. **Cimeies (Kimaris)** — Valiant warrior on a black horse. Teaches grammar, logic, and rhetoric; finds lost things; makes one a good soldier. 20 legions.
69. **Decarabia** — Star in a pentacle (then man). Knows the virtues of herbs and precious stones; can make birds fly before the magician. 30 legions.

### Presidents (approx. 12–13)
5. **Marbas** — Lion (then man). Answers hidden things; causes and cures disease; mechanical arts; changes shapes. 36 legions.
10. **Buer** — Star (or lion’s head with five goat legs). Teaches philosophy, logic, virtues of herbs; heals distempers; good familiars. 50 legions.
17. **Botis** — Viper (then human with horns and sword). Tells past and future; reconciles friends and foes. Also an Earl. 60 legions.
21. **Marax** — Great bull with a man’s face. Astronomy and liberal sciences; virtues of herbs and stones; good familiars. Also an Earl. 30 legions.
25. **Glasya-Labolas** — Dog with griffin wings. Teaches all arts and sciences instantly; author of bloodshed; invisibility; love. Also an Earl. 36 legions.
31. **Foras** — Strong man. Teaches logic and ethics; virtues of herbs and stones; invisibility; longevity; treasure. 29 legions.
39. **Malphas** — Crow (then man). Builds houses and high towers; destroys enemies’ desires and thoughts; good familiars. 40 legions.
48. **Haagenti** — Bull with griffin wings (then human). Makes men wise; turns metals to gold and wine to water. 33 legions.
53. **Caim** — Thrush (then man with a sharp sword). Answers in burning ashes; understands bird speech; gives true answers; future. 30 legions.
57. **Ose** — Leopard (then man). Makes one expert in liberal sciences; changes shapes of men; true answers. 30 legions.
58. **Amy** — Flaming fire (then man). Teaches astrology and liberal sciences; gives good familiars; discovers treasures. 36 legions.
62. **Valac** — Child with angel’s wings on a two-headed dragon. Finds treasures; rules over serpents. 38 legions.

### Earls/Counts
Several spirits hold dual ranks (noted above). Additional pure Earls include:

34. **Furfur** — Hart with a fiery tail (then angel). Causes love between man and woman; raises storms; true answers (if constrained). 26 legions.
38. **Halphas** — Stock-dove (hoarse). Builds towers and supplies weapons; sends men to war. 26 legions.
40. **Raum** — Crow (then man). Steals treasure; destroys cities and dignities; reconciles friends and foes. 30 legions.
46. **Bifrons** — Monster (then man). Teaches astrology, geometry, and other sciences; moves corpses; lights candles on graves. 6 legions.
72. **Andromalius** — Man holding a serpent. Returns stolen goods; discovers thieves and wickedness; finds hidden treasure. 36 legions.

### Knight
50. **Furcas** — Cruel old man with long beard and white head, on a pale horse with a sharp weapon. Teaches philosophy, astronomy, rhetoric, logic, chiromancy, and pyromancy. 20 legions.
