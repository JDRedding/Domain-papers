# Continuous-Index Ledger, Trial-Balance, and Balance-Sheet Book Combined
***US 714,003***

**Patentee:** Charles W. Marshall, Greenwich, Huron County, Ohio  
**Patent date:** November 18, 1902  
**Application filed:** April 5/6, 1902  
**Serial No.:** 101,588  
**Status:** No model  
**Sheets:** 3  

**1.** A backing-sheet with one set of leaves attached along a vertical intermediate line and extending to one margin, and another set attached along the opposite margin and extending to that intermediate line, so the first set can fold onto the second; plus a second backing-sheet hinged at the first-mentioned margin, carrying leaves hinged along its upper horizontal portion.

**2.** The same combination, in which the upper-hinged leaves are balance-sheets with horizontal rulings and columns that register with the vertically hinged leaves on both front and back pages.

**3.** The same, specifying columns: balance leaves have account-number and debit/credit columns; the opposite set has ledger-folio, account-name, and account-number columns.

**4.** The same as claim 3, plus a third backing-sheet hinged to the first assembly (the index wing).

---

## Field of the Invention

This invention relates to account-books, and more particularly to an index-ledger, trial-balance, and balance-sheet combined in a single book.

## Object

To provide a book in which balances on ledger accounts may be entered for a long series of months without having to enter the title of the ledger account but once; and in connection therewith to provide:

1. a balance-sheet for the general financial statement, and  
2. an alphabetical index of the ledger names for use with the balance account, which may also serve as an index to the ledger proper.

## Broad Statement of the Invention

An intermediate backing-sheet has a backing-sheet hinged to each vertical margin.

- The intermediate sheet carries one set of leaves containing the **ledger names**, and another set of leaves arranged to be brought consecutively opposite those names.  
- One outer backing-sheet carries one or more **balance-sheets** for financial statements.  
- The third backing-sheet carries a suitable **index**.

## Construction

### Backing sheets

- Intermediate backing-sheet (cardboard or fiber).  
- Backing-sheet attached by hinge.  
- Hinges may be cloth, leather, or similar material.  
- The three backing-sheets are preferably the same size.  
- Hinge is considerably wider, so the book can fold closed and then over.

### Name leaves and balance leaves

- Leaves are about the same width as backing-sheet and are fastened along their **middle vertical line** to the middle of sheet, so each sheet forms two half-width leaves.  
- Sheets , preferably the same size, are fastened along their middle vertical line **adjacent the left-hand margin** of sheet.  
- When folded with front pages up, the **right-hand margin of a leaf** lies next to the **left-hand margin of a leaf**.  
- Turning leaves in a prescribed sequence keeps a page of names always adjacent to a matching page of balances (pages 1 with 1, 2 with 2, 3 with 3, and so on).

### Rulings

- Pages of leaves: horizontal rulings for ledger **names or titles**.  
- Pages of leaves: matching horizontal rulings plus vertical columns, typically **Dr.** and **Cr.**, for the debit or credit balance at the end of a stated period (usually a calendar month).

### How monthly posting works

1. Enter ledger names once on the name pages.  
2. Enter January balances on the first matching balance pages.  
3. Turn a balance leaf under the name pages so the next unused balance page registers with the same names.  
4. Repeat for each month without rewriting titles.

If the name list occupies several pages, each name page is paired in turn with the next unused balance page. After the whole list is posted for a month, the next blank balance page is brought back to name-page 1 for the following month. Capacity is limited only by the number of balance leaves.

### Account numbers and folio columns

- Left column of name pages: **ledger folio** (page in the original ledger).  
- Right column of name pages: sequential **account number**.  
- Left margin of each balance sheet: account numbers matching the name pages.

### Leveling strips

Thick paper or cardboard strips sit under leaves 18 so the thinner name stack stays level with the thicker balance stack. As balance leaves are turned under the names month by month, strips are removed as needed to keep the two stacks even.

### Recapitulation / statement leaves

On backing-sheet, leaves are hinged along their **upper edges**. Horizontal rulings register with the balance sheets. They gather many account balances under a few headings, for example:

- inventory  
- loss and gain  
- resources and liabilities  
- firm or corporation debit/credit  

Each leaf has an account-number column. When one month needs more than one balance page, an equal number of leaves 20 are used and footings are carried forward.

### Alphabetical index

On backing-sheet, leaves are hinged at the upper part and ruled as an alphabetical index of ledger names, with columns for ledger folio and account number. Tabs preferably project along the lower margin. A rigid cover **22** is hinged with these leaves to protect them and hold them in place when the backing-sheets are folded.

### Alternate fold as ledger index only

Fold, then turn index sheet the opposite way so the back meets the back. Cover is then on top and the back rests on the table.
