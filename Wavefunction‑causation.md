# Wavefunction‑causation

This note contrasts the standard wavefunction‑centric formulation of quantum theory with an alternative causation‑centric framing that treats quantum evolution as explicit microphysical causal processes, and it lists research directions connecting the two views.

---

### ⚛️ Quantum Theory Overview
**Wavefunction‑centric view**
- Core equation:  

$$
  i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} |\psi\rangle = \hat{H} |\psi\rangle
$$

- **Wavefunction** $|\psi\rangle$: abstract state vector in Hilbert space.  
- **Hamiltonian operator** $\hat{H}$: total energy operator governing system evolution.  
- **Probability density** $|\psi|^2$: gives measurable outcomes.  
- Conceptual tone: *state, abstract, mystery.*

**Causation‑centric view**
- Reframes quantum evolution as **microphysical causation** — explicit causal links between past, system, and future variables.  
- Structure:
  ```
  PAST              SYSTEM              FUTURE
   X₁ ───────────►  Y₁ ───────────►  Z₁
   X₂ ───╳─────►   Y₂ ───╳─────►   Z₂
   Xₙ ───────────►  Yₘ ───────────►  Zₖ
  ```
- Mathematical form:
  
$$
  P(Z_k | Y_m, X_n) = \sum_{Y_m, X_n} P(Z_k | Y_m) \cdot P(Y_m | X_n)
$$

  or operator notation:

$$
  \hat{C} = \hat{T}_{ZY} \cdot \hat{T}_{YX}
$$

- Conceptual tone: *causal, operational, real.*

---

### 🧩 Interpretation Shift
The philosophical transition:
- From **wavefunction abstraction** → to **causal networks** describing how microphysical events propagate.
- Quantum theory becomes a **causal engine**, not just a probabilistic amplitude calculator.

---

### 🔍 Future Directions
- **Causal set theory** — spacetime as a discrete causal order.  
- **Process physics** — reality as emergent information flow.  
- **Hamiltonian operator details** — energy, momentum, and interaction structure.  
- **Causal network models** — formal mappings between quantum and classical causation.
