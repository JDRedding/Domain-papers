# SCP Group of Interest (GoI)

## 🧭 The SCP GoI Megamap 
— What You Need to Know First

The SCP universe isn’t built around a single canon — GoIs shift depending on the author. But across all versions, **GoIs fall into five functional categories**:

1. **Containment-aligned** — groups that try to protect humanity (GOC, Horizon Initiative, UIU).  
2. **Exploitative** — groups that weaponize or profit from anomalies (Chaos Insurgency, MC&D, Anderson Robotics).  
3. **Religious / metaphysical** — cults with cosmic agendas (Broken God, Sarkics, Fifth Church).  
4. **Anomalous communities** — groups advocating for anomalous rights or culture (Serpent’s Hand, Are We Cool Yet?).  
5. **Extra-dimensional / post-human** — groups not fully “Earth-based” (Alexylva University, Oneiroi Collective).

# 🏛️ Major Groups of Interest

---

## **Global Occult Coalition** (GOC)
A UN-backed paranormal military force. Their doctrine: **destroy anomalies**, not contain them.  
They use “liquidation” protocols, thaumaturgic tech, and the Physics Division strike teams.  
Often clash with the Foundation due to their aggressive methods.  

---

## **Chaos Insurgency**
Originally a rogue Foundation black-ops unit.  
They believe anomalies are tools for **global revolution** and weaponize SCPs.  
Led by the mysterious **Delta Command**.  

---

## **Serpent’s Hand**
Anomalous-rights activists based in the extradimensional **Wanderer’s Library**.  
They see the Foundation as “Jailers” and free sentient SCPs.  
Loose, decentralized, often militant.  

---

## **Church of the Broken God**
A techno-religious cult worshipping a mechanical deity, **Mekhane**.  
Goal: reassemble their god using anomalous machine parts.  
Opposes Sarkic cults (flesh vs. metal). 

---

## **Sarkic Cults**
Body-horror flesh cults worshipping **Grand Karcist Ion**.  
Practice biomancy, parasitism, and apotheosis through flesh.  
Ancient enemies of the Broken God.  

---

## **Marshall, Carter & Dark Ltd.**
Elite anomalous auction house for the ultra-rich.  
They sell SCPs, experiences, and metaphysical commodities.  
Profit-driven, amoral, highly influential. 

---

## **Doctor Wondertainment**
Creates whimsical, dangerous anomalous toys.  
Often connected to childlike anomalies, living products, and corporate weirdness.  
Sometimes linked to MC&D.  

---

## **Anderson Robotics**
Produces anomalous androids, AIs, and robotics.  
Often clashes with the Foundation and GOC.  
High-tech, post-human, ethically dubious.  

---

## **Are We Cool Yet?**
Anomalous art collective.  
Creates dangerous “art installations” using SCPs.  
Pretentious, chaotic, culturally influential.

---

## **Horizon Initiative**
A coalition of Abrahamic religious groups.  
Focuses on holy anomalies, divine artifacts, and theological containment.  
Sometimes allies with the Foundation.  

---

## **Unusual Incidents Unit**
FBI division handling low-level anomalies in the U.S.  
Underfunded, overwhelmed, but cooperative with the Foundation.

---

## **Wilson’s Wildlife Solutions**
A small-town Oregon wildlife rescue that handles **anomalous animals**.  
Friendly, wholesome, partnered with the Foundation.  

---

## **Oneiroi Collective**
Dream-based hive-mind entities.  
Operate in shared dreamscapes and manipulate consciousness.  
Often involved in memetics and psychic anomalies. 

---

## **Alexylva University**
A university from an alternate Greco-Latin-dominated reality.  
Ships anomalous objects via extradimensional postal systems.  
Possibly unaware of the danger they cause. 

---

## **The Factory**
Industrial hellscape producing mass-manufactured anomalies.  
Often tied to Wondertainment and MC&D.  
Represents capitalism-as-horror.  

---

## **Gamers Against Weed**
Internet-era anomalous pranksters.  
Create memes, digital anomalies, and ironic SCPs.  
Chaotic neutral, sometimes tragic.  

---

## **Fifth Church**
A celebrity cult with cosmic, reality-bending beliefs.  
Often tied to media, fame, and memetic worship. 

---

## **Children of the Scarlet King**
A bloodline cult serving the apocalyptic entity **Scarlet King**.  
Themes: ritual sacrifice, anti-modernity, cosmic horror.  

---

## **Herman Fuller’s Circus of the Disquieting**
An anomalous circus filled with freak-show SCPs.  
Often opposes Wondertainment.  
Chaotic, theatrical, dangerous.   

---

## **Valravn Corporation**
Private military contractor specializing in occult warfare.  
Corporate, ruthless, paramilitary.  

---

## **Vikander-Kneed Technical Media**
Creates anomalous media, broadcasts, and reality-warping content.  
Often tied to memetic hazards. 

---

## **Ambrose Restaurants**
Anomalous dining chain serving impossible foods.  
Sometimes linked to MC&D.  

---

## **Daevites**
Ancient empire with necromancy, blood rituals, and warlords.  
Often appears in SCP historical lore. 

---

## **ORIA**
Iranian government’s anomalous artifact recovery agency.  
Religious, nationalistic, highly secretive. 

---

## **GRU Division “P”**
Soviet/Russian anomalous operations unit.  
Cold War-era occult intelligence. 

---

## **IJAMEA**
Imperial Japanese anomalous research agency (WWII-era).  
Often tied to war crimes and historical SCPs.  

---

## **The Wandsmen**
Anomalous artisans creating magical wands and tools.  
Small but influential.  

---

## **The Three Moons Initiative**
Extra-dimensional humanitarian organization.  
Helps anomalies escape oppressive worlds.  

---

# 🧩 Minor / Esoteric Groups

- **Arcadia** — anomalous lifestyle brand.  
- **Black Queen** — reality-hopping rogue librarian.  
- **Chicago Spirit** — 1920s anomalous mafia.  
- **Commission on Unusual Cargo** — anomalous shipping regulators.  
- **Deer College** — anomalous liberal arts school.  
- **Febris Pharmaceuticals** — anomalous drug manufacturer.  
- **Goldbaker-Reinz** — anomalous legal firm.  
- **Greazeburger Inc.** — anomalous fast food.  
- **Just Girly Things** — memetic teenage-girl aesthetic cult.  
- **Medician Academy of Occult Art** — thaumaturgic medical school.  
- **Nobody** — mysterious individual/group with reality-bending identity.  
- **Parawatch** — paranormal conspiracy forum.  
- **Pseudogenesis Productions** — anomalous film studio.  
- **Prometheus Labs** — precursor to Foundation tech; anomalous R&D.  
- **Shark Punching Center** — yes, exactly what it sounds like.  
- **TotleighSoft** — anomalous software company.

All confirmed via SCP GoI listings.
