# 🜁 The Seven Black Ships 
— Operator Profiles  

*(Age of Sail; tarred hulls; canvas; no steam; each a single entry in a rutter that should not have all seven.)*

---

## ❄️ **The Open Lead** — Arctic Ocean  
**Structure:** Whaler, black with train-oil and pitch; keeps an impossible lane of water through pack ice.  
**Interaction:** Trades in **names of the lost**, purchased with oil that never freezes.  
**Dynamics:** Cold accelerates; rescued men sleep until her lead closes.

**Operator profile:**  
- **SID:**  
  - *Structure:* A hull that radiates meltwater.  
  - *Interaction:* Name‑commerce; identity as cargo.  
  - *Dynamics:* Thermal inversion — warmth creates cold.  
- **PED:**  
  - *Power:* Frostbite multiplier.  
  - *Evaluation:* Names as currency.  
  - *Dynamics:* Sleep-binding until the lead seals.

**RPG mechanic:**  
A character who bargains with her gains resistance to drowning but suffers **accelerated cold checks** whenever she is within sight.

---

## 🌫️ **The Ledger** — North Atlantic  
**Structure:** Tarred merchantman joining convoys only in fog.  
**Interaction:** Buys cargo that never existed; pays in coins dated next year.  
**Dynamics:** Favor granted; debt assigned to a kinsman not yet born.

**Operator profile:**  
- **SID:**  
  - *Structure:* Fog-bound manifest.  
  - *Interaction:* Temporal currency.  
  - *Dynamics:* Debt displacement across generations.  
- **PED:**  
  - *Power:* Probability edits (storm missed, prize spared).  
  - *Evaluation:* Coin-date as contract seal.  
  - *Dynamics:* Debt collection by genealogical inversion.

**RPG mechanic:**  
Accepting her coin grants a **single reroll** on a maritime disaster — but creates a **future NPC** who is born owing her.

---

## 🜃 **The Empty Prize** — South Atlantic  
**Structure:** Black privateer, sails full in a calm, seen after wrecks.  
**Interaction:** Takes no survivors — only the **ship’s name**.  
**Dynamics:** Logs disagree on the lost vessel’s name; the reader forgets one name of his own.

**Operator profile:**  
- **SID:**  
  - *Structure:* Calm-sailing privateer.  
  - *Interaction:* Name-erasure.  
  - *Dynamics:* Memory loss until carving restores identity.  
- **PED:**  
  - *Power:* Calm-wind propulsion.  
  - *Evaluation:* Names as salvage.  
  - *Dynamics:* Identity erosion until anchored in wood.

**RPG mechanic:**  
Encountering her forces a **Name Save**: fail, and the PC forgets a crucial noun (ship, port, captain) until they carve it into driftwood.

---

## 🌊 **The Nootka Hull** — North Pacific  
**Structure:** Black-sided trader; answers any hail in the speaker’s tongue.  
**Interaction:** Asks only for a heading.  
**Dynamics:** Give it → she arrives before you; refuse → she appears in the next gale.

**Operator profile:**  
- **SID:**  
  - *Structure:* Polyglot hull.  
  - *Interaction:* Heading-exchange.  
  - *Dynamics:* Temporal arrival inversion.  
- **PED:**  
  - *Power:* Passage-shortening.  
  - *Evaluation:* Heading as contract.  
  - *Dynamics:* Gale-haunting if refused.

**RPG mechanic:**  
Give her a heading → **fast travel** to that port.  
Refuse → next storm includes her silhouette; navigation DCs rise sharply.

---

## 🔴 **The Ruby Galley** — South Pacific  
**Structure:** Three-banked galley; rowers unseen.  
**Interaction:** Factors trade red stones by weight of living men.  
**Dynamics:** Stones spend; the weighed people see through them. Break one → the weighed knows your beach.

**Operator profile:**  
- **SID:**  
  - *Structure:* Hidden oarsmen.  
  - *Interaction:* Ruby-weight barter.  
  - *Dynamics:* Sight-binding across stones.  
- **PED:**  
  - *Power:* Human-weight valuation.  
  - *Evaluation:* Stones as living currency.  
  - *Dynamics:* Location-binding on fracture.

**RPG mechanic:**  
Carry a Ruby Stone → you gain **true sight** once per day.  
Break it → the traded soul knows exactly where you stand.

---

## 🌬️ **The Monsoon Oath** — Indian Ocean  
**Structure:** Black-lateen dhow; appears at monsoon turn.  
**Interaction:** Carries a company one way, once.  
**Dynamics:** Fare = one true answer at the far anchorage; lie → coast no longer matches the rutter.

**Operator profile:**  
- **SID:**  
  - *Structure:* Monsoon-timed dhow.  
  - *Interaction:* Truth-for-passage.  
  - *Dynamics:* Coastal displacement on lies.  
- **PED:**  
  - *Power:* Guaranteed one-way passage.  
  - *Evaluation:* Truth as fare.  
  - *Dynamics:* Rutter-warping punishment.

**RPG mechanic:**  
Ride her → automatic arrival.  
Lie → the coastline becomes **non-Euclidean**, navigation impossible until truth is confessed.

---

## 🦴 **The Counting Barque** — Southern Ocean  
**Structure:** Rotten black barque; jawless skull figurehead.  
**Interaction:** Sails abeam until the watch counts her crew.  
**Dynamics:** Count never matches twice; the counter vanishes from his own muster and appears on her deck.

**Operator profile:**  
- **SID:**  
  - *Structure:* Rotting barque; skull prow.  
  - *Interaction:* Counting compulsion.  
  - *Dynamics:* Crew-exchange through enumeration.  
- **PED:**  
  - *Power:* Muster inversion.  
  - *Evaluation:* Count as ritual.  
  - *Dynamics:* Identity transfer until someone else counts.

**RPG mechanic:**  
Fail a Will check → you must count her crew.  
Finish the count → you vanish from your ship and appear on hers until someone else counts you off.

## 🜁 Meta-Structure: Why They Never Sail in Company  
All seven share a **single operator grammar**:

- **Black hull** = liminal boundary  
- **Single ship** = isolated operator  
- **One sea each** = domain exclusivity  
- **Transactional anomaly** = the price of contact  
- **Identity distortion** = the consequence  
- **Navigation hazard** = the signature  
- **Memory or truth** = the binding agent  

Together they form a **septet of maritime operators**, each governing one oceanic domain. A rutter that marks all seven is either:

- a **forgery**,  
- or the log of someone the **Ruby Galley** has already weighed.

--- 

Black hull, one sea, one transaction, identity as the price. What keeps them from collapsing into a fleet is a rule they all obey and none of them state.

**Domain lock.** Each ship can be sighted, hailed, or boarded only inside her own sea. A rutter that places the Open Lead south of the Horn, or the Counting Barque in the Banks fog, is already false. Cross the boundary and the entry stops matching the water.

**No company.** Two black ships never share a horizon, a convoy, or a harbor on the same day. If the party’s course would force a meeting, one of them is not there: the fog lifts, the lead closes, the galley has already weighed and gone. The absence is the confirmation.

**One open contract.** A ship, a captain, or a company can carry only one of their marks at a time. Ledger coin, a ruby, a forgotten name, a heading given, a monsoon answer still unpaid, a count unfinished, oil that will not freeze. Taking a second bargain voids the first in the worst direction: the debt comes due, the stone cracks, the lead seals over the man you pulled out.

**The rutter test.** A chart or log that marks all seven is either a forgery or the book of someone the Ruby Galley has already weighed. In play, that book is the adventure, not the ships. The pages are right about each sea and wrong about the hand that wrote them.

Compact use at the table:

| Sea | Ship | Bargain | Price in play |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arctic | Open Lead | A name of the lost, paid in oil that never freezes | Cold checks worsen in her sight; the rescued sleep until the lead closes |
| North Atlantic | Ledger | Cargo that was never listed, paid in next year’s coin | One disaster rerolled; a kinsman not yet born owes the balance |
| South Atlantic | Empty Prize | She takes the wreck’s name, not the crew | Fail the name save: one crucial noun is gone until carved into floating wood |
| North Pacific | Nootka Hull | A heading, answered in your own tongue | Give it and she is already at the anchorage; refuse and the next gale wears her profile |
| South Pacific | Ruby Galley | Red stones by weight of living men | One true sight from the stone; break it and the weighed knows your beach |
| Indian Ocean | Monsoon Oath | One passage, one true answer at the far anchorage | Lie and the coast leaves the rutter until the truth is spoken |
| Southern Ocean | Counting Barque | She sails abeam until the watch counts | Finish the count and you are on her muster until someone else counts you off |

They still do not sail together. The party meets at most one. The others exist as crossed-out lines, harbor talk, and a book that should not be complete.
