# Vae Victus

## Overview 🧭
The **Vae Victus** is one of the most storied vessels in the entire Ultramarines fleet — a strike cruiser whose service record spans **over three millennia**, making it older than most Imperial civilizations and nearly as mythic as the Chapter itself.  The Vae Victus embodies the Ultramarines’ cultural identity: continuity, discipline, and the preservation of civilization across eras of destruction. It is a physical reminder that the Chapter’s strength is not only in its warriors, but in its institutions — ships, fleets, traditions — that endure even when individuals fall. In a universe where ships are often treated as semi‑sacred relics, the Vae Victus is practically a **moving shrine of Ultramar’s military history**.

### ⚔️ Core identity  
The Vae Victus is the **most famous Ultramarines Strike Cruiser**, constructed in the **orbital shipyards of Calth** more than 3000 years ago. It is currently commanded by **Lord Admiral Lazlo Tiberius**. Strike Cruisers are already elite, rapid‑response warships — but the Vae Victus is exceptional even among them, having survived battles that annihilated entire fleets.

### 📜 Campaign history  
Across its long service, the Vae Victus has:

- **Battle of Macragge** — Fought during the Tyranid invasion of Hive Fleet Behemoth. 
- **Destroyed Ghenas Malkorgh’s command barge** — A renegade Imperial officer.  
- **Delivered the killing blow to the Ork Space Hulk *Captor of Vice*** — One of its most famous victories.  
- **Destroyed Thracia’s orbital defenses** — During the Appolyon Crusade.  
- **Annihilated Dark Eldar raiders at Pavonis** — Including the destruction of the cruiser *Stormrider*. 
- **Defended Tarsis Ultra** — During another Tyranid conflict.
  
Across these engagements, the ship has repeatedly demonstrated the Ultramarines’ doctrine of disciplined, methodical warfare — but also their capacity for decisive, overwhelming force.

### 🛡️ Longevity   
Space Marines can live for centuries, but even they age compared to this vessel. The Vae Victus has:

- Carried **multiple generations** of Ultramarines into war.  
- Survived battles that have passed into legend.  
- Outlasted entire planetary governments, crusades, and naval reorganizations.  
- Remained in active service despite catastrophic damage, refits, and millennia of wear.
