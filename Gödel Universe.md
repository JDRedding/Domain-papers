# Gödel Universe 
## Key Equations and Notation
Pressureless perfect fluid (dust) whose 4-velocity is a Killing vector, plus a finely tuned negative $\Lambda$ that partially cancels the gravitational attraction of the dust.

## Fundamentals

- Exact solution of the Einstein field equations (1949, Kurt Gödel)
- Homogeneous rotating dust universe with a negative cosmological constant
- Admits closed timelike curves (CTCs)
- Signature typically $(- , + , + , +)$ or $(+ , - , - , -)$, depending on convention
- Units often set to $c = G = 1$

## Variables and Types

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| $a > 0$ | Constant length scale (related to rotation and density) |
| $\omega \neq 0$ | Angular velocity of the dust (often $\omega = 1/(\sqrt{2}\, a)$) |
| $t$ | Time coordinate |
| $r$ | Radial coordinate |
| $\phi$ | Angular coordinate |
| $z$ | Height coordinate |
| $x, y$ | Cartesian-like coordinates in the original chart |
| $\Lambda$ | Cosmological constant |
| $\rho$ | Dust energy density |
| $\Omega$ | Proper rotation rate of the matter lattice |
| $ds^2$ | Spacetime interval (line element) |

## Metric 1 — Form used in the original post

Cylindrical-type chart ( $z$-slice omitted):

$$
ds^2 = a^2 \Bigl[ dt^2 - dr^2 + \tfrac12 e^{2r}\, d\phi^2 - 2 e^r\, dt\, d\phi \Bigr]
$$

CTC condition:

$$
r > \log\bigl(1 + \sqrt{2}\bigr)
$$

## Metric 2 — Gödel’s original chart (Wikipedia convention)

$$
ds^2 = \frac{1}{2\omega^2} \Bigl[ -\bigl(dt + e^x\, dy\bigr)^2 + dx^2 + \tfrac12 e^{2x}\, dy^2 + dz^2 \Bigr]
$$

## Metric 3 — Common cylindrical form (Rindler)

$$
ds^2 = a^2 \Biggl\{
\bigl[ dt - \sqrt{2}\,(\cosh r - 1)\, d\phi \bigr]^2
- \bigl( dr^2 + \sinh^2 r\, d\phi^2 + dz^2 \bigr)
\Biggr\}
$$

## Einstein Equation Relations ($c = G = 1$)

$$
\Lambda = -\frac{1}{2a^2}, \qquad
8\pi\rho = \frac{1}{a^2}
$$

or equivalently

$$
\omega^2 = \frac{1}{2a^2}
$$

## With Restored Constants

$$
-\Lambda = \frac{\Omega^2}{c^2} = \frac{4\pi G\rho}{c^2} = \frac{1}{2a^2}
$$
