## Relational Dynamic Geometry meta-schema
Universal Relational Dynamic Geometry criterion for what counts as the **free data** of an object class:

> Remove that piece of data and the constructors go silent.

If deletion collapses the generative machinery, identified the correct right-hand word.

Every mathematical object class $\mathcal{C}$ has

```text
FreeData(𝓒) = minimal set of generators from which all constructors of 𝓒 derive.
```

The list below is the set of canonical instances of

```text
ObjectClass : FreeData
```

---

## Polynomial / algebraic objects

- **Cubic triad** — free data: $(r_1, r_2, r_3)$  
  A monic cubic is generated by its triad of roots.

- **Quartic tetrad** — free data: $(r_1, r_2, r_3, r_4)$  
  A monic quartic is generated by its tetrad.

- **Quadratic dyad** — free data: $(r_1, r_2)$

---

## Rational / iterative maps

- **Möbius triple** — free data: three point-images  
  These determine a fractional linear map.

- **Newton dyad** — free data: the two roots  
  These determine the Newton map of a quadratic.

---

## Linear / affine systems

- **Slope generator** — free data: $\dot{x} = a$

- **Affine pair** — free data: $(a, b)$  
  Determines $f(x) = ax + b$.

---

## Dynamical systems

- **Gradient potential** — free data: $V$  
  Determines $\dot{x} = -\nabla V$.

- **Hamiltonian energy** — free data: $H$  
  Determines the symplectic flow.

- **ODE vector field** — free data: $F(x)$  
  Determines $\dot{x} = F(x)$.

- **PDE jet** — free data: the full jet $J^k$  
  Determines the local PDE.

---

## Spectral / expansion systems

- **Fourier spectrum** — free data: an ordered list of frequencies and amplitudes.

- **Taylor jet** — free data: an ordered list of derivatives at a point.

---

## Linear algebra

- **Matrix columns** — free data: the columns that generate the matrix.

- **Basis images** — free data: where a linear map sends a basis.

- **Symmetric matrix** — free data: generates a quadratic form.

- **Metric** — free data: generates an inner product.

---

## Complex dynamics

- **Julia parameter** — free data: the seed $c$  
  Determines the Julia set of $z^2 + c$.

---

## Projective geometry

- **Point at infinity** — free data that generates the Riemann sphere.

- **Homogeneous pair** — free data: $[z : w]$  
  Generates the projective line.

---

## Algebraic structures

- **Group generators** — free data: a minimal generating set.

- **Presentation relations** — free data: the relations that complete the group specification.

---

## Category theory

- **Category arrows** — free data: the morphisms that generate the category.

- **Functor assignment** — free data: the object- and arrow-assignments that generate the functor.

---

## The Relational Dynamic Geometry deletion test

The criterion is the Relational Dynamic Geometry minimality test:

```text
Delete the proposed free data.
If the constructors cannot produce anything,
have identified the true generator.
```

This is the same test Relational Dynamic Geometry uses for SID-mode minimality:

- **S-mode:** structural free data
- **I-mode:** interaction constructors
- **D-mode:** dynamic propagation

If S-mode is empty, I-mode and D-mode collapse.
