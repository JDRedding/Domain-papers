# 40K rules 

## Option 1:  Warhammer 40K Core Rules
10th Edition

### Core Concepts
- **Units & Models:** Units are groups of one or more models. Datasheets define stats, weapons, and abilities.
- **Keywords:** Rules often apply to units with specific keywords such as `INFANTRY` or `VEHICLE`.
- **Distances:** Measured in inches between the closest points of bases.
- **Engagement Range:** Within 1" horizontally and 5" vertically of an enemy model.

### Turn Structure
A battle is played in **battle rounds**, each containing two player turns.

**Command Phase**
- Take Battle-shock tests for units below half-strength (roll 2D6 ≥ Leadership).
- Gain Command Points (CP).

**Movement Phase**
- Units make Normal, Advance, Fall Back, or Reinforcement moves.
- Reinforcements arrive from specified edges depending on the battle round.

**Shooting Phase**
- Select a unit → select targets → resolve attacks.
- Roll to hit using Ballistic Skill.
- Roll to wound using the Strength vs Toughness table.
- The target makes saving throws modified by Armour Penetration (AP).

**Charge Phase**
- Declare charges (targets must be within 12").
- Roll 2D6 for charge distance.

**Fight Phase**
- Units in Engagement Range fight.
- Sequence: Fights First → Remaining Combats.
- Pile In (3"), Fight, Consolidate (3").

### Morale & Battle-shock
On a failed Battle-shock test:
- Objective Control (OC) becomes 0.
- The unit cannot use Stratagems.
- Falling Back requires a Desperate Escape test.

### Objectives
- A model can control an objective if it is within 3" horizontally and 5" vertically.
- Add all OC values; the side with the highest total controls the objective.

### Universal Special Rules (examples)
- **Deep Strike:** Arrive more than 9" away from enemies.
- **Stealth:** −1 to be hit at range.
- **Lethal Hits:** Critical hits automatically wound.
- **Twin-linked:** Re-roll wound rolls.

## Option 2: One Page 40K 
Fan-Made System

A single-page simplified ruleset designed for fast play with 40K miniatures.

### Game Structure
- Alternating activations: players take turns activating one unit at a time.
- 4 rounds.
- Score points for destroying enemy units.

### Unit Stats & Tests
- **Quality Test:** Roll 1D6 ≥ the unit’s Quality to succeed.
- **Attacks:** Each attack requires a Quality test to hit; the defender makes Quality tests to block.

### Movement
- **Move:** Standard movement.
- **Run:** 12"; cannot shoot.
- **Assault:** 12"; move into melee.
- Units must stay within 1" of a member and within 12" of all others.

### Shooting
- One Quality test per attack; each success is a hit.
- The defender rolls Quality tests to block hits.
- Unblocked hits cause wounds.

### Melee
- Models within 2" may attack.
- Assaulting models get +1 Attack.
- Compare wounds inflicted; the loser takes a morale test.

### Morale
- On a fail: the unit becomes **Pinned** (idle next activation, strikes last).
- If below half size and the fail occurred in melee: the unit **Routs** (removed).

### Vehicles & Walkers
- Vehicles have **Armored** and **Relentless** rules.
- Move 12–18" depending on the action.
- A vehicle damage table applies when wounded.

### Special Rules (examples)
- **Deep Strike:** Arrive after round 1, 6"+ from enemies; scatter on a 1–2.
- **Fearless:** Roll an extra die for morale and pick the highest.
- **Rending:** Hits of 6 cause automatic wounds that ignore armor.
