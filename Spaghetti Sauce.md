# Tomato-Meat Sauce (Spaghetti Sauce)

**Makes:** 2 servings (about 1/2 cup each)

## Ingredients

- 1 small onion  
- 1 tablespoon fat or oil  
- 1 cup canned or cooked tomatoes  
- 1 cup finely cut-up, cooked beef or pork  
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce  

## Directions

1. Chop the onion.  
2. Cook the onion in the fat or oil in a fry pan until tender and lightly browned.  
3. Stir in the remaining ingredients.  
4. Cook over low heat about 30 minutes to blend flavors.  
5. Add a little water during cooking, if needed.

## Menu Idea

Serve on hot cooked spaghetti, macaroni, noodles, or rice. Serve with tossed salad and ice cream.
