# Appointment of Dante 
*Lord Regent of Imperium Nihilus*

## 🜂 Overview

**Roboute Guilliman** appointed **Commander Dante** as the **Lord Regent of Imperium Nihilus** immediately following the **Devastation of Baal** and the final defeat of Hive Fleet Leviathan. Dante’s appointment is one of the most consequential political restructurings in modern Imperial history—effectively creating a **dual Imperium**, with Guilliman ruling the Imperium Sanctus and Dante ruling Imperium Nihilus.

This pivotal event occurred in **999.M41** (Baal Time) or **006.M42** (Standard Galaxy Time), shortly after the opening of the **Great Rift** (*Cicatrix Maledictum*). Guilliman bestowed this immense authority upon Dante to coordinate the defense of the galaxy’s northern half, which was cut off from Terra’s light. The appointment was a direct result of Guilliman’s trust in Dante’s **1,100 years** of experience as Chapter Master, his resilience, and his unwavering dedication to duty—deeming him the most reliable choice to hold the fractured Imperium together in the Primarch’s absence. Dante was named **Warden of Imperium Nihilus** and given command of all Imperial forces north of the Great Rift, with Baal serving as the acting capital of the Dark Imperium.

### **1. Timing of the Appointment**  
All authoritative sources agree that Guilliman appointed Dante **right after the Blood Angels’ victory over Hive Fleet Leviathan** during the **Devastation of Baal**, in the opening phase of the **Era Indomitus**.  
- Dante is explicitly stated to have been declared **Lord Regent of Imperium Nihilus** following this battle.
- This corresponds to **999.M41** (Baal local chronology) / **early M42** (Imperial standard), aligning with stated **006.M42**.

### **2. Reason for Guilliman’s Choice**  
The sources reinforce your interpretation:  
- Dante has served as Chapter Master for **~1,100 years**, making him one of the most experienced commanders alive.   
- He is the **oldest living non‑Dreadnought Loyalist Space Marine**, over **1,500 years old** by the end of M41.   
- His reputation for discipline, strategic clarity, and moral resilience made him the most stable anchor for the Imperium’s northern half.

### **3. Scope of Authority Granted**  
Guilliman’s decree made Dante:  
- **Lord Regent of Imperium Nihilus**  
- **Warden of Imperium Nihilus**  
- De facto ruler of the **Dark Imperium**, the half cut off from Terra and the Astronomican.   

This matches your description precisely: Baal became the **administrative center** of the Dark Imperium, and Dante was empowered to coordinate **all Imperial military and political assets** north of the Rift.

### **4. Strategic Purpose**  
The appointment served several critical functions:  
- Establishing a **stable command hierarchy** in a region severed from Imperial communication.  
- Providing a **symbolic figure** whose legend could unify scattered worlds.  
- Ensuring that the Imperium Nihilus had a leader capable of resisting both external threats (Tyranids, Chaos, Orks) and internal collapse.

### **5. Post‑Appointment Developments**  
Sources also note that Dante later:  
- **Crossed the Rubicon Primaris**, becoming a Primaris Astartes to continue his service.  
- Continued to act as the Imperium’s most senior commander in the Dark Imperium, shaping its early Indomitus‑era campaigns.
