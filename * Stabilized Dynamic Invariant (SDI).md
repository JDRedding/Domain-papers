# Stabilized Dynamic Invariant (SDI)

## SDI Principle

> A stabilized dynamic invariant is the persistent relational organization maintained by a continuing dynamical process. Its identity derives from the ongoing dynamics that preserve it, not from any frozen representation of it.

## Status

Foundational concept for generator-level dynamics and recovery theory.

```text
Dynamics
=
Process

SDI
=
Persistent Organization

Projection
=
Representation
```

or equivalently:

```text
Generator
   ↓
SDI
   ↓
Representation
```

The SDI is the primary object of persistence; representations are compressed views of the SDI.

---

## Definition

Let

$$
D(t)
$$

be a continuing dynamical process.

A **Stabilized Dynamic Invariant (SDI)** is a relational organization

$$
I
$$

such that

$$
\frac{dD}{dt} \neq 0
$$

while

$$
I(D(t)) \approx \text{constant}.
$$

The underlying process continues to evolve, but the relational organization remains stable.

---

## Interpretation

An SDI is not a static object.

It is a pattern that remains coherent while being continuously maintained by ongoing dynamics.

In short:

```text
Motion persists.
Organization persists.
Identity persists.
```

The persistence is dynamic rather than static.

## Examples

### Kuramoto Synchronization

For

$$
\theta_i(t) = \Omega t + \delta_i,
$$

the oscillators continue to move.

The stabilized invariant is the maintained phase relation

$$
\delta_i - \delta_j.
$$

The label

```text
"synchronized"
```

is merely a projection of that invariant.

### Chimera States

A chimera maintains:

```text
coherent region
+
incoherent region
```

within a continuously evolving phase field.

The SDI is the maintained phase organization.

The classification

```text
"chimera"
```

is a projection of the invariant.

### Hopf Bifurcation

Before and after a Hopf bifurcation the system may possess different SDIs.

The bifurcation is a reorganization of the generator that changes the invariant structure being maintained.

```text
Generator_A
    ↓
SDI_A

   Hopf

Generator_B
    ↓
SDI_B
```

## Relation to Frozen Projections

Let

$$
\Pi : \mathrm{SDI} \to O
$$

be a projection into a representation space.

Then:

```text
SDI
=
Walker

Projection
=
Footprint
```

Many distinct SDIs may project to the same representation.

Therefore representations classify the projection, not necessarily the invariant that generated it.
