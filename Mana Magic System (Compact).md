# Compact Mana Magic System

A ready-to-use mana system you can drop into almost any homemade or rules-light RPG.

## Core Loop

You have a pool of Mana Points (MP).  
You spend MP to cast spells.  
When you run out, you can’t cast until you recover some.

That’s the whole engine.

## Mana Pool

- **Starting MP:** 8 + Magic stat (or just 10 if you don’t use stats)
- **Growth:** +2 MP per level (or per “rank”)

**Optional flavors:**

- Wizards / scholars get +2 extra starting MP
- Warriors who dabble get half the pool

## Casting

Pay the listed cost and the spell happens. No slots, no preparation.

| Power    | Cost   | Typical effect                                      |
|----------|--------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Cantrip  | 0 MP   | Tiny utility or 1d4 damage                          |
| Minor    | 1–2 MP | 1d6–1d8 damage, small buff, or simple utility       |
| Standard | 3–4 MP | 2d6–2d8 damage, decent control or heal              |
| Major    | 6+ MP  | Big area, strong control, or fight-changing effect  |

You cannot spend more MP than you currently have (unless you use Overdraw, below).

**Optional roll:** If you want a chance of failure, roll Magic + relevant skill vs a target number. Failure still spends the mana.

## Recovery

- **Short rest** (10–30 minutes, no fighting): recover 1d4 + Magic modifier MP (minimum 1)
- **Long rest** (sleep / camp): restore all MP
- **In combat** (optional): spend your action to meditate and recover 1 MP

## Overdraw (optional spice)

You may spend MP you don’t have.

- Each extra point costs you 1 fatigue or 1d4 damage (your choice of flavor).
- After the fight you are exhausted until you take a long rest.

This lets desperate casters go nova, but they pay for it.

## Tiny Starter Spell List

Use these as-is or as templates.

- **Spark (1 MP)** — 1d6 fire damage to one target within short range.
- **Mend (2 MP)** — Heal 1d8 hit points.
- **Gust (2 MP)** — Knock a target prone or push them 10 feet.
- **Ward (3 MP)** — +2 to AC or a save until your next turn.
- **Bolt (4 MP)** — 2d8 damage, or 1d8 to two targets.
- **Veil (3 MP)** — Become invisible until you attack or cast again.
- **Burst (6 MP)** — 2d6 damage to everyone in a small area.

You can invent new spells on the fly using the cost table above.

## Design Notes

Why this stays simple:

- One resource to track.
- No daily slots or “prepared vs known” lists.
- Recovery is predictable so players can plan.
- Overdraw gives drama without extra subsystems.

You can run an entire campaign with just the pool, the four cost bands, and a handful of effects. Add more only if the table asks for it.

# Spell Tiers

A clean spell-tier design that fits the simple mana system. Use 5 tiers — enough to feel like growth without becoming a spreadsheet.

## Tier Overview

| Tier     | Cost    | Role              | Feel                                      |
|----------|---------|-------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Cantrip  | 0 MP    | Always-on tools   | Weak, reliable, never fight-winning       |
| Lesser   | 1–2 MP  | Bread-and-butter  | Frequent combat/utility spells            |
| Standard | 3–4 MP  | Real power        | Noticeable swing in a fight               |
| Greater  | 6–8 MP  | Set-piece magic   | Changes the encounter                     |
| High     | 10+ MP  | Rare, costly      | Scene-defining or desperate               |

**Rule of thumb:** Don’t skip tiers. A 7 MP spell should feel clearly stronger than a 4 MP spell.

## What Each Tier Can Do

### Cantrip (0 MP)

- **Damage:** 1d4, single target, short range
- **Heal:** 1 HP, or stabilize only
- **Control:** tiny shove, snuff a candle, spark a torch
- **Duration:** instantaneous or 1 round
- **Area:** none
- **Limit:** 1 cantrip per turn

### Lesser (1–2 MP)

- **Damage:** 1d6–1d8, one target
- **Heal:** 1d6–1d8
- **Control:** knock prone, short slow, brief disadvantage
- **Utility:** lock/unlock simple things, small light, short message
- **Duration:** 1 minute or 1–2 fight rounds
- **Range:** short to medium
- **Area:** none, or a 5-foot splash

### Standard (3–4 MP)

- **Damage:** 2d6–2d8, or 1d8 to two targets
- **Heal:** 2d6–2d8, or remove one minor condition
- **Control:** restrain, silence one target, wall a doorway
- **Utility:** invisibility until you attack, speak with animals, detect magic clearly
- **Duration:** 1 minute, or concentration
- **Area:** small burst (about 10 feet)

### Greater (6–8 MP)

- **Damage:** 3d6–4d8, or solid area damage
- **Heal:** 3d8 or heal + remove a serious condition
- **Control:** hold several enemies, create a lasting barrier, dominate a weak mind briefly
- **Utility:** fly for a short time, teleport short distance, restore a broken object
- **Duration:** several minutes, or the whole fight
- **Area:** medium burst or a short wall/line

### High (10+ MP)

- **Damage:** fight-ending or multi-target devastation
- **Heal:** bring someone back from the brink, mass heal the party a little
- **Control:** change the battlefield (collapse a roof, freeze a river, banish one foe)
- **Utility:** long teleport, scry a known place, rewrite a short scene
- **Duration:** scene-long or until dawn
- **Cost of entry:** usually requires a short ritual, rare component, or Overdraw

## Example Spells by Tier

**Cantrips**

- Spark — 1d4 fire, 30 feet
- Glow — light equal to a lantern for 10 minutes
- Nudge — push a small unattended object 5 feet

**Lesser**

- Bolt (1) — 1d6 force damage
- Mend Flesh (2) — heal 1d8
- Trip Hex (2) — target must save or fall prone
- Whisper (1) — send a one-sentence message to someone you can see

**Standard**

- Fire Lance (4) — 2d8 fire, 60 feet
- Restore (4) — heal 2d8 and end one poison or fear
- Bind (3) — restrain one target until they break free
- Veil (3) — invisible until you attack or cast again

**Greater**

- Storm Burst (7) — 3d6 lightning to everyone in a 15-foot area
- Iron Ward (6) — +3 defense to one ally for the fight
- Chain (8) — restrain two targets
- Stride (6) — teleport 30 feet, including through a window or thin wall

**High**

- Sunder (12) — destroy a portcullis, collapse a balcony, or deal 6d8 to one huge target
- Dawn Hands (10) — heal 4d8 to three allies
- Exile (12) — banish one creature for 1 minute if it fails a hard save
- Gate Step (15) — teleport the party across the dungeon (once per day, GM call)

## Quick Spell-Design Recipe

When you invent a new spell, ask:

1. How big is the effect? (one target / small area / whole fight)
2. How long does it last? (instant → 1 minute → whole scene)
3. How hard is it to undo? (easy save / hard save / no save)
4. Does it end the problem or just help? (help = lower tier; end the problem = higher tier)

Then drop it into the nearest cost band.

**Cheap upgrades:**

- +1 MP: extra target or +1d6
- +2 MP: double duration or add a small area
- +3 MP: ignore cover, or affect a stronger creature type

## Play Advice

- Most fights should be solved with Lesser and Standard spells.
- Greater should feel like “I am spending my reserves.”
- High should be rare enough that the table remembers the moment.

That keeps mana meaningful without turning every caster into a walking artillery piece.

# Spell Slots System

A simple spell-slot system that matches the tiers above. It stays light: few numbers, easy tracking, no daily spell-prep spreadsheet.

## The Idea

You don’t spend mana one point at a time.  
You have a few slots of each tier.  
Casting a spell uses one slot of that tier (or a higher one).

That’s it.

## Slot Tiers

Use the same names as before:

| Slot     | What it casts         | Rough power |
|----------|-----------------------|-------------|
| Cantrip  | Unlimited / 0-cost    | Tiny        |
| Lesser   | 1–2 MP equivalent     | Common      |
| Standard | 3–4 MP equivalent     | Strong      |
| Greater  | 6–8 MP equivalent     | Big         |
| High     | 10+ MP equivalent     | Rare        |

You may cast a lower-tier spell with a higher slot.  
You may not cast a higher-tier spell with a lower slot.

## Starting Slots

**Full caster** (wizard, priest, sorcerer):

| Level | Lesser | Standard | Greater | High |
|-------|--------|----------|---------|------|
| 1     | 3      | —        | —       | —    |
| 2     | 4      | —        | —       | —    |
| 3     | 4      | 2        | —       | —    |
| 4     | 4      | 3        | —       | —    |
| 5     | 4      | 3        | 1       | —    |
| 6     | 4      | 3        | 2       | —    |
| 7     | 4      | 3        | 2       | 1    |
| 8+    | 4      | 4        | 2       | 1    |

**Half caster** (knight-mage, ranger, dabbler):  
Use half the slots, rounded down. No High slots until late game.

**Cantrips:** always available. Limit them to 1 per turn.

This table is small on purpose. Don’t add 9 spell levels.

## How Casting Works

1. Pick a known spell.
2. Spend one matching slot (or a higher one).
3. The spell happens.

No preparation list required unless you want that flavor.

**Optional prep rule:** At dawn, mark which known spells are “ready.” You can only cast the ready ones, but you can still use a higher slot to cast a ready lower-tier spell.

## Recovery

- **Short rest:** recover 1 Lesser slot, or convert one unused higher slot into two lower slots.
- **Long rest:** all slots return.

That’s enough recovery without turning every lunch break into a full refill.

## Overcast (optional)

If you have no slots left of the right tier, you may:

- Burn a higher slot for a lower spell (already allowed), or
- Cast anyway and take 1 fatigue / 1d6 damage per missing tier.

Example: casting a Standard spell with no Standard or Greater slots left costs 1 fatigue.

Use this only if you want last-ditch heroics.

## Known Spells

Keep the known-spell list tiny.

- Level 1: 4 Lesser spells + 2 cantrips
- Each new level: +1 spell of a tier you can cast
- Finding a teacher, book, or spirit can add 1 extra spell

You don’t need 300 spells. A caster with 8–12 spells already feels versatile.

## Example Day at Level 3

Slots: 4 Lesser, 2 Standard

Possible day:

- Bolt (Lesser)
- Mend Flesh (Lesser)
- Trip Hex (Lesser)
- Whisper (Lesser)
- Fire Lance (Standard)
- Bind (Standard)

That’s six real casts, plus cantrips. Enough for a dungeon crawl, not enough to solve every room with magic.

## If You Want Mana and Slots

Use one, not both, unless you like extra tracking.

**Simple hybrid:**

- Slots are the daily limit
- Mana is only for cantrips, rituals, and Overcast

Or the reverse:

- Mana is the main resource
- Slots are just a shorthand: Lesser = 2 MP, Standard = 4 MP, Greater = 7 MP, High = 12 MP

Don’t run full mana and full slots at the same time. That’s two resource systems for one job.

## Why This Stays Simple

- 4 slot types, not 9 levels
- One column of numbers per character
- Higher slots can fill lower needs
- Short rest gives a sip, long rest gives the tank

Use the earlier spell-tier list as the actual effects; this system only answers how many times you can cast them.
