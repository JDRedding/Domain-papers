# Sandwich-on-a-stick
**Sandwich-on-a-stick** is a deconstructed sandwich on a skewer: bread cubes, folded deli meat, cheese, and vegetables, with sauce on the side so nothing gets soggy. Use 4–6 inch bamboo skewers for lunches or 8–12 inch skewers for parties. Hearty bread (Italian, ciabatta, baguette, toasted white) holds up best. Fold meats accordion-style so they stay put.

## Assembly rules
- Cut everything into similar bite-size pieces (~¾–1 inch).
- Start and end with bread so the stack stays together.
- Keep wet ingredients (tomato, pickle, lettuce) away from bread until serving if packing ahead.
- Serve condiments on the side: mayo, mustard, ranch, Italian vinaigrette, Thousand Island, or marinara.

### Classic recipes ideas

**Ham & cheese (simplest lunch version)**  
Thread: bread cube → folded ham → cheddar cube → lettuce or pickle → ham → cheese → bread.  
Dip: mustard or mayo. Add a cherry tomato for extra color.

**Club sandwich skewers**  
Per 6-inch skewer (repeat the pattern once): toasted bread cube, lettuce, cherry tomato, cooked bacon piece, cheddar or Swiss, folded turkey and/or ham, bread.  
Optional: avocado cube. Dip: ranch or mayo. Toast the bread or brush with garlic butter for extra flavor.

**Italian sub / antipasto skewers**  
Fold salami, pepperoni, and ham. Add provolone or mozzarella cubes (or mini mozzarella balls), cherry tomato, olive, pepperoncini or roasted pepper, and a basil leaf. Bread is optional—many versions skip it for a lighter appetizer.  
Dip or drizzle: Italian vinaigrette or olive oil + balsamic. Marinate the cheese and tomatoes 30–60 minutes if you have time.

**BLT skewers**  
Toasted bread, cooked bacon pieces, iceberg or romaine chunks, cherry tomato halves.  
Dip: mayo, ranch, or avocado-sriracha mayo. Keep lettuce dry until the last minute.

**PB&J on a stick**  
Make a peanut butter and jelly sandwich, then cut it into 1-inch squares. Alternate with grapes and banana slices (or raspberries). Serve immediately so the fruit doesn’t weep.

### More ideas
- **Cubano:** ham, Swiss, pickle chunks. Dip: Dijon-mayo.
- **Reuben:** rye cubes, corned beef, Swiss, sauerkraut or cabbage. Dip: Thousand Island.
- **Muffuletta-style:** focaccia or Italian bread, ham, salami, mozzarella, olive, roasted pepper. Dip: Italian dressing.
- **Meatball “sub”:** toasted bread, cooked mini meatballs, provolone. Warm slightly and dip in marinara.
- **Caprese-adjacent:** bread, mozzarella, tomato, basil, drizzle of balsamic.
- **Kid lunch mix:** turkey + cheddar + cucumber + tomato + bread, mustard on the side.
- **Vegetarian:** bread, cheese, cucumber, tomato, olive, roasted pepper, lettuce. Hummus or vinaigrette for dipping.

### Packing and serving tips
For lunchboxes, assemble the night before but keep tomatoes and lettuce separate, or pack the skewer in a container so it doesn’t get crushed. Break off sharp skewer tips for young kids. For parties, make a platter of 3–4 flavor combinations and put small bowls of dips in the middle.

## Complete recipes with exact quantities

Scale by dividing the desired number of skewers by the recipe’s base yield, then multiply each ingredient by that factor. Round up whole items (slices, tomatoes, skewers). A typical adult lunch is 2–3 small (6-inch) skewers; a party appetizer is 1–2 small skewers per person.

SCALING
```

Let S  = desired number of skewers
Let S0 = recipe base yield
Let k  = S / S0

For any base quantity Q0:
    Q = k * Q0

Whole items (slices, tomatoes, skewers):
    Q_int = ceil(k * Q0)

Typical portions:
    Adult lunch        ~ 2-3 small (6 in) skewers
    Party appetizer    ~ 1-2 small skewers per person

```
### 1. CLUB SANDWICH SKEWERS
```
Yield S0 = 6 skewers
Time     ~ 20 min
k        = S / 6

INGREDIENTS (base)
  3 slices sandwich bread, toasted
  12 slices deli turkey
  6 slices cooked bacon
  12 small squares cheddar or Swiss   (~4 oz / 113 g cheese)
  12 cherry tomatoes
  18 small pieces leaf lettuce
  3 Tbsp butter, melted              (45 mL)
  1/2 tsp garlic puree               (2.5 mL)
  6 skewers, 10-12 in
  ranch or mayonnaise, to serve

BREAD CUT
  3 slices * 6 pieces/slice = 18 bread pieces
  pieces per skewer         = 3

BACON CUT
  6 slices * 4 pieces/slice = 24 small pieces

ASSEMBLY ORDER (repeat once per skewer)
  bread, lettuce, tomato, bacon, cheese, turkey, bread

METHOD
  1. Mix butter + garlic. Brush bread both sides. Pan-toast gold.
  2. Cut toasted bread into 6 pieces each.
  3. Cut bacon into quarters. Fold turkey.
  4. Thread order above. Serve dip on the side.

SCALE EXAMPLE
  S = 12  =>  k = 2
      bread 6 slices, turkey 24, bacon 12, cheese 24 squares,
      tomatoes 24, lettuce 36, butter 6 Tbsp, skewers 12

```

### 2. HAM AND CHEESE SANDWICH-ON-A-STICK
```
Yield S0 = 10 small skewers  (~4 servings)
Time     ~ 10 min
k        = S / 10

INGREDIENTS (base)
  2 cups hearty bread cubes          (~4-5 slices; 473 mL packed cubes)
  1.5 cups cubed mozzarella+cheddar  (~6 oz / 170 g total)
  1 thick deli ham slice, strips     (~3-4 oz / 85-113 g)
  10 cherry tomatoes
  10 skewers, 6 in
  mustard, to dip

ASSEMBLY ORDER (each skewer)
  bread, cheddar, ham, tomato, ham, mozzarella, bread

METHOD
  Cut all to ~1 in bites. Thread. Serve immediately.

OPTIONAL
  cucumber chunks, pickle slices, turkey in place of ham

SCALE
  Q = (S/10) * Q0
  tomatoes and skewers: Q_int = S   (1 each per skewer)

```

### 3. ITALIAN SUB SKEWERS
```
Yield S0 = 12 skewers
Time     ~ 20 min + 1 h marinade (optional)
k        = S / 12

VINAIGRETTE
  1/4 cup olive oil                  (60 mL)
  1/4 cup red wine vinegar           (60 mL)
  2 fresh basil leaves, chopped
  1 tsp Dijon                        (5 mL)
  1 tsp sugar                        (5 mL)
  1/2 tsp garlic powder              (2.5 mL)
  1/2 tsp onion powder               (2.5 mL)
  1/4 tsp kosher salt                (1.25 mL)
  1/4 tsp black pepper               (1.25 mL)

  Acid/oil ratio:
      V_oil / V_acid = 60 / 60 = 1

SKEWERS
  12 mini mozzarella balls
  6 cherry tomatoes, halved          => 12 halves
  12 slices Genoa salami
  12 slices pepperoni
  12 slices soppressata (or extra salami)
  12 fresh basil leaves
  12 skewers, 6 in

METHOD
  1. Whisk vinaigrette.
  2. Add mozzarella + tomato halves. Chill 1 h.
  3. Fold meats into fans.
  4. Thread: mozzarella, tomato, salami, pepperoni,
     soppressata, basil.
  5. Drizzle leftover vinaigrette or serve as dip.

NOTE
  Bread is optional. Add toasted Italian cubes for a full sub.

SCALE
  meats/cheese/basil/skewers: Q_int = S
  tomatoes: halves needed = S, whole tomatoes ~ ceil(S/2)
  vinaigrette volumes: multiply by k

```

### 4. BLT SKEWERS
```
Yield S0 = 8 small skewers
Time     ~ 15 min + bacon cook
k        = S / 8

INGREDIENTS (base)
  16 squares toasted rye or wheat    (1 in squares)
  8 wedges iceberg lettuce
  8 slices cooked bacon
  4 grape tomatoes, halved           => 8 halves
  8 toothpicks or 4 in skewers
  mayonnaise, to serve

ASSEMBLY
  bread, lettuce, folded bacon, tomato half, bread

LUNCH-SIZE VARIANT (S0 = 4 longer skewers)
  3 slices toasted bread, quartered  => 12 pieces
  12 half-slices bacon
  4 cherry tomatoes, halved
  torn lettuce as needed

  Order, then repeat:
    bread, bacon, tomato, lettuce, bread

METHOD
  Thread and serve at once so toast stays crisp.

```

### 5. PB AND J ON A STICK
```
Yield S0 = 4 skewers
Time     ~ 10 min
k        = S / 4

INGREDIENTS (base)
  2 peanut butter and jelly sandwiches
  1 cup seedless grapes              (237 mL)
  1 small banana, sliced
  4 wooden skewers, 5-6 in

CUT
  sandwiches -> 1 in squares

ASSEMBLY
  grape, sandwich square, banana, repeat

SERVE
  immediately (fruit weeps if held long)

SCALE
  sandwiches = (S/4)*2 = S/2
  For odd S, use ceil(S/2) sandwiches

```

### 6. CUBAN SKEWERS (NO BREAD)
```
Yield S0 ~ 16 to 20 mini appetizer skewers
Use midpoint S0 = 18 for scaling
Time     ~ 15 min

INGREDIENTS
  8 oz sliced honey ham              (227 g)
  8 oz block Swiss, cubed            (227 g)
  1 jar (16 oz) whole dill pickles   (454 g)
  mini wooden skewers

DIPPING SAUCE
  1/3 cup Dijon mustard              (80 mL)
  1/3 cup mayonnaise                 (80 mL)
  1/4 tsp onion powder
  pinch salt
  3 Tbsp honey, optional             (45 mL)

  Mix ratio (no honey):
      V_Dijon : V_mayo = 1 : 1

METHOD
  1. Ham into 1 in strips. Cube Swiss.
  2. Trim pickle ends. Cut pickles in halves or thirds.
  3. Thread: pickle, folded ham, Swiss. Repeat if long skewer.
  4. Mix sauce. Serve cold.

```

### Packing notes
- Meat, cheese, and bread can be assembled the night before and chilled.
- Add tomato and lettuce in the morning, or pack them separately so the bread stays dry.
- Break off sharp skewer tips for kids, and keep skewer length shorter than the lunchbox.
- Start and end with bread, and keep pieces a similar size so friction holds the stack.

```

Keep wet items off bread until eat-time:
    pack tomato, lettuce separate
    or assemble morning-of

Kid safety:
    break sharp skewer tips
    length L_skewer <= lunchbox length L_box

Stack stability:
    start and end with bread
    similar bite size so friction holds pieces

Make-ahead window:
    meat+cheese+bread: overnight OK chilled
    tomato/lettuce: add t <= 2 h before serving if possible
```
