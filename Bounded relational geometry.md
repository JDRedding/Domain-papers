**Bounded relational geometry** is the ultrafinitist version of the RDG/SID/PED framework once the *physical ceilings* are imposed.  It is the statement that **relations, modes, interactions, and geometric closures cannot refine indefinitely**. This is the deepest of the bounded‑structure consequences, because RDG is *already* a geometry of relations rather than points. Every relational structure has a **finite maximum representational depth**, fixed by the universe’s information and computation limits. 

Bounded relational geometry tells how far those relations can go.

**Bounded relational geometry** means:

> All relational structures — SID, PED, Q‑moderation, relational closures,  
> relational neighborhoods, relational diagrams—terminate at a finite  
> depth determined by physical information and computation limits.

This replaces:

- infinite relational refinement  
- infinite SID/PED depth  
- infinite closure  
- infinite relational neighborhoods  
- infinite Q‑moderation  
- infinite relational diagrams  

with:

- finite relational refinement  
- finite SID/PED depth  
- finite closure  
- finite relational neighborhoods  
- finite Q‑moderation  
- finite relational diagrams  

This is the ultrafinitist relational geometry.

# **Actual bounded relational geometry**

### **(a) Finite SID depth**  
SID = Structure, Interaction, Dynamics.

Classically, you can refine:

$$
S_1 \to S_2 \to S_3 \to \cdots
$$

Bounded SID says:

$$
S_1 \to S_2 \to \cdots \to S_{N_{\max}}
$$

No deeper structural refinement exists.

### **(b) Finite PED depth**  
PED = Power, Evaluation, Dynamics.

Classically, you can evaluate:

$$
P_1 \to P_2 \to P_3 \to \cdots
$$

Bounded PED says:

$$
P_1 \to P_2 \to \cdots \to P_{N_{\max}}
$$

Evaluation depth is finite.

### **(c) Finite relational closure depth**  
Classical closure:

$$
\text{Cl}(R) = \lim_{n\to\infty} R^{(n)}
$$

Bounded closure:

$$
\text{Cl}_{N_{\max}}(R) = R^{(N_{\max})}
$$

The closure operator stops.

### **(d) Finite relational neighborhood chains**  
Classical relational neighborhoods:

$$
\mathcal{N}_1 \supset \mathcal{N}_2 \supset \mathcal{N}_3 \supset \cdots
$$

Bounded relational neighborhoods:

$$
\mathcal{N}_1 \supset \mathcal{N}_2 \supset \cdots \supset \mathcal{N}_{N_{\max}}
$$

### **(e) Finite relational diagrams**  
Classical diagrams can have infinite depth.

Bounded diagrams have:

- finite nodes  
- finite arrows  
- finite compositional depth  
- finite relational resolution  

### **(f) Finite Q‑moderation depth**  
The Q‑slice cannot refine indefinitely.

It has a maximum representational depth.
