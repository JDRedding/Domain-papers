# **Exceptional Propagation Chain**

The exceptional series is organized by successive hinges that alternate between interaction-dominant constructions (multiplication laws and category-changing extensions) and evaluation-dominant ones (preservation of polynomial invariants). $\mathrm{G}_2$ and $\mathrm{F}_4$ arise from multiplicative data on the octonions and the Albert algebra; $\mathrm{E}_6$ and $\mathrm{E}_7$ arise from the preservation of a cubic form on a 27-dimensional space and a quartic form on a 56-dimensional space, respectively, with $\mathrm{E}_6$ marking the purest evaluation transition; and the passage from $\mathrm{E}_8$ to the Monster reintroduces an interaction-type hinge via an affine extension and $\mathbb{Z}_2$-orbifold of a lattice vertex operator algebra, yielding the Moonshine module. These successive defining data, unified in part by the Freudenthal–Tits magic square, thereby produce the observed hierarchy of relational propagation.

From the relational viewpoint, the exceptional structures are organized by a succession of hinges:

- **G₂** — hinge: octonion multiplication  
- **F₄** — hinge: Jordan product  
- **E₆** — hinge: cubic invariant  
- **E₇** — hinge: quartic invariant / Freudenthal structure  
- **E₈** — hinge: affine extension / VOA lift  
- **Monster** — hinge: $\mathbb{Z}_2$ orbifold twist  

A systematic pattern is thereby discernible. The transitions $\mathrm{G}_2 \to \mathrm{F}_4$ are primarily interaction-dominant, being grounded in multiplication laws. The transitions $\mathrm{E}_6 \to \mathrm{E}_7$ are primarily evaluation-dominant, being grounded in the preservation of invariants. The transitions $\mathrm{E}_8 \to$ Monster reintroduce interaction-dominant hinges through category-changing extensions that convert finite geometric structures into infinite propagating systems. This hierarchy accurately reflects the classical successive constructions of the exceptional series, and the interaction/evaluation distinction cleanly isolates the structural transition at $\mathrm{E}_6$.

Standard realizations proceed exactly as these hinges indicate. $\mathrm{G}_2$ is the automorphism group of the octonion algebra $\mathbb{O}$, so its Lie algebra is the derivation algebra $\mathfrak{der}(\mathbb{O})$ generated by the multiplication law. $\mathrm{F}_4$ is the automorphism group of the 27-dimensional Albert algebra $J = H_3(\mathbb{O})$ (3\times3 Hermitian octonion matrices under the Jordan product $X\circ Y = \frac12(XY+YX)$); again the defining data are multiplicative. The reduced structure group of the cubic norm (determinant) on the same 27-dimensional space is a form of $\mathrm{E}_6$; it preserves the cubic form

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix} a & C & \overline{B} \\ \overline{C} & b & A \\ B & \overline{A} & c \end{pmatrix} = abc - aN(A) - bN(B) - cN(C) + {Re}(ABC)
$$

but need not preserve the Jordan product. Its stabilizer of the unit is precisely $\mathrm{F}_4$, recovering the inclusion. The 56-dimensional minuscule representation of $\mathrm{E}_7$ carries a Freudenthal triple system structure whose quartic form (together with a symplectic form) is preserved by $\mathrm{E}_7$; the construction is again evaluation-dominant. These steps are unified by the Freudenthal–Tits magic square, which produces the series from pairs of composition algebras (or equivalently from a Jordan algebra and an alternative algebra) and isolates $\mathrm{G}_2$ as the residual automorphism group of the octonions.

### Contrast with neighboring hinges

| Group | Primary hinge | Character of hinge | Propagation type |
|-------|---------------|--------------------|------------------|
| $\mathrm{G}_2$ | Octonion multiplication | Interaction-dominant (product law) | Generates derivations |
| $\mathrm{F}_4$ | Jordan product on the Albert algebra | Interaction-dominant (algebra automorphism) | Preserves multiplication |
| **$\mathrm{E}_6$** | **Cubic determinant** | **Evaluation-dominant (invariant preservation)** | **Filters linear maps** |
| $\mathrm{E}_7$ | Quartic Freudenthal form | Evaluation-dominant | Constrains the 56-dimensional space |
| $\mathrm{E}_8$ | Affine extension / VOA lift | Interaction-dominant (category change) | Produces infinite-dimensional modes |

The passage from $\mathrm{E}_8$ to the Monster reintroduces an interaction-type hinge precisely by changing category. One route is the affine extension of the $\mathrm{E}_8$ root lattice, whose associated lattice VOA admits a $\mathbb{Z}_2$-orbifold that yields (after suitable choice of lattice and twist) the Moonshine module $V^\natural$. The Monster is then recovered as ${Aut}(V^\natural)$. McKay’s observation relating the extended $\mathrm{E}_8$ diagram to pairs of 2A-involutions, together with the subsequent “moonshine path” constructions that realize those involutions via conformal vectors inside subVOAs of $V^\natural$, make the same transition explicit: a finite geometric/lattice structure is lifted to an infinite-dimensional graded algebra whose symmetry group is sporadic.

$\mathrm{E}_6$ therefore occupies the transitional position between the multiplication-based hinges ($\mathrm{G}_2$, $\mathrm{F}_4$) and the purely invariant-based hinges ($\mathrm{E}_6$, $\mathrm{E}_7$), and is especially sharp as the purest evaluation hinge. A single irreducible cubic condition on a 27-dimensional space is rich enough to force the 78-dimensional Lie algebra (by taking the stabilizer in $\mathfrak{gl}(27)$ and verifying the root system, or by adjoining the structure group action to the derivations of the Albert algebra). The same cubic controls the geometry of the bioctonion plane $\mathbb{OP}^2$ and the collineation group of the Cayley plane. The subsequent quartic on the 56-space is a further evaluation constraint, while the $\mathrm{E}_8$–Monster step returns to generative, multiplicative structure only after the category change to VOAs has been performed. The alternation—interaction ($\mathrm{G}_2$, $\mathrm{F}_4$), evaluation ($\mathrm{E}_6$, $\mathrm{E}_7$), interaction via extension ($\mathrm{E}_8 \to$ Monster)—is therefore a direct consequence of the way the successive defining data are chosen. This hierarchy constitutes a significant consequence of viewing the exceptional series through the lens of relational propagation.
