## 🔧 PSI/GPM Reality Check for Property Maintenance
Most apartment maintenance teams end up in one of three practical bands:

### 1. **Light/General Exterior (Electric)**
- **PSI:** 1,600–2,000  
- **GPM:** 1.2–1.5  
- **Use:** patios, balconies, light mildew, breezeways  
- **Operator notes:**  
  - Use **25° or 40°** tips for siding.  
  - Pre-treat mildew with sodium hypochlorite mix (0.5–1%).  
  - Electric units struggle with large concrete pads due to low GPM.

### 2. **Medium/Heavy Concrete (Gas)**
- **PSI:** 2,700–3,200  
- **GPM:** 2.3–2.7  
- **Use:** sidewalks, dumpster pads, parking stops, breezeways  
- **Operator notes:**  
  - GPM matters more than PSI for speed.  
  - Pair with a **surface cleaner** (14–20") for efficiency.  
  - Keep 15° tip for stubborn stains; avoid 0° except for spot work.

### 3. **Grease/Bio/Dumpster Pads (Hot Water)**
- **PSI:** 2,000–3,000  
- **GPM:** 2.5–4.0  
- **Use:** grease, oil, sticky dumpster residue, mold colonies  
- **Operator notes:**  
  - Heat (140–200°F) dramatically reduces chemical use.  
  - Ideal for properties with recurring dumpster pad complaints.

## 🧪 Detergent & Chemical Quick Guide
- **Mold/mildew:** Sodium hypochlorite (bleach) + surfactant  
- **Grease/oil:** Degreaser (alkaline) + hot water if available  
- **Dumpster pads:** Enzyme cleaners or strong degreasers  
- **General siding:** Mild detergent + 40° tip, low pressure  

Use chemical injectors only with **soap nozzle** (low pressure). For stronger mixes, apply with a pump sprayer instead.

## 📋 Work-Order Style Notes (Apartment Maintenance)
- **Sidewalk cleaning:**  
  - Pre-treat stains → surface cleaner → edge rinse  
- **Dumpster pad:**  
  - Degreaser → dwell 5–10 min → hot water rinse  
- **Breezeways:**  
  - Sweep debris → 25° tip → avoid door thresholds  
- **Siding:**  
  - 40° tip → 12–18" distance → avoid upward spray under siding  
- **Make-ready patios:**  
  - Quick rinse → spot clean → avoid overspray into units
