# Relational Toroidal Geometry
## An RDG/MFE Formulation of Toroidal Differential Geometry

- The usual toroidal coordinates (η, θ, φ) are replaced by relational variables:
  - E = R/C: efficiency
  - S = Δα: oriented step
  - F = Φ_c: threaded flux / phase
- These are treated as relational facts, not literal spatial coordinates.
- The geometry is generated by a single accessibility function:
  - D(E,S) = cosh E - cos S
- The admissible manifold is where D(E,S) > 0, and the null case D = 0 marks a boundary of no accessibility.

---

## Abstract

Toroidal differential geometry is reformulated within the framework of Relational Dynamic Geometry (RDG) and the Momentum Flux Engine (MFE). This is a theoretical re-derivation of toroidal geometry as a relational, finite-state manifold whose geometry is generated by accessibility and whose evolution is governed by an MFE-like dynamical engine. The system preserves the classical toroidal geometry’s algebraic structure while reinterpreting it as emergent from relational interactions. Accessibility and metric structure arise from the interaction function D, not from a pre-existing geometric stage. The traditional toroidal variables are reinterpreted as relational quantities rather than coordinates of an embedded point in Euclidean space.

The classical toroidal labels

$$
(\eta,\theta,\phi)
$$

are replaced by

$$
(E,S,F),
$$

where

$$
E=\frac{R}{C},
\qquad
S=\Delta\alpha,
\qquad
F=\Phi_c.
$$

Geometry is generated through a relational accessibility function. This paper reformulates toroidal differential geometry as a relational system rather than a classical coordinate-based geometry

$$
D(E,S)=\cosh E-\cos S,
$$

which produces metric structure, accessibility, and the complete differential operator algebra. The resulting formulation preserves the operator algebra of the classical toroidal chart while replacing its Euclidean interpretation with a finite relational state space.

Core framework:
- RDG (Relational Dynamic Geometry): defines the geometry through the relation
  - (E, S, F) → D(E,S) → (h, k) → Γ
- MFE (Momentum Flux Engine): governs the evolution of the state
  - (E, S, F) → (E', S', F')

Key mathematical content:
- Scale factors:
  - h = a / D
  - k = a sinh E / D
- Accessibility tensor Γ is built from these
- Differential operators are derived from the same relational structure:
  - gradient
  - divergence
  - curl
  - scalar Laplacian

$$

$$

Interaction generates structure.

Geometry is therefore viewed as an emergent consequence of underlying relational interactions rather than as a pre-existing stage upon which interactions occur.

The relational toroidal chart defines accessibility and differential structure but does not itself specify state evolution.

### Appendix B — Null-Closure Boundary

The accessibility generator vanishes when

$$
D(E,S)=0.
$$

Since

$$
\cosh E\ge1,
\qquad
\cos S\le1,
$$

this occurs only at

$$
E=0,
\qquad
S\in2\pi\mathbb Z.
$$

Thus

$$
\partial\mathcal S=
\{
E=0,
\;
S\in2\pi\mathbb Z
\}.
$$

This boundary corresponds to

```text
No realized closure.
No effective update.
No accessibility.
No geometry.
```

The singularity is retained as a meaningful relational boundary.
