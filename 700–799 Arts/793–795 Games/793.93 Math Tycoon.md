# **📘 Math Tycoon**
Complete Rule Set (Full System Specification)

## **1. Core Objective**
Build the largest mathematical empire by generating:
**Cash**, 
**Math Points (MP)**, and 
**Knowledge**, 
unlocking deeper math domains, 
automating production, and 
achieving **Prestige Paradigm Shifts**.

---

# **2. Primary Resources**
### **Cash**  
Used for buildings, land, staff, upgrades.

### **Math Points (MP)**  
Earned by solving problems. Used for research, mastery, and prestige.

### **Knowledge**  
Passive resource generated by advanced staff and buildings. Unlocks new domains and advanced mechanics.

---

# **3. Starting State**
- $100 Cash  
- 1 **Arithmetic Desk**  
- Access to **addition/subtraction** only  
- No staff  
- No automation  

---

# **4. Core Gameplay Loop**
1. Solve problems at active stations → earn Cash + MP.  
2. Spend Cash to expand buildings and hire staff.  
3. Spend MP to unlock research, domains, and automation.  
4. Increase **Mastery Scores** to unlock new buildings.  
5. Prestige to reset progress and unlock new eras.

---

# **5. Mastery System (Domain Progression Gate)**
Each math domain has a **Mastery Score** (0–100).  
Solving problems increases mastery; harder problems increase it faster.

| Domain | Unlock Requirement | Mastery Source |
|-------|--------------------|----------------|
| Arithmetic | Start | Solve +, −, ×, ÷ |
| Algebra | Arithmetic Mastery 40 | Solve equations/inequalities |
| Geometry | Algebra Mastery 50 | Solve area/volume/trig |
| Statistics | Geometry Mastery 60 | Solve probability/data |
| Calculus | Statistics Mastery 70 | Solve derivatives/integrals |
| Number Theory | Prestige 1 | Solve primes/modular arithmetic |

Mastery prevents brute‑forcing MP to skip learning.

Can expand this with a **Mastery Dashboard**.

# **6. Buildings & Production**

| Building | Unlock | Base Production | Problem Types |
|---------|--------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Arithmetic Desk** | Start | Low Cash + MP | +, −, ×, ÷ |
| **Algebra Lab** | 40 Arithmetic Mastery | Medium | Equations, inequalities |
| **Geometry Studio** | 50 Algebra Mastery | Medium‑High | Area, volume, trig |
| **Statistics Bureau** | 60 Geometry Mastery | High | Mean, probability, data |
| **Calculus Engine** | 70 Statistics Mastery | Very High | Derivatives, integrals |
| **Number Theory Vault** | Prestige 1 | Extreme | Primes, modular arithmetic |

### **Building Upgrades**
- **Speed Upgrade** — faster problem generation  
- **Multiplier Upgrade** — more Cash/MP per correct answer  
- **Automation Upgrade** — staff solve problems  
- **Difficulty Tier** — harder problems = bigger rewards

# **7. Building Synergy System**
Buildings influence each other, creating strategic layouts.

- **Arithmetic → Algebra**: boosts equation spawn rate  
- **Geometry → Statistics**: increases statistical accuracy multiplier  
- **Calculus → Number Theory**: increases prime‑search speed  
- **Statistics → All**: increases global MP multiplier  

Can expand this with a **Building Synergy Map**.

---

# **8. Problem Generation & Solving Rules**
### **Problem Flow**
- Problems spawn on a timer or on demand.  
- Player or staff can solve them.

### **Correct Answer**
- Full Cash + MP reward  
- Small temporary speed boost  
- Contributes to mastery  
- Adds to combo multiplier

### **Wrong Answer**
- Small Cash penalty  
- Temporary slowdown  
- Combo reset

### **Combo System**
- Consecutive correct answers increase a temporary multiplier.  
- Resets on mistake.

### **Time Pressure (Optional)**
- Faster answers = higher multiplier.

### **Show Work Mode**
- Multi‑step problems  
- +50–200% MP reward  
- Required for advanced research

# **9. Staff System**

| Staff Type | Cost | Strength | Weakness |
|------------|------|----------|----------|
| **Intern** | Cheap | Basic arithmetic | Slow |
| **Tutor** | Medium | Algebra/Geometry | No advanced domains |
| **Professor** | Expensive | Calculus/Statistics | High salary |

- **Spatial Thinker** — geometry speed boost  
- **Data Whisperer** — statistics accuracy bonus  
- **Analytical Beast** — calculus throughput multiplier  
- **Chaos Tamer** — reduces penalty from wrong answers

# **14. Economy Balance Principles**
- Early game: player solves most problems  
- Mid game: staff + player  
- Late game: mostly automated  
- Harder math always pays more  
- Automation caps prevent runaway inflation  
- Staff efficiency decays unless players occasionally solve problems  
- Knowledge upkeep required for advanced domains
