# Photographers and Computer Artists

Photography has produced some of the most influential artists in history, while computer art has evolved into a major global creative force, blending technology and imagination. Photographers shaped visual storytelling, documentary truth, and artistic expression, leaving a legacy that still influences media and culture. Computer artists have expanded the boundaries of art through digital tools, enabling surrealism, interactivity, and global collaboration, while also pioneering new markets like NFTs.

## Photographers

- **Cindy Sherman** — American conceptual artist and photographer, known for her self-portraits exploring identity, representation, and media imagery. Her work is iconic in contemporary art and has appeared on lists of the most expensive photographs ever sold. http://cindysherman.com/
- **Ansel Adams** — Legendary American landscape photographer, famous for his black-and-white images of the American West, especially Yosemite. He co-founded Group f/64 and developed the “zone system” for photographic exposure. https://www.anseladams.com/
- **Dorothea Lange** — Renowned for her powerful documentary photography, especially during the Great Depression, capturing the human condition with empathy and clarity. https://www.dorothea-lange.com/
- **Henri Cartier-Bresson** — French photographer celebrated for his “decisive moment” philosophy, influencing generations of photojournalists. https://www.henricartierbresson.org/en/
- **Steve McCurry** — Known for his striking portrait of “Afghan Girl,” which became a global symbol of humanitarian awareness.

## Computer Art Artists

- **Beeple (Mike Winkelmann)** — American digital artist whose *Everydays: The First 5000 Days* NFT sold for $69.3 million at Christie’s, setting a record for digital art. His work blends 3D modeling, animation, and photobashing, often with political and dystopian themes. https://www.beeple-crap.com/
- **Lulu Chen** — Digital artist from San Francisco/Toronto, creating detailed children’s book illustrations and character designs using Adobe Photoshop and Corel Painter, often with traditional media influences. https://www.behance.net/polly51905
- **Alexis Franklin** — Texas-based digital painter and videographer producing surreal, oil-on-canvas-inspired works using Wacom tablets and time-lapse video documentation. https://www.alexisfranklinart.com/
- **Desmond Paul Henry** — Early pioneer of computer art, creating electromechanical drawing machines in the 1960s that generated original artworks. https://desmondhenry.com/
- **A. Michael Noll** — Early computer artist who programmed patterns inspired by Piet Mondrian and Bridget Riley, influencing generative art. http://noll.uscannenberg.org/ComputerArt.htm | https://annenberg.usc.edu/faculty/michael-noll
