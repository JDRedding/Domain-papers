# Decorative Arts
The 740-749 range in the classification system covers the study and practice of decorative arts, including crafts, design, and applied art forms.

Overview of 740-749
The system organizes knowledge into numbered classes. Class 700 deals with Arts and Recreation, and within this, the 740s specifically focus on decorative arts, which are arts concerned with design and aesthetics in objects meant for everyday use.

Works in this range often combine practical craft techniques with aesthetic principles, reflecting the tradition of decorative arts as applied artistry. Historical and stylistic aspects are included; for example, Victorian furniture, Art Deco ornamentation, Japanese lacquerware, or Renaissance metalwork.

Use these numbers to classify things for easy reference, especially instructional or scholarly texts on various art forms.

In summary, if you are looking for books, manuals, or research on crafts, applied arts, and decorative design, the 740-749 range provides a structured system to locate these materials in a library catalog.

## 740 — Drawing & Decorative Arts (General)
This section includes general works on decorative arts, art techniques, and aesthetics across multiple media. It may cover the philosophy, history, and principles of decorative styles.
## 741 — Drawing & Drawings
Focuses on drawing techniques, illustration, sketches, and line work. It often includes instructional manuals, sketchbooks, and guides for artistic drawing.

## 742 — Painting
Includes materials and techniques related to painting, such as murals, easel painting, watercolors, and mixed media painting.

## 743 — Prints
Covers printmaking methods such as etching, engraving, woodcuts, lithography, and the study of printed visual images.

## 744 — Specific Media in Graphic Arts
Encompasses engraving, calligraphy, and graphic methods not covered under printmaking.

## 745 — Decorative Arts and Ornamentation
Focused on applied arts, including textiles, furniture decoration, interior ornaments, and jewelry design.

## 746 — Textiles
Includes weaving, embroidery, lace-making, knitting, and other forms of fabric art.

## 747 — Leather, Leatherwork, and Saddle-making
Covers gloving, saddlery, leathercraft, and techniques of ornamental and functional leatherwork.

## 748 — Woodwork and Woodcraft
Includes carpentry, furniture making, wood carving, and marquetry.

## 749 — Metalwork
Encompasses jewelry, silverware, metal crafts, and decorative or functional metal objects.
