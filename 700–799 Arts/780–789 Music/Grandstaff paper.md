# Grandstaff paper 
Grandstaff paper resources allow musicians, teachers, and students to conveniently create, write, and print grand staff music sheets without needing specialized software.

## Features
- Clefs: Treble and bass (grand staff)
- Barlines: Optional
- Orientation: Portrait or landscape
- Formats: PDF for easy printing; some editors offer PNG/SVG export
- Usage: Composing, arranging, practicing piano, or educational exercises
