**Quantum Information and Spacetime Geometric Objects**

Information provides coordinates, stability defines the manifold, and entanglement builds distance. Spacetime is the user interface to a deeper layer of information processing. We perceive geometry; the universe computes stability. Geometry is the stable shape of information. The universe is made of information, and it is solving for shape.

- The metric tensor $g_{ij}$ or $I_{ij}$ supplies distances, angles, and volumes on the information manifold.  
- Renormalization-group / coarse-graining flow is a vector field on that manifold that drives the system toward an infrared (IR) fixed point.  
- Entanglement entropy / mutual information is used as a proxy for geodesic distance (ER = EPR): higher entanglement $\leftrightarrow$ shorter proper distance.  
- Curvature of the resulting metric is identified with gravitational tidal forces (gravity as the energetic cost of deforming the entanglement pattern).

**1. Information creates a metric**  
A state of information — a probability distribution or a quantum state — defines distance by distinguishability.

Classical (Fisher information metric):

$$
I_{ij}(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_\theta\bigl[(\partial_i\log p)(\partial_j\log p)\bigr]
$$

Distance is statistical distinguishability. A large change in information is a large distance.

Quantum (Fubini–Study metric):

$$
ds^2_{FS}=4\bigl(\langle d\psi|d\psi\rangle-|\langle\psi|d\psi\rangle|^2\bigr)
$$

Distance is quantum distinguishability. Geometry emerges from how information responds.

The most fundamental distance is not spatial. It is how much information differs.

**2. Stability defines a manifold**  
Only stable information survives as persistent shape.

Raw information (high entropy, chaotic UV fluctuations)  
→ renormalization / coarse-graining (energy minimization)  
→ equilibrium at an IR fixed point (stable manifold)

Stability filters the quantum soup. What remains is persistent, resistant to perturbation, and ready to be perceived as geometry. Stability is a local minimum in the information landscape.

**3. Entanglement builds spacetime**  
Geometry is the macroscopic map of entanglement.

ER = EPR: entanglement ↔ geometric bridge.  
More entanglement → shorter distance.  
Entanglement density → metric.  
Deforming entanglement costs energy → gravity as tension.

Holographically, bulk geometry is encoded in boundary entanglement. Spacetime is not fundamental. It is the collective shape of quantum correlations.

**Fisher information metric (classical)**

$$
I_{ij}(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_\theta\bigl[(\partial_i\log p)(\partial_j\log p)\bigr]
$$

or equivalently

$$
g_{ij}(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_\theta\left[\frac{\partial\log p(x|\theta)}{\partial\theta^i}\frac{\partial\log p(x|\theta)}{\partial\theta^j}\right]
$$

Notation  
- $p(x|\theta)$: probability density of data $x$ given parameters $\theta$  
- $\theta=(\theta^i)$: parameter vector (coordinates on the statistical manifold)  
- $\partial_i=\partial/\partial\theta^i$  
- $\mathbb{E}_\theta$: expectation with respect to $p(x|\theta)$  
- $I_{ij}$ or $g_{ij}$: Fisher information matrix (Riemannian metric)

The infinitesimal distance is then

$$
ds^2=g_{ij}(\theta)\,d\theta^i\,d\theta^j
$$

**Fubini–Study metric (quantum)**

$$
ds^2_{FS}=4\bigl(\langle d\psi|d\psi\rangle-|\langle\psi|d\psi\rangle|^2\bigr)
$$

Notation  
- $|\psi\rangle$: normalized quantum state (ray in projective Hilbert space)  
- $|d\psi\rangle$: infinitesimal variation of the state  
- $\langle\cdot|\cdot\rangle$: Hilbert-space inner product  
- The factor of 4 is conventional so that the metric on the Bloch sphere has radius 1

This is the unique (up to scale) unitarily invariant Riemannian metric on the space of pure states.
