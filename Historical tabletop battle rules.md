# Historical tabletop battles
A fast, miniature-agnostic ruleset for historical tabletop battles using simple activations, opposed rolls, morale, and streamlined combat in the spirit of OPR’s accessible design philosophy.

## Core Concepts
- **Miniature-agnostic:** Use any historical figures—ancients, medieval, musket era, or modern.
- **Units:** A unit is a group of 5–20 infantry, 3–10 cavalry, or 1–3 vehicles/artillery pieces.
- **Stats:** Each unit has Quality (Q), Defense (D), Movement (M), Weapons, and Special Rules.
- **Dice:** All tests use D6.

## Turn Structure
Players alternate activations.

On your activation, choose one unit and perform **two actions** (repeat or mix):
- Move
- Shoot
- Charge
- Rally
- Aim/Brace (shooting or defense bonus)

After both players have activated all units, the round ends.

## Movement
- **Infantry:** M 6"
- **Cavalry:** M 10"
- **Vehicles:** M 8–12", depending on type
- Terrain modifies movement (½ speed in rough ground, no cavalry in dense woods, and so on).

## Shooting
1. Choose a target in line of sight.
2. Roll Q tests for each attack.
3. The target rolls D tests to cancel hits.
4. Each uncancelled hit = 1 casualty.

**Range bands (example):**
- Short: +1 to hit
- Long: −1 to hit
- Artillery uses templates or D3 hits.

## Melee
- The charging unit strikes first.
- Both sides roll attacks using Q tests.
- The defender rolls D tests.
- Casualties are applied simultaneously after the first strike.

## Morale
When a unit takes 25% casualties in a round, test Q:
- **Pass:** Continue fighting.
- **Fail:** Become Shaken (−1 to all rolls).
- If already Shaken and it fails again, the unit **Routs** and flees off the table.

## Special Rules (Pick as Needed)
- **Shieldwall:** +1 D vs melee.
- **Volley Fire:** Reroll 1s when stationary.
- **Skirmishers:** Ignore movement penalties; −1 to be hit at long range.
- **Pike Formation:** Enemy cavalry cannot charge the front arc.
- **Machine Gun:** 6 attacks; cannot move and fire.
- **Armor (Vehicles):** Reduce incoming hits by 1 (minimum 1).

## Command & Control (Optional)
Assign a Commander with a 12" aura:
- Units in the aura may reroll failed morale.
- Spend 1 command point per round to give a unit a third action.

## Victory Conditions
Choose one:
- **Break the Enemy:** The opponent has 50% of their units routed or destroyed.
- **Hold Objectives:** Control more objectives at the end of round 5.
- **Historical Scenario:** Recreate a real battle with asymmetric goals.
