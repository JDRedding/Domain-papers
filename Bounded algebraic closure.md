**Bounded algebraic closure** is the ultrafinitist statement that *no algebraic structure can support an infinite tower of field extensions*, because every extension requires representational depth, information, and computation — all of which are physically finite.

**Bounded algebraic closure** means:

> Algebraic extension towers terminate at a finite depth  
> determined by physical information and computation limits.

This replaces:

- infinite algebraic closures  
- infinite splitting fields  
- infinite extension towers  
- infinite polynomial factorization  
- infinite Galois lattices  

with:

- finite algebraic universes  
- finite extension depth  
- finite splitting fields  
- finite factorization depth  
- finite Galois structures  

This is the ultrafinitist algebraic universe.

## **Classical algebraic closure assumptions** 
: **infinite extension towers**

In classical mathematics, you can build an infinite chain:

$$
K \subset K(\alpha_1) \subset K(\alpha_1,\alpha_2) \subset \cdots
$$

And ultimately reach the algebraic closure:

$$
\overline{K} = \text{all roots of all polynomials over } K
$$

This requires:

- infinite representational depth  
- infinite precision  
- infinite computation  
- infinite storage  
- infinite divisibility  
- infinite polynomial factorization  

## **Physical ceilings forcings**
: **Finite extension depth**

Every algebraic extension requires:

- storing coefficients  
- representing new algebraic numbers  
- factoring polynomials  
- computing minimal polynomials  
- performing field operations  
- maintaining precision  
- maintaining divisibility depth  

All of these consume:

- **information capacity**  
- **operations**  
- **energy**  
- **time**  
- **precision resolution**

And these are capped by:

- **Bekenstein bound**  
- **Holographic principle**  
- **Cosmological information limit**  
- **Lloyd bound**  
- **Planck cutoffs**  

Therefore:

> **You cannot build an infinite tower of algebraic extensions.  
> The tower must terminate at a finite depth.**

Call this depth $N_{\max}$.

## **Acyual bounded algebraic closure**

Instead of:

$$
K \subset K_1 \subset K_2 \subset \cdots \subset K_\infty
$$

Get:

$$
K \subset K_1 \subset K_2 \subset \cdots \subset K_{N_{\max}}
$$

Where:

- $N_{\max}$ is finite  
- determined by physical ceilings  
- cannot be exceeded by any computation or representation  

This is the **finite algebraic universe**.

## **Consequences**

---

: **Algebraic closure does not exist**
It is a **finite algebraic structure**.

Classically:

$$
\overline{K}
$$

is a field containing all algebraic numbers over $K$.

Under bounded closure:

$$
\overline{K}_{N_{\max}}
$$

is a **finite set** of algebraic numbers reachable within the representational depth limit.

This is not a field.  

---

: **Polynomial factorization is finite**
Classically, every polynomial can be factored in $\overline{K}$.

Bounded closure says:

- only polynomials whose factorization depth ≤ $N_{\max}$ can be fully resolved  
- deeper factorizations cannot be represented  
- some polynomials become *irreducible by physical limit*, not by algebraic property

---

**Galois theory becomes finite**
The entire Galois lattice becomes **finite**.

Classical Galois theory assumes:

- infinite extension towers  
- infinite splitting fields  
- infinite automorphism groups  

Bounded closure replaces this with:

- finite splitting fields  
- finite extension depth  
- finite automorphism groups  
- finite Galois correspondences  

---

**Algebraic numbers become finite‑precision intervals**
No algebraic number has infinite precision.

Every algebraic number $\alpha$ is represented as:

$$
\alpha_{\text{realizable}} = [\alpha^-_N,\; \alpha^+_N]
$$

with:

- finite precision  
- finite representational depth  
- finite divisibility depth  
- finite computational depth
