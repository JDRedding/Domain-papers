# Shelf‑mounting 

## 🧱 **SYSTEM OVERVIEW**
```
KITCHEN WALL (studs behind drywall)
||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||

[ BACKER BOARD ]
====================================================
|  HOOK PLATES (male) mounted solidly to board     |
|  using multiple screws into the board            |
====================================================

                ↓ SHELF DROPS DOWN

----------------------------------------------------
|  SLOT PLATES (female) mounted to shelf underside |
----------------------------------------------------

                ↓ LOCKING MECHANISM

ANTI‑LIFT LOCK (screw + washer OR metal tab)
```

## ⚙️ **ENGAGEMENT (How They Latch)**
```
SIDE VIEW

Shelf underside
------------------------------------
| SLOT PLATE                       |
------------------------------------
        ↓ drops onto hooks

Backer board
====================================
| HOOK PLATE                       |
====================================
```

When the shelf drops down, the hooks fully seat into the slots.

---

## 🔒 **ANTI‑LIFT LOCK OPTIONS**

### **1. Screw + Washer (your preferred method)**
```
TOP VIEW OF LOCK

Shelf underside
------------------------------------
| SLOT PLATE                       |
------------------------------------
   ^ top of slot

Washer overlaps slot edge:
      ________
     |        |
     | washer |
     |________|
        ↑ screw through washer
        into backer board
```

Effect:
- Washer covers the slot opening  
- Screw anchors into the board  
- Shelf cannot lift upward  

This is the simplest and strongest.

---

### **2. Metal Retainer Tab**
```
SIDE VIEW

Shelf underside
------------------------------------
| SLOT PLATE                       |
------------------------------------
        ↓ seated

Backer board
====================================
| HOOK PLATE                       |
====================================

Retainer tab:
   [L] swings down over top edge
```

---

### **3. Cross‑Pin (bolt or nail)**
```
FRONT VIEW

[ SLOT PLATE ]  O  [ HOOK PLATE ]
                 ↑ cross‑pin
```
