---

## Core Idea

An SDI is the persistent relational organization maintained by an evolving process.

A SID is the structural representation of that organization.

Thus:

```text
SDI
=
dynamic reality

SID
=
structural image of that reality
```

The SDI exists on the generator side of the hinge.

The SID exists on the representation side.

---

## Invariant Interaction Principle

A stabilized dynamic invariant projects into a SID description by preserving the relational organization that remains stable under evolution.

Symbolically:

```text
Generator
   ↓
Phase Geometry
   ↓
SDI
   ↓ Ξ
SID
```

where

```text
Ξ
```

is the projection from dynamic organization into structural representation.



## Kuramoto Example

For synchronized oscillators:

$$
\theta_i(t) = \Omega t + \delta_i
$$

the SDI is the maintained phase geometry

$$
\delta_i - \delta_j.
$$

Its SID projection is:

```text
S = locking structure

I = coupling relations

D = coherent phase organization
```

The SID records the invariant organization while omitting most trajectory details.

---

## Hopf Example

A Hopf bifurcation may transform:

```text
SDI_A
   →
SDI_B
```

through generator reorganization.

The SID observed before and after may appear similar at a structural level while the underlying generator changes.

Thus:

```text
SID equivalence
```

does not imply

```text
Generator equivalence.
```
