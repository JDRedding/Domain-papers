# **Norse pantheon**

---

# ⭐ major norse gods 
Æsir

- **Odin** — All‑Father; wisdom, war, death   
- **Thor** — thunder, storms, protection   
- **Frigg** — marriage, motherhood, fate   
- **Baldr** — light, purity, peace  
- **Týr** — law, justice, oaths; one‑handed  
- **Heimdall** — guardian of Bifröst, vigilance 
- **Bragi** — poetry, eloquence  
- **Höðr** — blind god; kills Baldr unwittingly    
- **Víðarr** — silent god; avenger of Odin   
- **Váli** — vengeance; born to avenge Baldr   
- **Forseti** — justice, mediation  
- **Ullr** — archery, skiing, winter  
- **Hermóðr** — messenger; rides to Hel  
- **Hœnir** — uncertain role; companion of Odin (attested but obscure)   

---

# ⭐ major norse goddesses 
Ásynjur

- **Freyja** — love, beauty, war, death; Vanir living among Æsir  
- **Frigg** — queen of the Æsir (listed above)  
- **Sif** — harvest, fertility; golden hair    
- **Iðunn** — keeper of golden apples of youth    
- **Skaði** — winter, mountains, hunting   
- **Nanna** — wife of Baldr; dies of grief   
- **Gefjon** — ploughing, land‑creation  
- **Fulla** — Frigg’s handmaid; secrecy  
- **Sjöfn** — love, affection  
- **Lofn** — forbidden love  
- **Vár** — oaths, agreements  
- **Vör** — awareness, wisdom  
- **Syn** — defense, refusal  
- **Hlín** — protection  
- **Snotra** — virtue, wisdom   
- **Gná** — messenger goddess   

---

# ⭐ vanir gods 
fertility & prosperity

- **Njörðr** — sea, wind, wealth  
- **Freyr** — fertility, prosperity, sunshine; ruler of Álfheim 
- **Freyja** — (listed above; Vanir)  
- **Nerthus** — earth mother; early Germanic deity 
- **Gullveig** — seiðr witch whose burning sparked the Æsir–Vanir war   
- **Kvasir** — wise being created after the war; mead of poetry origin   

---

# ⭐ liminal / ambiguous figures

- **Loki** — trickster, shapeshifter; not strictly Æsir but central   
- **Hel** — queen of the dead; daughter of Loki  
- **Ymir** — primordial giant; source of the world’s creation  
- **Búri** — first god; ancestor of Odin
- **Borr** — father of Odin, Vili, Vé 
- **Sól** — sun goddess 
- **Máni** — moon god   
- **The Norns** — fate‑weavers (Urðr, Verðandi, Skuld)  
- **The Valkyries** — choosers of the slain; battle spirits   

---

# ⭐ giants (jötnar) 
giants (jötnar) & monstrous children of loki

- **Fenrir** — giant wolf; kills Odin at Ragnarök  
- **Jörmungandr** — world serpent; Thor’s nemesis    
- **Skaði** — giantess; goddess (listed above)  
- **Surtr** — fire giant; leads destruction at Ragnarök  
- **Gerd** — giantess; wife of Freyr 
- **Angrboða** — mother of Loki’s monstrous children (Fenrir, Jörmungandr, Hel)   

---

# ⭐ dwarves & elves
(not gods but important)

- **Dwarves** — master smiths; creators of Mjölnir, Gungnir, Draupnir  
- **Álfar** — elves; associated with Freyr and fertility  

---

# ⭐ norse heroes & legendary figures

- **Sigurd** — dragon‑slayer; hero of the Völsung saga  
- **Brynhild** — valkyrie‑heroine of the Völsung saga  
- **Ragnar Lodbrok** — legendary Viking hero  
- **Ivar the Boneless** — son of Ragnar; strategist  
- **Beowulf** — Geatish hero; fights Grendel (not strictly Norse but Germanic)  
- **Hervor** — warrior‑maiden; Tyrfing saga  
- **Egill Skallagrímsson** — warrior‑poet
