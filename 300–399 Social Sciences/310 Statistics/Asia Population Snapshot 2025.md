# Asia Population Snapshot 

As of 2025, Asia’s population is approximately **4.84 billion**, or nearly **59%** of the world total.

Asia remains the world’s most populous and one of its most demographically dynamic continents. Population is heavily concentrated in a few countries—especially India and China—which shapes the region’s economic, social, and geographic patterns.

For country-level tables, see [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Asian_countries_by_population) and sites such as [Worldostats](https://worldostats.com/), [StatisticsTimes](https://statisticstimes.com/demographics/asia-population.php), and [Worldometer](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/asia-population/).

## Current Population

- Estimated population of Asia: **4,842,790,536** as of 7 October 2025, based on the latest United Nations data ([StatisticsTimes](https://statisticstimes.com/demographics/asia-population.php)).
- Share of world population: about **58.74%**, making Asia the most populous continent by a wide margin ([Worldometer](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/asia-population/)).

UN mid-year 2025 figures commonly cited from *World Population Prospects 2024* put Asia at about **4.835 billion**.

## Population Distribution

- **India** is the most populous country in Asia and the world, at about **1.46 billion**.
- **China** is second, at about **1.41 billion**.
- Together, India and China account for nearly **60%** of Asia’s population ([StatisticsTimes](https://statisticstimes.com/demographics/asian-countries-by-population.php); [Worldostats](https://worldostats.com/)).
- Other major contributors include **Indonesia**, **Pakistan**, and **Bangladesh**.

## Density and Growth

- Population density: about **156 people per km²** ([Worldometer](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/asia-population/)).
- Regional shares of Asia’s population:
  - Southern Asia: about **43%**
  - Eastern Asia: about **34.2%**
- Growth rate in 2025: about **0.58%**—lower than Africa’s, but still a large absolute increase ([StatisticsTimes](https://statisticstimes.com/demographics/asia-population.php)).

## Historical Context and Projections

- Asia’s population rose from under **1 billion** in 1900 to nearly **5 billion** in 2025, with the steepest growth after 1950 as health and food production improved ([Worldostats](https://worldostats.com/)).
- UN-based projections suggest Asia may peak near **5.29 billion around 2054** ([StatisticsTimes](https://statisticstimes.com/demographics/asia-population.php)).
