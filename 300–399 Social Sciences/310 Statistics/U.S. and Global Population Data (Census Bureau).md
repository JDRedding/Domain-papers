# U.S. and Global Population Data (Census Bureau)

U.S. Census Bureau resources cover age, sex, race, Hispanic origin, migration, and projections for the United States, with selected international comparisons.

## United States Population Overview

The Census Bureau publishes population statistics from the **decennial census**, the **American Community Survey (ACS)**, the **Current Population Survey (CPS)**, and annual **population estimates**.

Coverage includes the nation, states, counties, cities, and metropolitan and micropolitan areas. Tables typically break out:

- Age and sex  
- Race and Hispanic origin  
- Ancestry and language use  
- Veteran status  

The [Population Projections Program](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/popproj.html) publishes projections of the resident population by age, sex, race, and Hispanic origin for planning and research.

Main portals: [census.gov](https://www.census.gov) and [data.census.gov](https://data.census.gov).

## Key U.S. Demographic Insights

### Older adults

The Census report *[An Aging World: 2025](https://www.census.gov/library/publications/2026/demo/P95-26-1.html)* finds that the global share of people **age 65 and older** is projected to nearly double, from **10.5% in 2025 to 19.6% in 2060**. Between 2020 and 2025, that group surpassed the population **age 5 and under** for the first time on record.

The United States is ageing too, but more slowly than many other countries:

- **2025:** 18.9% age 65+ (48th oldest of 227 countries)  
- **2060:** 23.4% age 65+ (projected rank: 110th)  

### Migration

County-to-county migration flows by age, sex, race, and Hispanic origin are available from **ACS 5-year estimates**.

### Income and poverty (calendar year 2025)

Released 15 September 2026 ([Income, Poverty and Health Insurance Coverage in the United States: 2025](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/income-poverty-health-insurance-coverage.html)):

| Measure | 2025 |
| --- | ---: |
| Real median household income | **$87,460** (record high since 1967; +2.6% from 2024) |
| Official poverty rate | **10.2%** |
| Hispanic official poverty rate | **13.9%** (lowest on record) |
| Child official poverty rate | **13.4%** (historic low) |

### Foreign-born population

Census publications track the foreign-born population and countries of origin across more than two centuries of census records.

## Geographic Detail

Users can pull data for more than **100,000** geographies, including:

- States, counties, and cities  
- Tribal areas and ZIP codes  
- Congressional districts  

Topics include education, employment, health, and housing. Maps, charts, and tables can be customized and downloaded from [data.census.gov](https://data.census.gov).

## International Context

The Census Bureau’s domestic products are U.S.-focused. Comparative global ageing, including socioeconomic context, is in *[An Aging World: 2025](https://www.census.gov/library/publications/2026/demo/P95-26-1.html)*. Country estimates and projections also appear in the [International Database (IDB)](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/international-programs/about/idb.html).
