# South America Population Snapshot (2026)

As of 2026, South America has about **440–444 million** people, or **5.3–5.4%** of the world total. South America in 2026 is defined by **slower growth**, **very high urbanization**, **ageing**, and **uneven fertility and density**. **Brazil** remains the demographic centre of gravity.

## Overall Population and Growth

*Primary source: [Worldometer](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/south-america-population/) (UN World Population Prospects 2024)*

| Indicator | 2026 |
| --- | ---: |
| Population | **440,484,432** (other trackers: up to ~444 million) |
| Share of world population | **5.31%** |
| Annual growth rate | **0.54%** |
| Land area | 17,461,112 km² |
| Density | **25 people/km²** |

The population has grown nearly fivefold since **1950** (112,973,121). Growth peaked above **2.7%** in the late 1950s and early 1960s.

## Urbanization and Age Structure

| Indicator | Figure | Source |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Urban share | **88.7%** (390,840,159 people) | Worldometer |
| Median age | **33.5 years** | Worldometer |
| Age 65+ | ~**10–11%** | draft / PopulationPyramid (~11.4%) |

Large metro areas include São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Rio de Janeiro, Lima, and Bogotá. São Paulo exceeds **22 million** residents. The 65+ share is projected to roughly **double by 2050**. Ageing is furthest along in Chile, Uruguay, and Argentina.

## Fertility and Life Expectancy

- Continental TFR (Worldometer, 2026): **1.68** children per woman (draft/other sites sometimes cite **1.78**). Both are below the replacement level of **2.1**.
- Life expectancy: about **76 years** continent-wide in the draft compilation; higher in Chile (~81) and Uruguay (~79), lower in Bolivia (~71).

Country TFRs from the source draft ([worldpopulationclock.net](https://worldpopulationclock.net/south-america-live-population-clock/)):

## Historical Growth and Projections

| Year | Population | Growth rate |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1950 | ~113 million | 2.7% |
| 1970 | ~192 million | 2.4% |
| 1990 | ~297 million | 1.8% |
| 2010 | ~393 million | 1.0% |
| 2024 | ~436–439 million | ~0.5–0.6% |
| 2026 | **440–444 million** | **0.5–0.54%** |

Projections in the draft put the continent at about **468–491 million by 2050–2055**, with a lower growth rate and a median age near **42** by mid-century. Worldometer’s 2026 TFR and age profile already point in that direction.

## Key Trends

- Urbanization is already above **85%** and still rising.
- Ageing is accelerating, especially in the Southern Cone.
- Fertility is below replacement in most large countries, so growth is slowing.
- Migration—including Venezuelan emigration—has shifted national totals and city size within the continent.
