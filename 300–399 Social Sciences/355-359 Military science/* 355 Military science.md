**Military science** is the systematic study of warfare, military operations, strategy, tactics, and technology. It examines how armed forces are organized, trained, and employed in support of national defense. Military science is an interdisciplinary field that draws on history, strategy, technology, leadership, and logistics. It seeks to explain the principles of warfare and the use of armed forces in pursuit of defense objectives. The discipline includes both the theory of strategy and the practical study of military operations.

## Core Areas

Military science is the disciplined study of conflict. It combines theory, operations, leadership, and technology in order to inform planning and improve effectiveness in defense and strategy. It is useful both for historical analysis and for contemporary security studies, and it remains central to the education of military professionals and scholars.

## Historical and Modern Perspectives

The field has been shaped by theorists such as Sun Tzu, Carl von Clausewitz, and Antoine-Henri Jomini, who treated war as both a science and an art. Modern military science incorporates cyber operations, unmanned systems, and advanced command-and-control technologies, and it highlights the interaction between human judgment and technological change.

### Strategy and tactics

**Strategy** concerns high-level planning and long-term aims in war or conflict. **Tactics** concern battlefield maneuver and operational decision-making. Military scientists study historical campaigns, adversary capabilities, and geography in order to understand how strategies have been formed and applied.

### Leadership and command

Military science analyzes leadership, decision-making, and chains of command, with attention to cohesion, discipline, and performance in both combat and non-combat settings.

### Logistics and organization

Logistics covers the supply, movement, and maintenance of personnel and equipment. The field emphasizes how resources, infrastructure, and support systems affect readiness and sustainability.

### Military technology and weapons systems

This area studies the development and use of weapons, vehicles, communications, and intelligence technologies. It traces change from conventional systems to later developments such as unmanned systems, cyber capabilities, and precision-guided munitions.

### Intelligence and security studies

Military science also includes intelligence collection, threat assessment, and countermeasures. Understanding an adversary’s capabilities and vulnerabilities is treated as essential to informed strategic and tactical judgment.

## Applications

Military science informs professional military education, defense policy, and national security planning. It is also relevant to crisis management, peacekeeping, and the study of international conflict. Contemporary work often stresses joint operations, combined-arms coordination, and technological advantage as means of pursuing strategic aims while limiting risk.
