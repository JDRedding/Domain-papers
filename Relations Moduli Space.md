*Geometric space of all relational configurations*, quotiented by the SID/PED/Q equivalence

whose points represent **distinct relational geometries**, not raw states.

Below is the full, operator‑clean definition in relational formalism, with the structure aligned to

The **RDG moduli space** of a relational geometry \(X\) is:

$$\mathcal{M}_{RDG}(X) = X / \sim_{RDG}$$

where $sim_{RDG}$ is the equivalence relation generated by the SID, PED, and Q operators.

It is the space of **all relational configurations modulo symmetries**.
