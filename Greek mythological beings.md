# Greek mythological beings
— **major gods, minor gods, Titans, primordial beings, demigods, and heroes** 

This is **not** every single figure (there are hundreds), but it is the **complete canonical set of categories and the major representatives in each**.

---

# Greek pantheon (complete structure)

## ⭐ **Major Olympian Gods**  

- **Zeus** — king of the gods; sky, thunder  
- **Hera** — marriage, queenship  
- **Poseidon** — sea, earthquakes  
- **Demeter** — agriculture  
- **Athena** — wisdom, strategy  
- **Apollo** — music, prophecy, healing  
- **Artemis** — hunt, moon  
- **Ares** — war  
- **Aphrodite** — love, beauty  
- **Hephaestus** — forge, craftsmanship  
- **Hermes** — messenger, trade, thieves  
- **Dionysus** — wine, ecstasy  
- **Hestia** — hearth, home  

---
