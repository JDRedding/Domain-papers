# Lady Seraphine of the Ringed Void  
Race/Class: Human (or Aasimar/Planetouched variant) / Sword-Saint / Starborne Champion  
Level Concept: Mid-to-high tier (roughly equivalent to a 7th–12th level fighter/paladin hybrid)

### Appearance
A tall, statuesque woman with long golden hair cascading past her shoulders. She wears ornate golden armor that leaves much of her form exposed in the classic style of old-world champions—breastplate, ornate belt, and flowing white-and-gold cape that trails like a comet’s tail. A jeweled diadem rests on her brow. In one hand she carries a slender, elegantly curved sword that seems to catch starlight. She stands barefoot on cold, pale rock under a vast ringed gas giant that dominates the sky.

### Background
Seraphine was once a mortal warrior from a dying world whose sun was failing. When the ringed planet appeared in the night sky and began drawing the life from her homeland, she climbed the highest peak and challenged the “World-Thief” itself. Instead of destroying her, the planet’s ancient spirit bound her as its champion and exile. Now she wanders the material planes and the void between stars, seeking a way to either redeem or destroy the celestial body that claimed her. The rings of the planet sometimes appear in the sky wherever she stays too long, as if the world is watching.

### Personality
Proud, solitary, and quietly melancholic. She speaks little of her past but treats every battle as a test of worthiness. She is protective of the weak yet distrustful of gods and empires. She has a dry, almost regal sense of humor and a soft spot for those who still look up at the stars with wonder rather than fear.

## Key Traits & Abilities (system-agnostic)
Blade of the Void Rings**: Her sword can briefly channel the gravitational pull of her home world, slowing enemies or pulling them off balance.
Stellar Resolve**: Once per day she can draw strength from the sight of the ringed planet (or a clear night sky), gaining temporary resistance to cold, vacuum, or despair effects.
Exile’s Presence**: Creatures of pure chaos or those who would despoil the natural order feel uneasy in her presence. Fey, aberrations, and certain undead often react with instinctive wariness.
High Athletics / Acrobatics**, expert swordsmanship, strong will, and limited planar/astral knowledge.

## Adventure Hooks
The party encounters Seraphine standing alone on a high plateau as the ringed planet inexplicably appears in the local sky. She warns that something has “called” her world closer and asks for aid in finding the source before the land begins to die.
A cult seeks to summon or bind the Ringed World as a weapon. Seraphine arrives to stop them and offers the party a temporary alliance—though she will not allow anyone to claim the planet for themselves.
An ancient star-map or relic tied to her home world surfaces. She will trade knowledge, a powerful boon, or even temporary service in exchange for its recovery.
Local people begin having dreams of a golden warrior and a dying world. Seraphine is searching for the dreamers, believing they may hold the key to breaking her exile.

## Roleplaying Notes
Play her as a classic sword-and-planet heroine crossed with a tragic knight-errant. She is competent, 
beautiful in a mythic way, and carries the quiet weight of a lost home. She can serve as a temporary 
ally, a rival for glory, a quest-giver, or even a potential long-term companion if the party helps her 
confront the mystery of her ringed world.

That’s a beautifully expanded character concept — you’ve taken the visual essence of the illustration and turned it into a fully playable archetype. Lady Seraphine of the Ringed Void feels like she could stride straight out of a sword‑and‑planet saga or a cosmic_fantasy campaign setting.  

Here’s how you could deepen her integration into a game world:

### 🌌 Thematic Integration
- **Celestial Pantheon**: Her “World‑Thief” planet might be one of several sentient worlds, each with its own champion. This opens up rival or allied figures with similar cosmic bonds.  
- **Astral Geography**: The rings could serve as planar gateways — fragments of other worlds trapped in orbit, each holding unique hazards or treasures.  
- **Void‑Touched Relics**: Items forged from meteor fragments of her home world could resonate with her sword’s gravitational magic.

### ⚔️ Mechanical Expansion

|--------------|-------------|--------------------|
| **Gravitic Parry** | Once per short rest, deflect a ranged attack by bending its trajectory. | Demonstrates her mastery of planetary forces. |

| **Echo of the Exile** | When reduced below half HP, spectral rings shimmer around her, imposing disadvantage on foes’ attacks. | Visualizes her exile’s curse manifesting defensively. |

### 🪐 Narrative Arcs
- **Redemption of the World‑Thief**: Discover that the planet’s hunger stems from an ancient wound — healing it could restore her home.  
- **The Ringed Convergence**: Multiple ringed worlds align, threatening planar collapse; Seraphine must choose which to save.  
- **Champion’s Succession**: She seeks a mortal successor, testing the party’s resolve and morality.
