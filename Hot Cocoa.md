# Hot Cocoa

**Makes:** 2 servings (1 cup each)

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon cocoa  
- 2 tablespoons sugar  
- 2 cups water  
- 2/3 cup instant nonfat dry milk  
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla  

## Directions

1. Mix cocoa, sugar, and 1/2 cup of the water in a pan.  
2. Heat to boiling and cook slowly for 5 minutes to make cocoa syrup.  
3. Remove cocoa syrup from heat.  
4. Add the rest of the water.  
5. Add dry milk.  
6. Stir or beat until smooth.  
7. Heat but do not boil.  
8. Add vanilla.

---

# Chocolate Milk

Use the recipe for hot cocoa.

1. Make the cocoa syrup and cool it.  
2. Add the rest of the water, milk, and vanilla.  
3. Stir or beat until smooth.  
4. Chill quickly.  
5. Stir before serving.
