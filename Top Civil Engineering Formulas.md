# Top 20 Civil Engineering Formulas

Key equations for construction calculations, including steel weight, concrete volume, cement and sand quantities, brickwork, plastering, stress, strain, bending moment, shear force, and basic geometry such as circle area and cylinder volume.

**Note:** Use consistent units in every calculation.

### 1. Steel weight

$$W = \dfrac{D^{2}}{162.5}$$

- \(W\) = weight of steel (kg/m)
- \(D\) = diameter of the steel bar (mm)

### 2. Concrete volume

$$V = L \times B \times H$$

- \(V\) = volume (m³)
- \(L\) = length (m)
- \(B\) = breadth (m)
- \(H\) = height (m)

### 3. Dry volume

$$\text{Dry volume} = \text{Wet volume} \times 1.54$$

(1.54 is an approximate factor for converting wet concrete volume to the volume of dry materials.)

### 4. Cement quantity

$$\text{Cement (kg)} = \dfrac{\text{Dry volume} \times \text{Cement part}}{\text{Total mix ratio}} \times 1440$$

### 5. Cement bags

$$\text{Bags} = \dfrac{\text{Cement (kg)}}{50}$$

(1 bag = 50 kg)

### 6. Sand quantity

$$\text{Sand} = \dfrac{\text{Sand part}}{\text{Total mix ratio}} \times \text{Dry volume}$$

### 7. Aggregate quantity

$$\text{Aggregate} = \dfrac{\text{Aggregate part}}{\text{Total mix ratio}} \times \text{Dry volume}$$

### 8. Brick calculation

$$\text{Bricks} = \dfrac{\text{Volume of wall}}{\text{Volume of one brick}}$$

$$\text{Volume of wall} = L \times B \times H$$

$$\text{Volume of one brick} = L_{b} \times B_{b} \times H_{b}$$

(Add 5–10% for mortar joints and breakage.)

### 9. Mortar quantity

$$\text{Mortar} = 23\% \text{ to } 30\% \text{ of brickwork volume}$$

(Use about 23% for dry brick masonry and up to 30% for modular bricks.)

### 10. Plaster area

$$\text{Area} = L \times H$$

- \(L\) = length (m)
- \(H\) = height (m)

### 11. Plaster volume

$$\text{Volume} = \text{Area} \times \text{Thickness}$$

### 12. Unit weight

$$\text{Unit weight} = \dfrac{\text{Weight}}{\text{Volume}}$$

Unit: N/m³ or kN/m³

### 13. Load calculation

$$W = w \times A$$

- \(w\) = uniformly distributed load (kN/m²)
- \(A\) = area (m²)
- \(W\) = total load (kN)

### 14. Stress

$$\sigma = \dfrac{F}{A}$$

- \(\sigma\) = stress (N/m² or Pa)
- \(F\) = force (N)
- \(A\) = area (m²)

### 15. Strain

$$\varepsilon = \dfrac{\Delta L}{L}$$

- \(\varepsilon\) = strain (unitless)
- \(\Delta L\) = change in length (m)
- \(L\) = original length (m)

### 16. Modulus of elasticity

$$E = \dfrac{\text{Stress}}{\text{Strain}}$$

- \(E\) = modulus of elasticity (N/m² or Pa)

### 17. Bending moment

$$BM = F \times d$$

- \(BM\) = bending moment (N·m)
- \(F\) = applied load (N)
- \(d\) = perpendicular distance from the load to the section (m)

### 18. Shear force

Shear force \(V\) = algebraic sum of the vertical forces on one side of the section.

(The sign convention depends on which side of the section is considered.)

### 19. Area of a circle

$$A = \pi r^{2}$$

- \(A\) = area (m²)
- \(\pi \approx 3.1416\)
- \(r\) = radius (m)

### 20. Volume of a cylinder

$$V = \pi r^{2} h$$

- \(V\) = volume (m³)
- \(r\) = radius (m)
- \(h\) = height (m)
